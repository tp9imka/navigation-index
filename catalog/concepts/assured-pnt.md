# Assured / resilient PNT

`concept:assured-pnt`

Architectures that keep position, navigation and timing trustworthy under denial and deception.

**Sub-concepts:** [Timing and time transfer](../concepts/timing.md), [Terrain-relative navigation](../concepts/terrain-nav.md), [Magnetic anomaly navigation](../concepts/magnetic-nav.md), [Celestial and star-tracker navigation](../concepts/celestial-nav.md), [Signals of opportunity](../concepts/signals-of-opportunity.md), [Electronic warfare resilience](../concepts/ew-resilience.md)

- [When GNSS fails, what will you do? MarRINav!](../resources/ion-webinar-marrinav.md) - talk - UK maritime resilient navigation study (MarRINav) on backups for GNSS at sea, including eLoran and R-Mode style terrestrial systems; co-sponsored by the RNT Foundation.
- [White Paper: Protecting GNSS Against Intentional Interference](../resources/protecting-gnss-intentional-interference-arxiv.md) - paper - Open white paper summarizing the threat of jamming and spoofing to civil GNSS and the countermeasure landscape (signal authentication, antenna arrays, consistency checks, regulation).
- [Who cares about the Baltic Jammer? Terrestrial Navigation in the Baltic Sea Region (39C3)](../resources/39c3-baltic-jammer-r-mode.md) - talk - 39th Chaos Communication Congress talk from DLR researchers on R-Mode, a terrestrial maritime navigation backup built on existing radio beacons and coastal infrastructure, tested in the heavily jammed Baltic.
