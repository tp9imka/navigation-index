# Event-based Vision: A Survey

`res:gallego-event-vision-survey-2019` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08405>

**By:** Guillermo Gallego, Tobi Delbruck, Garrick Orchard, et al. - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Comprehensive survey of event cameras: sensing principle, algorithms (feature tracking, optical flow, VO/SLAM) and applications.

## Key takeaways

- Microsecond latency and high dynamic range
- Algorithms must be redesigned for asynchronous data
