# Solution Separation Versus Residual-Based RAIM

`res:joerger-2014-solution-separation-vs-residual-raim` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1002/navi.71>

**By:** Mathieu Joerger, Fang-Cheng Chan, Boris Pervan - **Year:** 2014 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

NAVIGATION 61(4) paper with a step-by-step derivation of residual-based and solution-separation RAIM from common integrity and continuity risk definitions, including a parity-space view of solution separation. Solution separation can monitor better because its statistics are tailored to each fault hypothesis, but residual-based RAIM gives tighter integrity bounds in conventional efficient implementations.

## Key takeaways

- Side-by-side derivation of the two main RAIM families
- Parity-space interpretation of solution separation
- Useful when choosing an FDE scheme for a fused alt-PNT filter
