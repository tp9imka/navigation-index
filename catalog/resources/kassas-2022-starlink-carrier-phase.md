# The First Carrier Phase Tracking and Positioning Results With Starlink LEO Satellite Signals

`res:kassas-2022-starlink-carrier-phase` - paper - <https://ece.osu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-09/The_first_carrier_phase_tracking_and_positioning_results_with_Starlink_LEO_satellite_Signals.pdf>

**By:** Joe Khalife, Mohammad Neinavaie, Zaher M. Kassas - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

IEEE TAES letter showing carrier-phase tracking of Starlink signals with unknown structure and positioning from several satellites.

## Key takeaways

- Positioning from Starlink is feasible without knowing the signal format
- Six satellites tracked; 7.7 m horizontal error with known receiver altitude
