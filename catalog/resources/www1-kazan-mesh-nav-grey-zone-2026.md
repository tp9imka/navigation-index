# Систему для полёта дронов в «серой» зоне без GPS создали в России

`res:www1-kazan-mesh-nav-grey-zone-2026` - blog - <https://www1.ru/news/2026/02/04/sistemu-dlia-poleta-dronov-v-seroi-zone-bez-gps-sozdali-v-rossii.html>

**By:** www1.ru - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'A system for drone flight in the grey zone without GPS was created in Russia'. Kazan engineers, funded through the NTI Foundation, describe an adaptive wireless mesh of ground base stations and onboard modules in several frequency bands that provides positioning and control without GNSS. Claimed: up to 10 m positioning accuracy and 10-15 km range in open terrain; field trials with four base stations and two UAVs planned for end of 2026.

## Key takeaways

- Terrestrial radio positioning plus comms in one mesh
- Numbers are announced targets; trials not yet done at publication
- Positioning algorithm not described
