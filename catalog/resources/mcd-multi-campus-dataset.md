# MCD: Diverse Large-Scale Multi-Campus Dataset for Robot Perception

`res:mcd-multi-campus-dataset` - dataset - <https://mcdviral.github.io/>

**By:** Thien-Minh Nguyen, Shenghai Yuan, Thien Hoang Nguyen, Pengyu Yin, Haozhi Cao, Lihua Xie, et al. - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Multi-campus sequences in Asia and Europe across seasons with two lidar types, stereo, IMUs, UWB and survey-grade prior maps.

## Key takeaways

- Sensors: lidar, stereo, IMU, UWB
- Ground truth: continuous-time with prior maps
