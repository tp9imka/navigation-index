Hardware and OS requirements for Parrot's Sphinx drone simulator.

## Key takeaways

- Ubuntu 20.04/22.04/24.04 64-bit only
- NVIDIA GPU required (2 GB min, RTX 2070 recommended)
- Not usable on an Apple Silicon Mac
