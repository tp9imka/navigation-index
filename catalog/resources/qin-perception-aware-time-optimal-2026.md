# Perception-Aware Time-Optimal Planning for Quadrotor Waypoint Flight

`res:qin-perception-aware-time-optimal-2026` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04305>

**By:** Chao Qin, Jiaxu Xing, Rudolf Reiter, Angel Romero, Yifan Lin, Hugh H.-T. Liu, Davide Scaramuzza - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Time-optimal trajectory optimisation with full dynamics, rotor limits, camera field-of-view constraints and an information-theoretic position-uncertainty metric, tracked by contouring MPC. Abstract reports real flights up to 9.8 m/s and Split-S success rising from 55% to 100%.

## Key takeaways

- Recent, strong perception-aware baseline for racing-style flight
- Gate courses, not cluttered A-to-B
