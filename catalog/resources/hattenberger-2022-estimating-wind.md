# Estimating wind using a quadrotor

`res:hattenberger-2022-estimating-wind` - paper - <https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/17568293211070824>

**By:** Gautier Hattenberger, Murat Bronz, Jean-Philippe Condomines - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Wind estimation using attitude and ground speed with experimental calibration. The conclusion identifies online drag estimation and observability conditions as further work; not evidence that wind is observable without a ground reference.
