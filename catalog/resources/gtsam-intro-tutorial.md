# Factor Graphs and GTSAM: A Hands-on Introduction

**By:** Frank Dellaert, Georgia Tech Borg Lab - **Year:** ? - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Hands-on introduction to the GTSAM library: modelling robot odometry, landmark and pose-graph SLAM as factor graphs, solving with Levenberg-Marquardt or iSAM2, and reading marginal covariances, with C++ and MATLAB snippets.

## Key takeaways

- Best first hour with GTSAM
- Shortest path to a working pose-graph (C++ or MATLAB examples)
- Pattern reusable for GNSS/INS/visual factor fusion
