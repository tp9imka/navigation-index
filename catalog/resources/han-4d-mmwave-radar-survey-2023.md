# 4D Millimeter-Wave Radar in Autonomous Driving: A Survey

`res:han-4d-mmwave-radar-survey-2023` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.04242>

**By:** Zeyu Han, Jiahao Wang, Zikun Xu, et al. - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Survey of 4D imaging radar: signal processing, datasets, calibration, perception and SLAM/odometry uses.

## Key takeaways

- 4D radar adds elevation and works in adverse weather
- Sparse, noisy point clouds need radar-specific methods
