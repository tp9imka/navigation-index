# AI-IMU Dead-Reckoning

`res:brossard-ai-imu-dr` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.06064>

**By:** Martin Brossard, Axel Barrau, Silvère Bonnabel - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Car localisation from IMU alone by combining an invariant EKF with a neural network that adapts measurement noise covariances (pseudo-measurements), evaluated on KITTI.

## Key takeaways

- Learned covariances, classical filter structure
- IMU-only ground vehicle dead reckoning
