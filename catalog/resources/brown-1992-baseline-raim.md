# A Baseline GPS RAIM Scheme and a Note on the Equivalence of Three RAIM Methods

`res:brown-1992-baseline-raim` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1002/j.2161-4296.1992.tb02278.x>

**By:** R. Grover Brown - **Year:** 1992 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

NAVIGATION 39(3) paper written as the RTCA GPS MOPS were released. It proposes a baseline RAIM scheme for performance comparisons and shows that, at equal alarm rates, the range-comparison, least-squares-residual and parity methods give identical results.

## Key takeaways

- Range comparison, LS residual and parity RAIM are equivalent at equal alarm rate
- Intended as a simple standard baseline, not an optimal scheme
- Ties RAIM to the aviation MOPS context
