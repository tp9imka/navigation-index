# EASA Artificial Intelligence Concept Paper Issue 2: Guidance for Level 1 & 2 machine learning applications

`res:easa-ai-concept-paper-issue-2` - doc - <https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/general-publications/easa-artificial-intelligence-concept-paper-issue-2>

**By:** EASA - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

EASA's guidance for ML in aviation: classification of AI levels (assistance, human-AI teaming, autonomy), learning assurance objectives, explainability and safety-risk mitigation. About 240 pages. RMT.0742 is meant to turn it into rules and AMC.

## Key takeaways

- Learning assurance is the ML counterpart of DO-178C development assurance
- Guidance, not rules; expect it to evolve
