# Uncertainty-aware receding horizon exploration and mapping using aerial robots

`res:papachristos-uncertainty-aware-exploration-2017` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA.2017.7989531>

**By:** Christos Papachristos, Shehryar Khattak, Kostas Alexis - **Year:** 2017 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Two-step receding-horizon planner: a random tree finds branches that explore new space, then the branch that minimises expected localization uncertainty of a stereo visual-inertial system is flown. Verified on aerial robots in lit and dark conditions. ICRA 2017; journal version Auton. Robots 2019.

## Key takeaways

- Route choice driven by predicted VIO uncertainty
- Exploration task, not A-to-B
