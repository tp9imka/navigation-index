# UAV-VisLoc: A Large-scale Dataset for UAV Visual Localization

`res:uav-visloc-dataset-2024` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11936>

**By:** Wenjia Xu, Yaxuan Yao, Jiaqi Cao et al. - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Introduces a dataset of real UAV flight images paired with satellite maps over varied terrain for evaluating UAV visual localization.

## Key takeaways

- Real flights with ground truth
- Covers terrain types beyond cities
