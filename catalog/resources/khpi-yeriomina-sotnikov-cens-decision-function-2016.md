# Формування вирішальної функції кореляційно-екстремальних систем навігації за критерієм максимуму узагальненого коефіцієнта взаємної кореляції

`res:khpi-yeriomina-sotnikov-cens-decision-function-2016` - paper - <https://repository.kpi.kharkov.ua/handle/KhPI-Press/29493>

**By:** N. Yeriomina, O. Sotnikov, V. Tarshyn - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

English: 'Forming the decision function of correlation-extremal navigation systems by the maximum generalised cross-correlation criterion' (Bulletin of NTU KhPI, No. 50, 2016). Studies how viewing geometry, mainly perspective distortion, degrades the decision function when onboard images are matched to stored references, and proposes adapting the references: per resolution element take the peak cross-correlation and combine into a generalised correlation function. Keywords cite UAVs.

## Key takeaways

- 'Correlation-extremal navigation' (КЕНС) is the Soviet/Ukrainian term for map-matching navigation
- Perspective distortion dominates the geometric error in image matching
- Background theory for TERCOM/scene-matching style entries
