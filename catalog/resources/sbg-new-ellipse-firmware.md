# SBG Systems Ellipse INS - 2025 GNSS-denied firmware

`res:sbg-new-ellipse-firmware` - product - <https://www.sbg-systems.com/news/introducing-new-ellipse/>

**By:** SBG Systems - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

A March 2025 free firmware update for SBG's Ellipse (v3 hardware) adds jamming/spoofing detection flags and lets users reject or re-enable GNSS and other aiding sensors without resetting the filter.

## Key takeaways

- Vendor claim: improved accuracy in GNSS-denied and magnetically disturbed conditions
- Integrity flags plus manual sensor rejection is now an expected feature even in small INS
