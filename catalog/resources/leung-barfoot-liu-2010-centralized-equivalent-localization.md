# Decentralized Localization of Sparsely-Communicating Robot Networks: A Centralized-Equivalent Approach

`res:leung-barfoot-liu-2010-centralized-equivalent-localization` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/TRO.2009.2035741>

**By:** Keith Y. K. Leung, Timothy D. Barfoot, Hugh H. T. Liu - **Year:** 2010 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE T-RO 26(1) paper on decentralized state estimation for robot teams that are not always fully connected. The central result is that a robot can reproduce the centralized estimate using only its own knowledge of the communication history, whenever that estimate is attainable.

## Key takeaways

- Targets intermittent, sparse communication
- Centralized-equivalent result when the information has actually reached the robot
- Extended to cooperative SLAM in later work by the same group
