# On Degeneracy of Optimization-based State Estimation Problems

`res:zhang-kaess-singh-2016-degeneracy-state-estimation` - paper - <https://frc.ri.cmu.edu/~kaess/pub/Zhang16icra.html>

**By:** Ji Zhang, Michael Kaess, Sanjiv Singh - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

ICRA 2016 (abstract read). Detects degenerate directions of the state space online from the constraint geometry and solves the problem only in the well-conditioned directions, tested on camera and lidar ego-motion.

## Key takeaways

- Direction-wise partial update in the well-conditioned subspace is an established pattern in lidar/visual odometry
- Same mechanism as an eigen-direction gate on aero parameters, different domain
