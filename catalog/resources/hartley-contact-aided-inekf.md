# Contact-Aided Invariant Extended Kalman Filtering for Robot State Estimation

`res:hartley-contact-aided-inekf` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.09251>

**By:** Ross Hartley, Maani Ghaffari, Ryan M. Eustice, Jessy W. Grizzle - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Applies the invariant EKF to legged robots, fusing IMU with leg contact kinematics, and evaluates it against a quaternion EKF.

## Key takeaways

- Contact points as extra states in the group
- Faster convergence than quaternion EKF
