# Champion-level drone racing using deep reinforcement learning

`res:swift-champion-drone-racing-nature` - paper - <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06419-4>

**By:** Elia Kaufmann, et al. (UZH, Intel Labs) - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

Swift, a quadrotor using only onboard camera and IMU with an RL-trained policy, beat human world-champion pilots in head-to-head races.

## Key takeaways

- Onboard VIO plus learned control reached human-champion level
- Sim-to-real with residual models from real data
