# Consistent Distributed Cooperative Localization for Ultra Large-Scale Multi-agent Systems

`res:pedroso-2026-ulss-cooperative-localization` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04872>

**By:** Leonardo Pedroso, W. P. M. H. Heemels, Pedro Batista - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Preprint on cooperative localization for very large networks (the paper's example is satellite mega-constellations) where per-agent resources must not grow with network size. It uses overlapping covariance intersection to exploit limited structure in the cross-correlations while staying consistent, and is fully distributed.

## Key takeaways

- Per-agent cost independent of team size
- Overlapping CI: less conservative than plain CI
- Relevant to large drone swarms as well as constellations
