# MassMIND: Massachusetts Maritime INfrared Dataset

`res:massmind-paper-2022` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.04097>

**By:** Shailesh Nirgudkar, Michael DeFilippo, Michael Sacarny, Michael Benjamin, Paul Robinette - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Paper describing a labelled LWIR maritime dataset of 2,916 images from in and around Boston Harbor, segmented into seven classes, with UNet, PSPNet and DeepLabv3 baselines. Data is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial).

## Key takeaways

- Thermal segmentation for USV situational awareness
- Semantic and instance masks provided
- Non-commercial licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
