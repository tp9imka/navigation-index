# Weighted Covariance Intersection for Range-based Distributed Cooperative Localization of Multi-Vehicle Systems

`res:tu-2025-weighted-ci-range-cooperative-localization` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12207>

**By:** Chenxin Tu, Xiaowei Cui, Gang Liu, Mingquan Lu - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Published in IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology (2026). Argues that standard CI weighting criteria break down for 3D range-based distributed cooperative localization, where state components differ strongly in scale and observability, and proposes a weighted CI criterion for GNSS-denied multi-vehicle systems.

## Key takeaways

- Targets 3D range-only cooperative localization
- Shows why plug-in CI criteria give unbalanced estimates
- Recent (2025-2026) follow-up to the classic CI line
