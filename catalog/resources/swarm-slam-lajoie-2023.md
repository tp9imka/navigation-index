# Swarm-SLAM: Sparse Decentralized Collaborative Simultaneous Localization and Mapping Framework for Multi-Robot Systems

`res:swarm-slam-lajoie-2023` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.06230>

**By:** Pierre-Yves Lajoie, Giovanni Beltrame - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Decentralized collaborative SLAM aimed at swarms, prioritizing which inter-robot loop closures to compute and share to limit communication.

## Key takeaways

- Communication budget is a first-class design constraint
- Supports LiDAR, stereo and RGB-D
