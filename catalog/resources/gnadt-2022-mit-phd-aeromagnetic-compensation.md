# Advanced Aeromagnetic Compensation Models for Airborne Magnetic Anomaly Navigation

`res:gnadt-2022-mit-phd-aeromagnetic-compensation` - report - <https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/145137>

**By:** Albert R. Gnadt - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

MIT AeroAstro PhD thesis (advisor Alan Edelman) on removing the aircraft's own magnetic field so magnetic anomaly maps can be used for navigation. It studies four groups of methods: linear compensation, nonlinear (neural) compensation, online compensation and covariance-adaptive filtering. The models are implemented in MagNav.jl. DSpace terms: in copyright, educational use permitted.

## Key takeaways

- The full technical background behind MagNav.jl
- Compares Tolles-Lawson, neural and online compensation
