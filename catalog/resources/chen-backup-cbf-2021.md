# Backup Control Barrier Functions: Formulation and Comparative Study

`res:chen-backup-cbf-2021` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11332>

**By:** Yuxiao Chen, Mrdjan Jankovic, Mario Santillo, Aaron D. Ames - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

General backup-CBF formulation: a fixed backup controller grows a small, easy control-invariant set into a larger one, and the filter keeps the system able to fall back to it. Compares backup CBFs with other safety methods.

## Key takeaways

- Turns a hover or brake controller into a safety filter
