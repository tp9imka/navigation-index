# Троє 20-річних хлопців за 24 години створили дрон, якому не потрібен GPS

`res:devua-24h-gps-free-drone-2024` - blog - <https://dev.ua/news/hrupa-20-richnykh-khloptsiv-za-24-hodyny-stvoryla-dron-iakomu-ne-potriben-gps-1726137453>

**By:** dev.ua - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'Three 20-year-olds built a drone that needs no GPS in 24 hours'. Ukrainian retelling (citing Defense One) of a US hackathon-born build motivated by Ukrainian front-line feedback: downward camera frames matched to stored imagery, ~256 GB of compressed map tiles covering 10,000+ sq km on an SD card, under $500 of hardware.

## Key takeaways

- Hackathon-scale VPS is feasible with commodity compute and pre-loaded tiles
- Map storage and preloading time are the practical bottlenecks
- Body text says several days of work, not 24 hours; open problems are altitude and speed envelope
