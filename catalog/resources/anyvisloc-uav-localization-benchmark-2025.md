# Exploring the best way for UAV visual localization under Low-altitude Multi-view Observation Condition: a Benchmark

`res:anyvisloc-uav-localization-benchmark-2025` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10692>

**By:** Yibin Ye, Xichao Teng, Shuo Chen et al. - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Builds a benchmark for low-altitude, oblique-view UAV absolute visual localization and systematically compares retrieval, matching and refinement choices.

## Key takeaways

- Low-altitude oblique views are much harder than nadir
- Gives evidence for picking retrieval/matcher combos
