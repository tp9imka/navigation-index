# OrthoLoC: UAV 6-DoF Localization and Calibration Using Orthographic Geodata

`res:dhaouadi-2025-ortholoc` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18350>

**By:** Oussema Dhaouadi, Riccardo Marin, Johannes Meier, Jacques Kaiser, Daniel Cremers - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

NeurIPS 2025 Datasets and Benchmarks. UAV images paired with orthophotos and surface models so localization and calibration can be evaluated without the retrieval step; also proposes a matcher-agnostic refinement (AdHoP).

## Key takeaways

- Abstract: 16,425 UAV images over 47 regions in Germany and the US
- Repo LICENSE is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: evaluation only, no commercial use
- Project page states about 287 GB (not checked)
