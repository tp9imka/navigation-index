# Self-Driving Cars Specialization (University of Toronto)

`res:coursera-self-driving-cars-specialization` - course - <https://www.coursera.org/specializations/self-driving-cars>

**By:** University of Toronto - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** free-registration

Four-course specialization covering vehicle dynamics and control, state estimation and localization, visual perception, and motion planning, using the CARLA simulator for assignments.

## Key takeaways

- End-to-end autonomy pipeline in one program
- CARLA-based assignments transfer to hackathon sims
