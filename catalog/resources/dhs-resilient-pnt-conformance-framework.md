# Resilient PNT Conformance Framework

`res:dhs-resilient-pnt-conformance-framework` - standard - <https://dhs.gov/sites/default/files/publications/2020_12_resilient_pnt_conformance_framework.pdf>

**By:** DHS Science and Technology Directorate - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Defines four cumulative resilience levels for PNT user equipment based on prevent, respond and recover behaviours, so buyers and vendors can describe how a receiver behaves under jamming or spoofing.

## Key takeaways

- Gives vocabulary to grade your solution: prevent, respond, recover
- Solution-agnostic, so it fits any sensor mix
- Later versions and an IEEE standards effort followed
- Levels escalate from basic recovery to full prevention and response
- Scope is PNT-producing equipment, not downstream consumers
- A later v2.0 was reported in 2022 with evaluation guidance
