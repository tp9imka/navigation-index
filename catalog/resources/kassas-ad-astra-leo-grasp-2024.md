# Ad Astra: Navigation with Megaconstellation LEO Satellites (GRASP on Robotics)

`res:kassas-ad-astra-leo-grasp-2024` - talk - <https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/events/fall-2024-grasp-on-robotics-zak-kassas/>

**By:** Zak Kassas - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

UPenn GRASP seminar by Zak Kassas on opportunistic PNT from Starlink, OneWeb and other LEO megaconstellations, addressing unknown signal structure, poor ephemerides and clock instability with his STAN (simultaneous tracking and navigation) framework validated on ground and aerial vehicles.

## Key takeaways

- LEO signals of opportunity as a GNSS backup
- Covers SDR receiver design to navigation filter
