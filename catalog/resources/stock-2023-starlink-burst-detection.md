# LEO-PNT With Starlink: Development of a Burst Detection Algorithm Based on Signal Measurements

`res:stock-2023-starlink-burst-detection` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09535>

**By:** Winfried Stock, Christian A. Hofmann, Andreas Knopp - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Measures Starlink signals and builds a correlation-based burst detector, then measures burst carrier frequency (Doppler) and estimates attainable positioning accuracy. The authors argue it works with an omnidirectional antenna in an operationally relevant setup.

## Key takeaways

- Burst detection as the first step of Doppler positioning
- Omnidirectional antenna, no dish
- European (UniBw Munich) LEO-PNT work
