# Data-Driven Protection Levels for Camera and 3D Map-based Safe Urban Localization

`res:gupta-gao-2021-data-driven-protection-levels` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.06379>

**By:** Shubh Gupta, Grace X. Gao - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Computes protection levels for localization that matches camera images to a LiDAR-built 3D map, for urban areas where GNSS is unreliable. Position error is modelled as a Gaussian mixture from deep-network data-driven models with outlier weighting, and the PL is found by numerical line search. Submitted to NAVIGATION.

## Key takeaways

- Protection levels for map-based visual localization, no GNSS
- Learned error model plus statistical outlier weighting
- Template for PLs on visual geolocalization fixes
