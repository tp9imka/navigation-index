# Preliminary Lessons in Conventional Warfighting from Russia's Invasion of Ukraine: February-July 2022

`res:rusi-preliminary-lessons-ukraine-2022` - report - <https://rusi.org/explore-our-research/publications/special-resources/preliminary-lessons-conventional-warfighting-russias-invasion-ukraine-february-july-2022>

**By:** J. Watling, N. Reynolds (RUSI) - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

RUSI analysis of the first months of the war, including the scale of Russian electronic warfare and its effect on Ukrainian UAV survival and GNSS-dependent systems.

## Key takeaways

- EW is a dominant factor in small-UAV attrition
- Lessons for resilience of navigation and C2 links
