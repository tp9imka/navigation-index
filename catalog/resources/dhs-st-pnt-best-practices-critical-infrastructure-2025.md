# Best Practices for Resilient Positioning, Navigation, and Timing (PNT) Supporting Critical Infrastructure

`res:dhs-st-pnt-best-practices-critical-infrastructure-2025` - doc - <https://www.dhs.gov/science-and-technology/publication/pnt-best-practices-supporting-critical-infrastructure>

**By:** DHS Science and Technology Directorate - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

DHS S&T guide released in February 2025 under EO 13905 to help providers, owners and operators use PNT responsibly. It gives general best practices with GPS/GNSS examples and use cases including stationary timing and moving platforms, applied through risk assessment.

## Key takeaways

- Includes a stationary-timing use case
- Risk-based: assess, then choose resilience measures
