# ITU-T G.8272: Timing characteristics of primary reference time clocks

`res:itu-t-g8272-prtc` - standard - <https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.8272>

**By:** ITU-T - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Defines the primary reference time clock (PRTC), the GNSS-fed time source at the top of a telecom timing chain, and its output limits.

## Key takeaways

- PRTC = the 'grandmaster from GNSS' in telecom vocabulary
