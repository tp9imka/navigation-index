# Unveiling Starlink for PNT

`res:kozhaya-saroufim-kassas-2025-unveiling-starlink-pnt` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.33012/navi.685>

**By:** Sharbel E. Kozhaya, Joe Saroufim, Zaher M. Kassas - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Open-access NAVIGATION 72(1) paper describing the full Starlink OFDM beacon via blind beacon estimation, the processing gain this gives over previously published sequences, achievable C/N0 for different reception setups, and the first carrier phase, Doppler and code phase observables from Starlink OFDM. Uses signal collections from 2021 to 2024 and notes step-like corrections that contaminate OFDM observables. Won the ION Samuel Burka Award.

## Key takeaways

- Full beacon gives large processing gain for low-gain antennas
- Pilot-tone vs OFDM observable quality compared
- Open access, with an ION webinar companion
