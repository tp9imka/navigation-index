# ASTM F3269 - An Industry Standard on Run Time Assurance for Aircraft Systems

`res:nagarajan-2021-astm-f3269-scitech` - paper - <https://elib.dlr.de/144352/>

**By:** Pranav Nagarajan, Suresh K. Kannan, Christoph Torens, Mike E. Vukas, George F. Wilber - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

AIAA SciTech 2021 paper by members of the F3269 working group explaining the RTA architecture in the standard, the reasoning behind its revision, and recommended design practices. The open DLR copy is the free way to learn what F3269 says.

## Key takeaways

- Free, author-side explanation of the paywalled standard
- Points at certifying AI/ML and adaptive controllers as a target use
