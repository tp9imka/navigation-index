# Integrity monitoring of Graph-SLAM using GPS and fish-eye camera

`res:bhamidipati-gao-2020-graph-slam-integrity` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1002/navi.381>

**By:** Sriramya Bhamidipati, Grace Xingxin Gao - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

NAVIGATION 67(3) paper (journal version of ION GNSS+ 2019 / arXiv 1910.02165) that computes protection levels for a graph-SLAM estimator fusing GPS pseudoranges and fish-eye camera pixel intensities. Vision faults are isolated with a superpixel-based piecewise RANSAC, and protection levels come from worst-case failure slope analysis. Tested in urban and semi-urban driving.

## Key takeaways

- Multi-fault integrity for joint GPS + vision
- Protection levels from failure-slope analysis on linearized graph SLAM
- Open arXiv preprint: 1910.02165
