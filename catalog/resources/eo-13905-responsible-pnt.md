# Executive Order 13905: Strengthening National Resilience Through Responsible Use of PNT Services

`res:eo-13905-responsible-pnt` - standard - <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2020/02/18/2020-03337/strengthening-national-resilience-through-responsible-use-of-positioning-navigation-and-timing-services>

**By:** The White House - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

US executive order that defines 'responsible use of PNT' and tasks Commerce/NIST, DHS and others to produce PNT profiles, identify critical infrastructure dependencies and pilot-test resilience. It is the root of NIST IR 8323 and the DHS conformance framework.

## Key takeaways

- Treats PNT disruption as a critical infrastructure risk
- Directs creation of voluntary PNT profiles
- Calls for testing PNT resilience in federal contracts
