# ALTO: A Large-Scale Dataset for UAV Visual Place Recognition and Localization

`res:alto-dataset-2022` - dataset - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12317>

**By:** Ivan Cisneros, Peng Yin, Ji Zhang, Howie Choset, Sebastian Scherer - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Helicopter-flown aerial dataset over Ohio and Pennsylvania (two long trajectories) with GPS-INS ground truth, accelerometer, laser altimeter and downward RGB imagery, plus reference imagery for VPR benchmarking. The MetaSLAM/ALTO repo is BSD-3-Clause; no separate data licence is stated.

## Key takeaways

- Long-range aerial VPR and registration benchmark
- Includes laser altimeter (TRN-adjacent)
- Repo BSD-3-Clause
