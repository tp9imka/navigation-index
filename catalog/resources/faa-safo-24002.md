# FAA SAFO 24002: Recognizing and Mitigating GPS/GNSS Disruptions

`res:faa-safo-24002` - standard - <https://www.faa.gov/other_visit/aviation_industry/airline_operators/airline_safety/safo/all_safos/SAFO24002.pdf>

**By:** Federal Aviation Administration - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Safety alert for operators describing how jamming and spoofing manifest in the cockpit and what crews should do: revert to conventional procedures, report to ATC, and file written reports.

## Key takeaways

- Describes cockpit symptoms of jamming vs spoofing
- Advises readiness to use conventional navaids
- Later FAA guidance revisions followed in 2026
