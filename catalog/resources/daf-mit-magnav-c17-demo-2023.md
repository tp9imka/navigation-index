# DAF-MIT AI Accelerator MagNav real-time C-17 demonstration (2023)

`res:daf-mit-magnav-c17-demo-2023` - program - <https://www.airandspaceforces.com/air-force-magnetic-navigation-gps/>

**By:** DAF-MIT AI Accelerator, AFIT, USAF - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

In May 2023 the Air Force and MIT flew a real-time MagNav demo on a C-17A during exercise Golden Phoenix, training the compensation network in flight on a laptop. Follows the open MagNav challenge problem and dataset.

## Key takeaways

- No accuracy figure is public: the linked article and the official releases defer it to a government technical report. The value shown is RF independence
- Public dataset and Julia/Python code make this the most reproducible alt-PNT track for a hackathon
