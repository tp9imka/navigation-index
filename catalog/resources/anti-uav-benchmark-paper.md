# Anti-UAV: A Large Multi-Modal Benchmark for UAV Tracking

`res:anti-uav-benchmark-paper` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08466>

**By:** Jiang, Zhao et al. - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Introduces the Anti-UAV RGB and thermal infrared video benchmark for UAV tracking and an evaluation protocol.

## Key takeaways

- Thermal IR matters for night and small-target tracking
- Targets frequently disappear, so trackers must handle absence
