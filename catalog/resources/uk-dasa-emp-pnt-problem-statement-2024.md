# UK DASA Early Market Engagement: PNT problem statement for uncrewed systems

`res:uk-dasa-emp-pnt-problem-statement-2024` - program - <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/early-market-engagement-pilot-uncrewed-systems/problem-statement-position-navigation-and-timing-pnt>

**By:** UK Defence and Security Accelerator - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Published September 2024. Asks for assured PNT for small UAS: low-cost, low-SWaP CRPA (multi-frequency, SDR-based), SDR/GPU GNSS receivers, AI for spoofing detection and multi-source fusion, as plug-in replacements for COTS GNSS.

## Key takeaways

- Explicit platform example: under 1.5 m wingspan
- Unit cost must suit mass use - cost is a first-class requirement
