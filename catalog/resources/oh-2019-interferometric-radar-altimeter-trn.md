# Accurate Measurement Calculation Method for Interferometric Radar Altimeter-Based Terrain Referenced Navigation

`res:oh-2019-interferometric-radar-altimeter-trn` - paper - <https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/19/7/1688>

**By:** Juhyun Oh, Chang-Ky Sung, Jungshin Lee, Sang Woo Lee, Sang Jeong Lee, Myeong-Jong Yu - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Open-access Sensors paper on TRN with an interferometric radar altimeter, which outputs slant range and cross-track look angle to the closest terrain point. It derives a formula for that point's 3D relative position using angle of attack and sideslip instead of Euler angles, checks it on real flight data, and shows in TRN simulation that wind otherwise biases the result.

## Key takeaways

- Interferometric altimeters give range plus look angle, not just height
- Wind (alpha/beta) matters for the measurement model
- Real flight data used for validation
