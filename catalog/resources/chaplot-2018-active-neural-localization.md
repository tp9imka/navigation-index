# Active Neural Localization

`res:chaplot-2018-active-neural-localization` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.08214>

**By:** Devendra Singh Chaplot, Emilio Parisotto, Ruslan Salakhutdinov - **Year:** 2018 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

ICLR 2018. A differentiable structured belief over pose combined with a reinforcement-learned policy that moves to localize in few steps; tested in 2D mazes, Doom and an Unreal office. Abstract only read.

## Key takeaways

- Learned counterpart of active Markov localization
- Discrete actions, ground agent
