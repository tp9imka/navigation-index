# Pilots and Other Predictable Elements of the Starlink Ku-Band Downlink

`res:qin-psiaki-2026-starlink-pilots` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02627>

**By:** Wenkai Qin, Mark L. Psiaki, John R. Bowman, Todd E. Humphreys - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Identifies dedicated edge-pilot symbols and other predictable elements in the Starlink Ku-band waveform, publishes the edge-pilot sequences, and shows most QPSK symbols follow a regular template rather than carrying user data. Exploiting these elements gives a large processing gain so compact low-gain receivers can get precise time of arrival even from side beams.

## Key takeaways

- Explicit Starlink pilot sequences disclosed
- Predictable frame content enables TOA with small antennas
- UT Austin with Psiaki (Virginia Tech)
