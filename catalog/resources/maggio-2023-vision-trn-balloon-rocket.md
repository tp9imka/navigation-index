# Vision-Based Terrain Relative Navigation on High-Altitude Balloon and Sub-Orbital Rocket

`res:maggio-2023-vision-trn-balloon-rocket` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08011>

**By:** Dominic Maggio, Courtney Mario, Brett Streetman, Ted Steiner, Luca Carlone - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

AIAA SciTech 2023 paper evaluating camera-based TRN that matches landmarks from a satellite image database and uses inertial data between frames. Tested on a World View high-altitude balloon descending from 33 km and on Blue Origin New Shepard NS-23 camera data, with robustness to fast rotation and cloud obstruction.

## Key takeaways

- Real flight data, not simulation
- Visual TRN across a large altitude range
- Draper/MIT work
