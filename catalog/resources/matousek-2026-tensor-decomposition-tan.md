# Tensor Decompositions for Online Grid-Based Terrain-Aided Navigation

`res:matousek-2026-tensor-decomposition-tan` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07728>

**By:** J. Matousek, J. Krejci, J. Dunik, R. Zanetti - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Preprint (in review for FUSION 2026) that scales grid-based filters to higher state dimensions with a Lagrangian time update and low-rank tensor decompositions, exploiting block-diagonal dynamics and sparsely coupled measurements. Demonstrated on terrain-aided navigation, with reproducible MATLAB code.
