# The Use of GPS Disciplined Oscillators as Primary Frequency Standards for Calibration and Metrology Laboratories

`res:nist-lombardi-gpsdo-calibration` - paper - <https://tf.nist.gov/general/pdf/2297.pdf>

**By:** M. A. Lombardi (NIST) - **Year:** 2008 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Explains how GPSDOs work, why they are attractive as self-calibrating references, and where they fall short of a cesium standard, including behaviour when the GPS signal is lost.

## Key takeaways

- A GPSDO is only as good as its local oscillator once GNSS is gone
- Discusses traceability and verification of GPSDO outputs
