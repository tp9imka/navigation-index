# EnRicH 2025 - The European Robotics Hackathon

`res:enrich-2025-robotics-hackathon` - competition - <https://enrich.european-robotics.eu/enrich-2025/>

**By:** European Robotics, Fraunhofer FKIE - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Event page for a UAV and UGV trial inside the never-operated Zwentendorf nuclear plant: radiation mapping, 3D mapping, search and rescue and valve manipulation in dark, cluttered, comms-hostile interiors. Runs last about 30 minutes; there is no point formula.

## Key takeaways

- UAV and UGV start from the same point in an unknown interior
- Radiation sensor mandatory; maps handed over as point clouds and ROS bags
- Judges' awards instead of a published score
