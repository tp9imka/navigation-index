**By:** ModalAI - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

How voxl-vision-hub turns stereo depth into OBSTACLE_DISTANCE messages for PX4 or ArduPilot collision prevention on VOXL boards.

## Key takeaways

- Disabled by default; enable en_voa and the DFS server
- Stereo is the documented input; ToF is community-only
- Still needs PX4 Collision Prevention params
