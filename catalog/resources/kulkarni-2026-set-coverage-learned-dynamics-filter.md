# Proprioceptive-only State Estimation for Legged Robots with Set-Coverage Measurements of Learned Dynamics

**By:** Abhijeet M. Kulkarni, Ioannis Poulakakis, Guoquan Huang - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Preprint. Replaces the Gaussian noise assumption on learned measurement models with distribution-free set-coverage statements and shows how to use them inside a Gaussian filter. Legged robots, but the mechanism transfers to any learned measurement fed to an EKF.
