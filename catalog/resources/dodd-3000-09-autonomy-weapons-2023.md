# DoD Directive 3000.09: Autonomy in Weapon Systems (2023 reissue)

`res:dodd-3000-09-autonomy-weapons-2023` - standard - <https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodd/300009p.PDF>

**By:** US Department of Defense - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

DoD policy governing development and use of autonomous and semi-autonomous weapon systems, reissued January 2023. Cited here as the boundary marker: navigation and awareness tools are outside its scope, weapon autonomy is not.

## Key takeaways

- Clear line between navigation/autonomy for mobility and weapon autonomy
- Keep hackathon scope on the navigation and awareness side
