# DSEC: A Stereo Event Camera Dataset for Driving Scenarios

`res:uzh-dsec-dataset` - dataset - <https://dsec.ifi.uzh.ch/>

**By:** University of Zurich RPG - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Stereo event and frame cameras plus lidar and RTK GNSS on a car, including night and challenging illumination.

## Key takeaways

- Sensors: stereo event, stereo RGB, lidar, RTK GNSS
- Good for low-light perception
