# Інтелектуальна кіберфізична система для навігації БПЛА в умовах дії засобів РЕБ противника

`res:khmnu-berezka-cps-uav-nav-under-ew-2026` - paper - <https://vottp.khmnu.edu.ua/index.php/vottp/article/download/866/822/1732>

**By:** K. Berezka, V. Sevostianov, Khmelnytskyi National University - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

English: 'Intelligent cyber-physical system for UAV navigation under enemy EW'. Journal article fusing IMU data with optical-flow cameras via an EKF to hold position without any external radio signal, with a comparison of sparse (Lucas-Kanade), dense (Farneback) and learned (RAFT) optical flow.

## Key takeaways

- Side-by-side of LK, Farneback and RAFT for UAV flow is a handy reference
- EKF remains the default fusion choice in Ukrainian applied work
- Framed explicitly around full loss of external RF
