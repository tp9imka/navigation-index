# ORB-SLAM3: An Accurate Open-Source Library for Visual, Visual-Inertial and Multi-Map SLAM

`res:orb-slam3-campos-2020` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.11898>

**By:** Carlos Campos, Richard Elvira, Juan J. Gómez Rodríguez, José M. M. Montiel, Juan D. Tardós - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Describes ORB-SLAM3's tightly integrated visual-inertial SLAM, MAP-based IMU initialization and the multi-map Atlas that recovers from tracking loss.

## Key takeaways

- Multi-map atlas lets the system restart and later merge maps
- Supports pinhole and fisheye models
