# FAST-LIVO2: Fast, Direct LiDAR-Inertial-Visual Odometry

`res:fast-livo2-zheng-2024` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14035>

**By:** Chunran Zheng, Wei Xu, Zuhao Zou, et al. - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Presents an error-state iterated Kalman filter that sequentially fuses LiDAR and image measurements against a shared voxel map, with exposure and plane-prior handling.

## Key takeaways

- Sequential updates handle LiDAR/camera dimension mismatch
- Robust when one modality degrades
