**By:** Sebastian O. H. Madgwick - **Year:** 2010 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Internal report introducing the gradient-descent orientation filter now known as the Madgwick filter, for IMU and MARG sensors.

- Low computational cost attitude filter
- Single tuning parameter
