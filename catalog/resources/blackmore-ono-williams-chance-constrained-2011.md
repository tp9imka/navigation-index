# Chance-Constrained Optimal Path Planning With Obstacles

`res:blackmore-ono-williams-chance-constrained-2011` - paper - <https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/80914>

**By:** Lars Blackmore, Masahiro Ono, Brian C. Williams - **Year:** 2011 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Plans the future probability distribution of a linear-Gaussian vehicle so that the chance of collision or leaving the allowed region stays under a set bound. Bounds the collision probability so the problem becomes a disjunctive convex program, solved by branch-and-bound or a faster near-optimal method. IEEE T-RO 27(6), doi 10.1109/TRO.2011.2161160.

- The theory behind 'margin grows with position uncertainty'
