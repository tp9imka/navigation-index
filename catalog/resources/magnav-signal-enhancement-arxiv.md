# Signal Enhancement for Magnetic Navigation Challenge Problem

`res:magnav-signal-enhancement-arxiv` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.12158>

**By:** Gnadt, Belarge, et al. - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Paper defining the MagNav challenge: the physics of aircraft magnetic interference, the Tolles-Lawson baseline compensation, the dataset, and evaluation, framing it as a physics-informed ML problem.

## Key takeaways

- Tolles-Lawson is the classical baseline to beat
- Scalar magnetometer mixes Earth core, crustal anomaly and aircraft fields
- Dataset released on Zenodo
