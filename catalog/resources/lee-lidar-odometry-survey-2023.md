# LiDAR Odometry Survey: Recent Advancements and Remaining Challenges

`res:lee-lidar-odometry-survey-2023` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.17487>

**By:** Dongjae Lee, Minwoo Jung, Wooseong Yang, Ayoung Kim - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Survey of LiDAR-only, LiDAR-inertial and multi-sensor LiDAR odometry, with discussion of datasets, evaluation and open challenges such as degeneracy.

## Key takeaways

- Taxonomy of LiDAR, LiDAR-inertial and LiDAR-plus-other fusion
- Geometric degeneracy (tunnels, open fields) is a key open issue
