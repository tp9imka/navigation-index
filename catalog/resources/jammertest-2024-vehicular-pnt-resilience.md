# Data-Driven Analysis of PNT Resilience: Insights and Lessons from Jammertest 2024 Vehicular Data

`res:jammertest-2024-vehicular-pnt-resilience` - paper - <https://osuva.uwasa.fi/handle/11111/19667>

**By:** Jaakko Yliaho, Petri Välisuo, Elham Ahmadi, Kannan Selvan, Mahmoud Elsanhoury, Heidi Kuusniemi - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Analyses vehicle-mounted GNSS, phone, IMU and camera data collected at Jammertest 2024.
