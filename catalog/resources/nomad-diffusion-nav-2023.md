# NoMaD: Goal Masked Diffusion Policies for Navigation and Exploration

`res:nomad-diffusion-nav-2023` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07896>

**By:** Sridhar et al., UC Berkeley - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Single diffusion policy that handles both goal-directed navigation and undirected exploration by masking the goal.

## Key takeaways

- One model for explore and goal-reach
- Runs on small robots
