# NMEA 2000 Standard (CAN-based marine network)

`res:nmea-2000-standard` - standard - <https://www.nmea.org/nmea-2000.html>

**By:** National Marine Electronics Association - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** paywalled

CAN bus based marine network standard that carries position, heading, AIS and engine data as PGNs between devices. Paid standard; the community has reverse-documented many PGNs.

## Key takeaways

- PGNs carry GNSS position, COG/SOG, heading and AIS
- Plug-and-play backbone on modern vessels
- Unauthenticated bus, relevant to maritime spoofing resilience
