# Researchers home in on origins of Russia's Baltic GPS jamming

`res:defensenews-baltic-jamming-origins-2025` - blog - <https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2025/07/02/researchers-home-in-on-origins-of-russias-baltic-gps-jamming/>

**By:** Linus Höller, Linus Höller (Defense News) - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Reporting on Gdynia Maritime University research tracing Baltic jamming to Russian sites such as Baltiysk in Kaliningrad, DLR comment on how cheap and mobile jammers are, and an analyst's heatmap of likely jammer locations built from aircraft ADS-B integrity data.

## Key takeaways

- Emitter localization from distributed monitors plus ADS-B is feasible
- Baltic interference has shifted to include spoofing
- Emitter localization from distributed measurements
- Shows the Baltic as a persistent interference zone
