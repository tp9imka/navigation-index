# Researchers home in on origins of Russia's Baltic GPS jamming

Report on Polish research tracing Baltic GNSS jamming to emitters in the Kaliningrad region, with context on the effects on aviation and shipping.

## Key takeaways

- Emitter localization from distributed monitors plus ADS-B is feasible
- Baltic interference has shifted to include spoofing
- Emitter localization from distributed measurements
- Shows the Baltic as a persistent interference zone
