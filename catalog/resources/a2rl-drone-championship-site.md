Official event page and season blogs. Season 1 (April 2025): single front-facing camera, IMU and onboard Jetson Orin NX, no GPS or remote control, 22 gates over 170 m. Season 2 (January 2026, UMEX): 14-gate track with ladder and split-S obstacles.

## Key takeaways

- Camera plus IMU only, onboard compute
- Race time is the score; no public rulebook found
