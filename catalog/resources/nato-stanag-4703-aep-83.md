# STANAG 4703 / AEP-83: Light Unmanned Aircraft Systems Airworthiness Requirements

`res:nato-stanag-4703-aep-83` - standard - <https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7ec5c940f0b62305b83155/20140916-STANAG-4703_AEP-83_A__1_.pdf>

**By:** NATO - **Year:** 2014 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

NATO airworthiness code for fixed-wing light UAS with MTOW up to 150 kg and impact energy above 66 J (public UK copy of the 2014 edition). STANAG 4671 / AEP-4671 covers 150-20,000 kg fixed-wing and derives from CS-23. A military customer will map a nav product against one of these.

## Key takeaways

- 150 kg is the dividing line between 4703 and 4671
- Current edition per retailer listings is STANAG 4703 Ed 3 (2023); this link is the 2014 text
