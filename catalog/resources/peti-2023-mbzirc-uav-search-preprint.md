# A Search Strategy and Vessel Detection in Maritime Environment Using Fixed-Wing UAVs

`res:peti-2023-mbzirc-uav-search-preprint` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12767>

**By:** Marijana Peti, Ana Milas, Natko Krasevac, Marko Krizmancic, Ivan Loncar, Nikola Miskovic, Stjepan Bogdan - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Preprint by the UNIZG-FER MBZIRC 2023 team. States the challenge frame: find one target vessel among seven types in a 4 km2 sea area within 60 minutes, without GNSS and with short-range comms, then compares coverage patterns and a radar-informed search in the official simulator.

## Key takeaways

- B is unknown: the task is search, then approach
- GNSS banned for positioning; limits on UAV and sensor counts
- Synthetic data from a second simulator trained the detector
