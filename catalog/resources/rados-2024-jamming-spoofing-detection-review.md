# Recent Advances on Jamming and Spoofing Detection in GNSS

`res:rados-2024-jamming-spoofing-detection-review` - paper - <https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/24/13/4210>

**By:** Katarina Radoš, Marta Brkić, Dinko Begušić - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Open-access Sensors review of GNSS jamming and spoofing detection methods, finding machine learning is now the dominant approach, and covering combined jamming-plus-spoofing scenarios.

## Key takeaways

- ML-based detection dominates recent work
- Separates jamming-only, spoofing-only and combined attacks
