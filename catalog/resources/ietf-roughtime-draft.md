# Roughtime (draft-ietf-ntp-roughtime)

`res:ietf-roughtime-draft` - standard - <https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-ntp-roughtime/>

**By:** IETF NTP working group - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

IETF draft specifying Roughtime: signed coarse time responses that let clients chain queries across servers and prove misbehaviour.

## Key takeaways

- Coarse but cryptographically accountable time
- Good boot-time sanity bound for GNSS time
