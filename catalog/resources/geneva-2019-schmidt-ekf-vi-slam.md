# An Efficient Schmidt-EKF for 3D Visual-Inertial SLAM

**By:** Patrick Geneva, James Maley, Guoquan Huang - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

CVPR 2019. Treats prior-map features as Schmidt (consider) states: their covariance and cross-correlations enter the update without being estimated, keeping the filter consistent and cheap.
