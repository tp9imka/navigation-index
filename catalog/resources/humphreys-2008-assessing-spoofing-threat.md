# Assessing the Spoofing Threat: Development of a Portable GPS Civilian Spoofer

`res:humphreys-2008-assessing-spoofing-threat` - paper - <https://rnl.ae.utexas.edu/images/stories/files/papers/ion2008r01_for_distributionW.pdf>

**By:** Todd E. Humphreys, Brent M. Ledvina, Mark L. Psiaki, Brady W. O'Hanlon, Paul M. Kintner - **Year:** 2008 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Landmark ION GNSS 2008 paper that built a receiver-based civil GPS spoofer and analysed how hard it is to detect, kicking off modern civil spoofing defense research.

## Key takeaways

- Receiver-based spoofers can align with authentic signals and drag the victim smoothly
- Simple checks are insufficient against a sophisticated spoofer
- Motivates cryptographic and signal-level defenses
