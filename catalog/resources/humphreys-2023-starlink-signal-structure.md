# Signal Structure of the Starlink Ku-Band Downlink

`res:humphreys-2023-starlink-signal-structure` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.11578>

**By:** Todd E. Humphreys, et al. - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Reverse-engineers the Starlink Ku-band downlink as OFDM and identifies synchronization sequences usable for opportunistic ranging and positioning.

## Key takeaways

- Starlink frames contain known sequences usable as pilots
- Enables LEO signals-of-opportunity PNT without operator cooperation
