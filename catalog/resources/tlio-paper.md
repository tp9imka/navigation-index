# TLIO: Tight Learned Inertial Odometry

`res:tlio-paper` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01867>

**By:** Wenxin Liu, David Caruso, Eddy Ilg, Jing Dong, Anastasios I. Mourikis, Kostas Daniilidis, Vijay Kumar, Jakob Engel - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Combines a network that predicts displacement and uncertainty from IMU data with an EKF, for pedestrian inertial odometry.

## Key takeaways

- Learned measurement plus learned uncertainty in a filter
