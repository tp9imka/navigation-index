# The Invisible War: Inside the electronic warfare arms race that could shape course of war in Ukraine

`res:kyiv-independent-invisible-war-ew` - blog - <https://kyivindependent.com/the-invisible-war-inside-the-electronic-warfare-arms-race-that-could-shape-course-of-the-war>

**By:** Oleksandr Tartachnyi - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Long-form reporting on the EW and counter-EW cycle in Ukraine, covering jamming of drones and navigation and the rapid iteration of countermeasures.

## Key takeaways

- GPS-guided Excalibur shells and JDAM bombs lost accuracy to Russian jamming within months to a year of delivery
- Ukraine's Pokrova system jams and spoofs satellite navigation; officials claim it cut the accuracy of spoofed missiles by 40%
