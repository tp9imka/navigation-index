# MASt3R-SLAM: Real-Time Dense SLAM with 3D Reconstruction Priors

`res:mast3r-slam-murai-2024` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12392>

**By:** Riku Murai, Eric Dexheimer, Andrew J. Davison - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Builds a real-time dense monocular SLAM system on the MASt3R two-view reconstruction prior, without assuming a fixed parametric camera model.

## Key takeaways

- 3D foundation models can serve as SLAM front ends
- Works with uncalibrated video
