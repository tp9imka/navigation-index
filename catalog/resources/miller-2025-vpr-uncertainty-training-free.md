# Through the Lens of Doubt: Robust and Efficient Uncertainty Estimation for Visual Place Recognition

`res:miller-2025-vpr-uncertainty-training-free` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13464>

**By:** Emily Miller, Michael Milford, Muhammad Burhan Hafez, SD Ramchurn, Shoaib Ehsan - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Preprint. Three training-free confidence scores for any VPR method computed from the similarity-score distribution (separation, ratio spread, and a combination). Cheap inputs for a calibrated accept/abstain gate.

## Key takeaways

- No training, no geometric verification needed
- Abstract read only
