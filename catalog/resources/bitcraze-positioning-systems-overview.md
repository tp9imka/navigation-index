# Positioning Systems Overview

`res:bitcraze-positioning-systems-overview` - doc - <https://www.bitcraze.io/documentation/system/positioning/>

**By:** Bitcraze - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Bitcraze comparison of Crazyflie positioning options: Lighthouse (listed 'mm', 8x8x3 m, up to 4 base stations), Loco UWB (listed 'dm', at least 4 anchors, 8 preferred, 50x50 m untested), motion capture (mm, external cameras) and relative options such as the Flow deck.

## Key takeaways

- Vendor table: read the paper for measured numbers
- Lighthouse and mocap need line of sight; UWB tolerates some occlusion at dm accuracy
