# Onboard Safety Guarantees for Racing Drones: High-speed Geofencing with Control Barrier Functions

`res:singletary-racing-drone-geofencing-cbf-2022` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.04331>

**By:** Andrew Singletary, Aiden Swann, Yuxiao Chen, Aaron D. Ames - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Implicit CBF from a backup controller that avoids derivatives, cheap enough to run on a 10 g microcontroller. Geofences a 7-inch racing drone at over 100 km/h per the abstract.

## Key takeaways

- Geofence safety filter that fits on the flight controller
- Backup-set CBF on full quadrotor dynamics needs more compute
