# Exploiting Starlink Signals for Navigation: First Results

`res:kassas-starlink-first-results-2022` - paper - <https://ece.osu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-09/Exploiting_Starlink_signals_for_navigation_first_results.pdf>

**By:** Mohammad Neinavaie, Joe Khalife, Zaher M. Kassas - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Early experimental demonstration of opportunistic positioning by tracking Doppler from Starlink satellites, without cooperation from the operator.

## Key takeaways

- Starlink can be used as an uncooperative PNT source
- Ephemeris error is a dominant limitation
