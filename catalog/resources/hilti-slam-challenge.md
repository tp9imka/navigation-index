# Hilti x Trimble SLAM Challenge 2026

`res:hilti-slam-challenge` - competition - <https://hilti-challenge.com/>

**By:** Hilti, Trimble, University of Oxford (Dynamic Robot Systems Group) - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Yearly SLAM benchmark challenge with datasets and leaderboards from 2021 to 2026. The 2026 edition provides 360-degree camera and IMU sequences captured on active construction sites with floor-plan priors, scored in SLAM and floor-plan Localization categories; the live evaluation system stays open after the challenge. Datasets are CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (non-commercial).

## Key takeaways

- Hard GNSS-denied indoor sequences with accurate ground truth
- Non-commercial licence (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0): fine for a hackathon demo, not for a product
- Sensors: lidar, cameras, IMU
- GNSS-denied indoor
- Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, non-commercial
- 2026 edition is the Hilti x Trimble challenge with 360 camera, IMU and floor plan priors
