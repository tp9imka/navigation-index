# Hilti SLAM Challenge

`res:hilti-slam-challenge` - competition - <https://hilti-challenge.com/>

**By:** Hilti, University of Oxford, University of Bonn - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

SLAM benchmark challenge with datasets from handheld and robot-mounted multi-sensor rigs in construction sites and buildings, run with an associated ICRA workshop and public leaderboard.

## Key takeaways

- Hard GNSS-denied indoor sequences with accurate ground truth
- Sensors: lidar, cameras, IMU
- GNSS-denied indoor
