English: 'European Defense Tech Hackathon: how startups in Munich tackle Ukraine's defense problems'. Report from the Feb 2025 Munich edition (48 h, 200+ participants). ## Key takeaways

- Hackathon outputs are valued as quickly integrable components
- Underwater navigation appeared as a track
- Teams, networks and investor access are the main realistic outcomes of 48 h
