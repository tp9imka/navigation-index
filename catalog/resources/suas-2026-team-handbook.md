# SUAS 2026 Team Handbook

`res:suas-2026-team-handbook` - competition - <https://robonation.gitbook.io/suas-resources/2026-team-handbook>

**By:** RoboNation - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Rules for the 2026 Student Unmanned Aerial Systems competition: GPS waypoint laps handed out at check-in, a GPS flight boundary with an altitude band, risk mapping, and search-detect-deliver of two objects to a mannequin and a tent. Mission demonstration is worth 1000 points with published formulas.

## Key takeaways

- Waypoints within 100 ft, up to 15 per lap of about 2 miles
- Endurance 20 + 30 + 200 x (laps/10)^2; operators 200 x min(1, (4 - O)/2)
- Each delivery 20 survive + 50 within 50 ft + 30 correct target
