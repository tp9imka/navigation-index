# NATO Joint Military Symbology (APP-6) - overview

`res:app-6-joint-military-symbology` - doc - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO_Joint_Military_Symbology>

**By:** NATO (summary: Wikipedia) - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Overview of APP-6, the NATO standard for map symbols of units, equipment and tactical graphics, now at edition E and aligned with the US MIL-STD-2525 family. Explains frames, affiliation colours and the symbol identification code.

## Key takeaways

- Affiliation (friend/hostile/neutral/unknown) is encoded by frame shape and colour
- APP-6 and MIL-STD-2525 share a common symbol code structure
- Open renderers implement it for COP displays
