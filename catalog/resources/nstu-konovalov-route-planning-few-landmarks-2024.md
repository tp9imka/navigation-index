English: 'UAV route planning algorithm under a shortage of visual landmarks'. Journal article (Analysis and Data Processing Systems, vol. 94 no. 2) on planning routes so a video-navigation system keeps seeing enough landmarks when satellite navigation is unavailable.

- Navigation-aware path planning: route choice is a resilience lever
