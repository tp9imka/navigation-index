# UNITE - Brave NATO: Brave1 and NATO Innovation HQ joint competition (2026)

`res:brave-nato-unite-2026` - program - <https://resiliencemedia.co/brave1-and-nato-innovation-hq-team-up-to-fast-track-defence-tech/>

**By:** Brave1, NATO - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

March 2026 programme pairing Ukrainian and NATO-country companies on joint solutions: first round EUR 10M, up to EUR 1M per project split between partners. Focus areas are counter-UAS and navigation where EW disrupts positioning; NATO-side funding limited to non-lethal categories.

## Key takeaways

- Ministerial framing: 'robust navigation in GPS-denied environments' as a critical field
