# GPS: An Introduction to Satellite Navigation, with an interactive Worldwide Laboratory using Smartphones

`res:stanford-gps-mooc-van-diggelen-enge` - course - <https://sites.google.com/view/frankvandiggelen/gps-mooc>

**By:** Frank van Diggelen, Per Enge, Stanford University - **Year:** 2014 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Stanford MOOC (originally on Coursera) now freely available as a long video series: how GPS works, pseudoranges, orbits and signals, receiver design, assisted GPS and the future of GNSS, with smartphone-based labs.

## Key takeaways

- Best intro to pseudoranges and receiver basics
- Smartphone labs pair with Android raw GNSS measurements
