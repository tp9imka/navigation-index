# GPS: глушилки, спуфинг и уязвимости

`res:habr-gps-jammers-spoofing-2015` - blog - <https://habr.com/ru/companies/neuronspace/articles/254877/>

**By:** MagisterLudi, Neuron Hackspace - **Year:** 2015 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'GPS: jammers, spoofing and vulnerabilities'. Early Russian-language overview: how spoofing gradually walks a receiver off, the 2013 UT Austin yacht demonstration, consumer jammers sold in Russia, lab results on car jammers, and proposed defenses. The comment thread adds practitioner views (TERCOM as a GPS backup, antenna shielding limits).

## Key takeaways

- Gradual pull-off is the core spoofing technique
- Cheap personal jammers were already widespread years before 2022
- Comments are a source of practitioner folklore, not verified data
