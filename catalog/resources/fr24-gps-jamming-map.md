# Flightradar24 GPS jamming map

`res:fr24-gps-jamming-map` - dataset - <https://www.flightradar24.com/data/gps-jamming>

**By:** Flightradar24 - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Flightradar24's map of GNSS interference, computed from navigation integrity category values broadcast by aircraft over its receiver network.

## Key takeaways

- Grey areas mean insufficient flights or receivers, not absence of jamming
- Covers all GNSS, not just GPS
