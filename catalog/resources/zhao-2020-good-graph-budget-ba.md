# Good Graph to Optimize: Cost-Effective, Budget-Aware Bundle Adjustment in Visual SLAM

`res:zhao-2020-good-graph-budget-ba` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.10123>

**By:** Yipu Zhao, Justin S. Smith, Patricio A. Vela - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

arXiv 2008.10123 preprint. Predicts upcoming estimation needs, sets a per-step size budget and picks the best-conditioned subgraph for local bundle adjustment; better accuracy under compute limits and better closed-loop tracking. Abstract only read.

## Key takeaways

- Budget-aware SLAM back end with an explicit forecast of need
- Code builds on ORB-SLAM2 and inherits GPL-3.0
