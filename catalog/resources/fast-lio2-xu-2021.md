# FAST-LIO2: Fast Direct LiDAR-inertial Odometry

`res:fast-lio2-xu-2021` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.06829>

**By:** Wei Xu, Yixi Cai, Dongjiao He, Jiarong Lin, Fu Zhang - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Introduces direct registration of raw LiDAR points in an iterated Kalman filter with an incremental k-d tree map, avoiding hand-crafted feature extraction.

## Key takeaways

- Raw-point registration generalizes across LiDAR types
- ikd-Tree enables efficient incremental mapping
