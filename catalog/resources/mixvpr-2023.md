# MixVPR: Feature Mixing for Visual Place Recognition

`res:mixvpr-2023` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02190>

**By:** Amar Ali-bey, Brahim Chaib-draa, Philippe Giguere - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

WACV 2023 paper proposing a holistic aggregation that mixes backbone feature maps through a cascade of feature-mixing blocks instead of NetVLAD-style local aggregation, aiming at accuracy with low latency. Trained on GSV-Cities.

## Key takeaways

- Simple, fast global descriptor
- Trained on GSV-Cities
- Good baseline for onboard VPR
