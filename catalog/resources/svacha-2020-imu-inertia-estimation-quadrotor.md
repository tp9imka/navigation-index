# IMU-Based Inertia Estimation for a Quadrotor Using Newton-Euler Dynamics

`res:svacha-2020-imu-inertia-estimation-quadrotor` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2020.2976308>

**By:** James Svacha, James Paulos, Giuseppe Loianno, Vijay Kumar - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE RA-L 5(3). A 32-state UKF, 14 of them constant parameters, estimates tilt, angular and linear velocity and inertial parameters from an IMU and motor-speed feedback, with an analytical observability result; a blade-flapping moment term is reported as necessary for a sensible inertia estimate. Only the abstract was read here.

## Key takeaways

- Online parameter estimation from IMU plus motor speed is shown observable analytically (abstract claim)
