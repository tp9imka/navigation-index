# SandboxAQ AQNav geomagnetic navigation

`res:sandboxaq-aqnav` - product - <https://www.sandboxaq.com/post/sandboxaq-and-acubed-achieve-remarkable-progress-in-magnetic-navigation>

**By:** SandboxAQ - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

AQNav is SandboxAQ's magnetic anomaly navigation system: magnetometers plus learned compensation, matched against magnetic anomaly maps. Flight-tested with Acubed (Airbus) on a Beechcraft Baron using public maps, with the USAF on C-17/C-130J, and in 2026 on a Northrop Grumman Lumberjack drone.

## Key takeaways

- Vendor claims: RNP 2 compliance over 150+ flight hours; outperformed free-inertial INS on all >2 h flights
- Sept 2026 drone test announcement gave no error figures (noted by press)
- Accepted into the 2025 NATO DIANA cohort (vendor/press)
