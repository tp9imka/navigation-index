# Navipedia

`res:esa-navipedia` - doc - <https://gssc.esa.int/navipedia/index.php/Main_Page>

**By:** ESA, European Space Agency - **Year:** 2011 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

ESA-maintained wiki on GNSS: systems, signals, receivers, error sources, positioning algorithms (SPP, DGNSS, RTK, PPP), augmentation and applications.

## Key takeaways

- Best free reference for GNSS concepts
- Good RAIM and error-model explanations
- Reliable quick reference for GNSS equations
- Covers Galileo signal and service details
