# Quadrotors and Accelerometers: State Estimation with an Improved Dynamic Model

`res:leishman-2014-quadrotors-accelerometers` - paper - <https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/facpub/1419>

**By:** Robert C. Leishman, John Macdonald, Randal W. Beard, Timothy W. McLain - **Year:** 2014 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

IEEE Control Systems Magazine 34(1), 2014. Explains why a multirotor accelerometer measures rotor drag rather than gravity tilt in steady flight, and uses a drag-augmented dynamic model to estimate attitude and velocity from the IMU alone.

## Key takeaways

- The physics argument behind EKF3/EKF2 drag fusion
- Hardware results show better attitude, velocity and position estimates (numbers not checked here)
- Open copy on BYU ScholarsArchive
