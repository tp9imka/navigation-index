# Terrain-Aided Navigation Without Raycasting: A Sliding Grid Approach for Point Cloud Processing

`res:sanlan-2025-sliding-grid-tan-point-cloud` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06470>

**By:** Abdulbaki Sanlan, Fatih Erol, Murad Abu-Khalaf, Emre Koyuncu - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Compares two DEM-based measurement models for point-cloud (lidar-style) terrain-aided INS: classic ray casting versus a 'sliding grid' that needs no ray casting. The sliding grid gives comparable accuracy, especially at higher altitude, at much lower compute, targeting embedded hardware.

## Key takeaways

- Point-cloud TAN beyond a single altimeter beam
- Avoids GPU-hungry ray casting
- Relevant to scanning lidar or radar altimeters
