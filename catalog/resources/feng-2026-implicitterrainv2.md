# ImplicitTerrainV2: Wavelet-Guided Spatially Adaptive Neural Terrain Representation

`res:feng-2026-implicitterrainv2` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22556>

**By:** Haoan Feng, Xin Xu, Leila De Floriani - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Preprint. Adds wavelet-guided spatially adaptive frequency control, derivative-aware training and quantization plus entropy coding to neural terrain fields, aiming at a compact DEM format with good gradients.

## Key takeaways

- Search-snippet numbers: 1.23 bits per pixel with a 0.28 dB PSNR drop on 50 Swiss tiles
- MIT code
- Derivative-aware training matters for gradient-based TRN
