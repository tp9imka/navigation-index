# GPS Standard Positioning Service (SPS) Performance Standard, 5th Edition

`res:gps-sps-performance-standard-2020` - standard - <https://archive.gps.gov/technical/ps/2020-SPS-performance-standard.pdf>

**By:** US DoD - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

The formal commitment the US government makes about civil GPS signal-in-space accuracy, availability, continuity and integrity. It is the baseline to compare against when judging whether a receiver anomaly is the signal or interference.

## Key takeaways

- States guaranteed SIS user range error and availability levels
- Defines what counts as a major service failure
- Basis for RAIM and integrity assumptions in aviation
