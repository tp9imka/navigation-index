# TrustPoint - C-band LEO PNT

`res:trustpoint-leo-pnt` - company - <https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250625377848/en/TrustPoint-Launches-Third-Satellite-and-Successfully-Establishes-First-Contact>

**By:** TrustPoint - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

TrustPoint is developing a commercial C-band LEO navigation constellation. Its third demonstration satellite (Time Flies) launched in June 2025 carrying a C-band payload for testing partner receivers.

## Key takeaways

- Vendor claim: commercial GPS alternative from LEO; no independent performance reports found
- C-band choice moves PNT away from crowded and jammed L-band
