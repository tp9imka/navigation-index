# End-of-Life (EOL) Announcement for some Intel RealSense products

`res:intel-realsense-tracking-eol` - doc - <https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000087450/emerging-technologies/intel-realsense-technology.html>

**By:** Intel - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Intel support notice listing the Tracking Camera T265 and T261 among discontinued RealSense products; stereo D400 cameras stay available. Sales of T265 stopped in 2022 and newer SDKs dropped some EOL support code.

## Key takeaways

- Do not plan a new build around T265
- Replacement path: D435i / D455 plus third-party VIO
