# Navigation using carrier Doppler shift from a LEO constellation: TRANSIT on steroids

`res:psiaki-transit-on-steroids-2021` - paper - <https://navi.ion.org/content/68/3/621>

**By:** Mark L. Psiaki - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Analyzes positioning from carrier Doppler measurements of a large LEO constellation with a batch filter estimating position, velocity and clock terms, showing accuracy potentially comparable to pseudorange GNSS.

## Key takeaways

- Doppler-only LEO positioning is geometrically viable
- Needs many satellites in view; mega-constellations supply them
