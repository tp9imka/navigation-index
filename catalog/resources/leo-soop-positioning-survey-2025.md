# Survey on positioning technology based on signal of opportunity from low earth orbit

`res:leo-soop-positioning-survey-2025` - paper - <https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physics/articles/10.3389/fphy.2025.1592447/full>

**By:** Frontiers in Physics authors - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Open-access survey of LEO signals-of-opportunity positioning: Doppler methods, error sources, and extracting observables from signals never designed for navigation (unknown codes, poor onboard clocks).

## Key takeaways

- Doppler positioning is the workhorse for LEO SoOP
- Unknown modulation and clock quality are the core difficulties
