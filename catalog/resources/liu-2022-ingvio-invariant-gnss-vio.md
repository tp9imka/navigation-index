# InGVIO: A Consistent Invariant Filter for Fast and High-Accuracy GNSS-Visual-Inertial Odometry

`res:liu-2022-ingvio-invariant-gnss-vio` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.15145>

**By:** Changwu Liu, Chen Jiang, Haowen Wang - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Invariant filter that tightly fuses visual-inertial data with raw GNSS and degrades to VIO as satellites drop. Datasets include a fixed-wing aircraft with raw GNSS.
