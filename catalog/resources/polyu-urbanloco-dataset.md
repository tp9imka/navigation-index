# UrbanLoco: A Full Sensor Suite Dataset for Mapping and Localization in Urban Scenes

`res:polyu-urbanloco-dataset` - dataset - <https://advdataset2019.wixsite.com/urbanloco>

**By:** UC Berkeley, PolyU - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Driving data from Hong Kong and California urban canyons with a full sensor suite, targeted at GNSS outliers and degraded localization.

## Key takeaways

- Sensors: lidar, cameras, GNSS, IMU
- Focus: dense urban GNSS failure
- Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial academic use
