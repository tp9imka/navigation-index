# Empirical Evaluation of GNSS Timing Spoofing and Detection Using Machine Learning

`res:safi-2025-ipin-timing-spoofing-ml` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/IPIN66788.2025.11212945>

**By:** Muhammad Safi, Heidi Kuusniemi, Mahmoud Elsanhoury, Petri Valisuo - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** paywalled

IPIN 2025 paper on timing spoofing detection with Jammertest 2024 data. Position parameters were compromised at lower spoofing power than timing parameters, which could give early warning; NMEA validity flags detected much but not all of the attack; an unsupervised Isolation Forest closed the gap. Self-archived copies exist in the Vaasa and Tampere repositories.

## Key takeaways

- Position degrades before time: use it as an early warning
- Unsupervised detector, no labelled spoofing needed
- Targets critical-infrastructure timing receivers
