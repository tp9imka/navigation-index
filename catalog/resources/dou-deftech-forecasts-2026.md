# «Очікую прориву в українській ракетній програмі». Прогнози українського deftech на 2026

`res:dou-deftech-forecasts-2026` - blog - <https://dou.ua/lenta/articles/deftech-forecasts-for-2026/>

**By:** Ya. Zhakhalov (DOU) - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'Ukrainian deftech forecasts for 2026'. Industry round-up (25 Dec 2025). Navigation-relevant views: Vyriy expects mass adoption of GPS-free navigation based on radio beacons; Athlon Avia's CTO names the lack of a compact, reliable optical system that fully replaces GNSS as the year's main disappointment; Swarmer says visual SLAM, visual navigation and optic flow fused with inertial sensors passed field tests and that GPS-denied is now a baseline condition. EW vendors discuss CRPA-equipped threats and mesh-networked drones.

## Key takeaways

- Practitioners report optical navigation as still not a full GNSS replacement
- Beacon navigation expected to scale in 2026
- Operators value stable links and predictable behaviour under EW over range
- Strike-related forecasts in the article are out of scope here
