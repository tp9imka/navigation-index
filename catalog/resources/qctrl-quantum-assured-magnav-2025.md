# Quantum-assured magnetic navigation achieves positioning accuracy better than a strategic-grade INS in airborne and ground-based field trials

`res:qctrl-quantum-assured-magnav-2025` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08167>

**By:** Murat Muradoglu, Mattias T. Johnsson, Nathanial M. Wilson et al. - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Reports airborne and ground field trials of magnetic anomaly navigation using quantum magnetometers with map-matching, comparing against strategic-grade inertial navigation.

## Key takeaways

- Recent field evidence that MagNav is a practical GNSS alternative
- Sensor quality and noise rejection drive performance
