# Safe and Robust Observer-Controller Synthesis using Control Barrier Functions

`res:agrawal-panagou-observer-controller-cbf-2022` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14364>

**By:** Devansh R. Agrawal, Dimitra Panagou - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Builds CBFs from the bounded or input-to-state-stable error of the observer, giving QP controllers that stay safe from estimated states. Shows that a separately designed observer and controller can be unsafe. Quadrotor simulations and experiments. IEEE L-CSS 7:127-132.

## Key takeaways

- Design estimator and safety filter together
- Quadrotor flight test where ignoring estimate error leads to collision (per search summary)
