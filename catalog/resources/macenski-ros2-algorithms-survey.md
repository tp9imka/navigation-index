# From the Desks of ROS Maintainers: A Survey of Modern & Capable Mobile Robotics Algorithms in the Robot Operating System 2

`res:macenski-ros2-algorithms-survey` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15236>

**By:** Steve Macenski, Tom Moore, David V. Lu, Alexey Merzlyakov, Michael Ferguson - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Survey by ROS maintainers of the mobile robotics algorithms available in ROS 2: localization, SLAM, planning, control and perception packages, with their maturity and use.

## Key takeaways

- Map of which ROS 2 packages are production-ready
- Good orientation before choosing a localization or planning stack
