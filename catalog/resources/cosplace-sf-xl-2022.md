# Rethinking Visual Geo-localization for Large-Scale Applications

`res:cosplace-sf-xl-2022` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02287>

**By:** Gabriele Berton, Carlo Masone, Barbara Caputo - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

CVPR 2022 paper introducing San Francisco eXtra Large (SF-XL), a city-wide geo-localization dataset, and CosPlace, which casts VPR training as classification to avoid expensive negative mining. SF-XL is obtained through a request form that states it is 'publicly available for research purposes'; no formal licence is given. The CosPlace code is MIT.

## Key takeaways

- Classification training scales to city-size data
- SF-XL access via form, research use
- CosPlace code: MIT
