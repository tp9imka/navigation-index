# Trajectory Optimization for Self-Calibration and Navigation

`res:preiss-2017-trajectory-optimization-self-calibration` - paper - <https://roboticsproceedings.org/rss13/p54.html>

**By:** James Preiss, Karol Hausman, Gaurav S. Sukhatme, Stephan Weiss - **Year:** 2017 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

RSS 2017. Adds a state-observability cost to trajectory optimisation, with a statistical procedure to combine states of different units, so a robot calibrates while flying its mission. Only the abstract was read here.

## Key takeaways

- Observability cost inside mission planning removes a separate calibration flight (abstract claim)
- Relevant to the excitation-request part of the idea, not to update gating
