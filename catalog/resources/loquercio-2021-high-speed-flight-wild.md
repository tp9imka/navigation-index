# Learning High-Speed Flight in the Wild

`res:loquercio-2021-high-speed-flight-wild` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.05113>

**By:** Antonio Loquercio, Elia Kaufmann, Rene Ranftl, Matthias Muller, Vladlen Koltun, Davide Scaramuzza - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

A network trained only in simulation by imitating a privileged planner maps onboard depth and state to collision-free trajectories, flown fast in forests and ruins (Science Robotics 2021).

## Key takeaways

- Privileged-expert imitation transfers sim to real
- Depth plus state is enough for fast obstacle flight
