# EASA SIB 2022-02R3: Global Navigation Satellite System Outage and Alterations Leading to Communication / Navigation / Surveillance Degradation

`res:easa-sib-2022-02-gnss-outages` - doc - <https://ad.easa.europa.eu/blob/EASA_SIB_2022_02_R3.pdf/SIB_2022-02R3_1>

**By:** EASA - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

EASA's safety information bulletin on GNSS jamming and spoofing affecting aircraft, revised repeatedly since 2022 as interference spread around conflict zones. Lists symptoms and recommendations for authorities, ANSPs, operators and manufacturers; later revisions (R4) followed.

## Key takeaways

- Spoofing is harder for crews to detect than jamming and so the bigger safety risk
- Recommends cross-checking with non-GNSS navaids and reporting events
- Affected flight information regions are tracked on EASA's GNSS page
