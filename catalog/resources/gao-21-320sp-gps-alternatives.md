# GAO-21-320SP: Defense Navigation Capabilities - DOD Is Developing PNT Alternatives to GPS

`res:gao-21-320sp-gps-alternatives` - report - <https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-21-320sp>

**By:** US Government Accountability Office - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Technology assessment of DoD's alternative PNT efforts (inertial, vision, celestial, magnetic, signals of opportunity, clocks), with maturity status and challenges. A concise government map of the alt-PNT landscape.

## Key takeaways

- DOD keeps GPS at the core and adds complements
- Reviews maturity of many alt-PNT technology families
- Flags integration and testing as recurring challenges
