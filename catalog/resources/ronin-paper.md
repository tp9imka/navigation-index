# RoNIN: Robust Neural Inertial Navigation in the Wild: Benchmark, Evaluations, and New Methods

`res:ronin-paper` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.12853>

**By:** Sachini Herath, Hang Yan, Yasutaka Furukawa - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Introduces a large smartphone IMU dataset with ground truth and benchmarks neural architectures for pedestrian velocity regression.

## Key takeaways

- Benchmark dataset for learned pedestrian navigation
- Body-frame heading-agnostic representation
