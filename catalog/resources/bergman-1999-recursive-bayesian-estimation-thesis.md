# Recursive Bayesian Estimation: Navigation and Tracking Applications

`res:bergman-1999-recursive-bayesian-estimation-thesis` - report - <https://rt.isy.liu.se/research/reports/Ph.D.Thesis/PhD579.pdf>

**By:** Niclas Bergman - **Year:** 1999 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Linkoping University PhD thesis (Dissertation No. 579) on nonlinear, non-Gaussian recursive estimation motivated by terrain-aided aircraft navigation and target tracking. Covers the point-mass filter and particle filters for terrain navigation, compared with the Cramer-Rao bound. A standard English-language TRN reference with free full text.

## Key takeaways

- Point-mass filter and particle filter for TRN in one place
- Cramer-Rao bounds for terrain navigation
