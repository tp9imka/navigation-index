# Timing Properties of the Starlink Ku-Band Downlink

`res:qin-2025-starlink-timing-properties` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05302>

**By:** Wenkai Qin, Andrew M. Graff, Zachary L. Clements, Zacharias M. Komodromos, Todd E. Humphreys - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

IEEE TAES 62 (2026) paper characterizing Starlink frame timing for pseudorange-based opportunistic PNT. It finds ns-level jitter periods (good) but also abrupt timing adjustments, episodic high-jitter periods and only loose disciplining to GPS time, which the authors judge incompatible with GPS-like PNT unless corrected, and attribute to software choices.

## Key takeaways

- Starlink frame timing is disciplined to GPS time only loosely
- Abrupt per-second timing jumps on v1.0/v1.5 satellites
- Third-party corrections could fix it
