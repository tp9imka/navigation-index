# UK national eLoran, Timing Centre and GNSS warning investment (2025)

`res:uk-eloran-programme-2025` - program - <https://rntfnd.org/2025/11/19/uk-announces-155m-investment-in-timing-centre-eloran-gnss-warning/>

**By:** UK Government (reported by RNT Foundation) - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

November 2025 UK announcement of about GBP 155M for a national timing centre, a sovereign eLoran system and GNSS interference warning; a Find a Tender notice targets eLoran IOC by 2028 and FOC by 2030.

## Key takeaways

- Terrestrial backup and GNSS warning, not only alt-nav on the vehicle
