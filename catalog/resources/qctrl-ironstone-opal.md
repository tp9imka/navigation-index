# Q-CTRL Ironstone Opal quantum-assured magnetic navigation

`res:qctrl-ironstone-opal` - product - <https://q-ctrl.com/case-study/q-ctrl-delivering-quantum-advantage-to-airborne-systems>

**By:** Q-CTRL - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Q-CTRL's navigation product pairs quantum magnetometers with its software to match measured crustal magnetic anomalies against maps (MagNav). The company reports flight, ground and maritime trials and positions it as a GNSS backup for aviation, including work with Airbus.

## Key takeaways

- Vendor claims (not independent): RNP 0.3 met in flight (error under 0.3 nm for 95% of a flight); the linked case study claims 50x lower positioning uncertainty than an INS over a ~500 km flight and at least 11x with the sensor inside the cabin; other Q-CTRL materials give different multipliers
- Vendor claim: RTCA DO-160 environmental qualification - not an airworthiness or RNP operational approval
- Underlying method is the same map-matching problem as the open AFRL/MIT MagNav challenge
