# Game4Loc: A UAV Geo-Localization Benchmark from Game Data

`res:game4loc-2024` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16925>

**By:** Yuxiang Ji et al. - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Builds a large synthetic drone-satellite dataset from a video game with partial-match pairs and trains weighted contrastive geo-localization.

## Key takeaways

- Synthetic data transfers to real benchmarks
- Partial-match pairs are more realistic than perfect crops
