# GNSS L1 Jammed Signal 10MHz Recording - Triangular Wave (Part 1)

`res:zenodo-l1-jamming-iq-2025` - dataset - <https://zenodo.org/records/14859561>

**By:** LINKS Foundation - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

One of a series of February 2025 Zenodo records from LINKS Foundation with GNSS L1 IQ recordings at 10 and 20 MHz sample rates under several jamming waveforms (triangular, tick, linear narrow, linear wide fast) at stepped jamming powers. CC BY 4.0; Horizon Europe grant 101073909.

## Key takeaways

- More recent real IQ jamming captures
- Each file is about 10 GB of int8 complex samples
