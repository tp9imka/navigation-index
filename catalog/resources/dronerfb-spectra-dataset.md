# DroneRFb-Spectra: A RF spectrogram dataset for drone recognition

`res:dronerfb-spectra-dataset` - dataset - <https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/dronerfb-spectra-rf-spectrogram-dataset-drone-recognition>

**By:** Ningning Yu et al. - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** free-registration

Spectrograms derived from USRP IQ captures of drone controllers across ISM bands in urban settings, tied to an IEEE TIFS 2024 open-set drone recognition paper.

## Key takeaways

- Ready-made spectrograms - no SDR processing needed
- Covers many common controller brands
- Open-set recognition angle for unknown drones
