# Cooperative Localization in Wireless Networks

`res:wymeersch-2009-cooperative-localization-wireless` - paper - <https://winslab.lids.mit.edu/wp-content/papercite-data/pdf/wymliewin-j09.pdf>

**By:** Henk Wymeersch, Jaime Lien, Moe Z. Win - **Year:** 2009 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Proceedings of the IEEE 97(2) tutorial on cooperative localization in wireless networks, applied to ultra-wideband ranging. It surveys the approaches and introduces SPAWN, a fully distributed message-passing (factor graph) algorithm with low communication overhead.

## Key takeaways

- Good tutorial entry point for range-based cooperative positioning
- SPAWN: distributed belief propagation over the network
- UWB ranging used as the running application
