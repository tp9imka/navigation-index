# The Visual-Inertial-Dynamical Multirotor Dataset

`res:zhang-2021-vid-dataset-paper` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11152>

**By:** Kunyi Zhang, Tiankai Yang, Ziming Ding, Sheng Yang, Teng Ma, Mingyang Li, Chao Xu, Fei Gao - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Describes a multirotor dataset with camera, IMU, commanded and measured rotor speeds, motor currents, GPS/RTK and mocap ground truth, recorded on a custom platform with hardware time sync. arXiv lists no venue.

## Key takeaways

- Measured per-motor speed and current, not just commands
- Hardware time sync across IMU, camera and motors
- Venue not listed on arXiv
