# Sampling-based Algorithms for Optimal Motion Planning

`res:karaman-frazzoli-rrtstar-ijrr-2011` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.1186>

**By:** Sertac Karaman, Emilio Frazzoli - **Year:** 2011 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Peer-reviewed (IJRR 2011); arXiv copy linked. Introduces RRT* and PRM*, proving asymptotic optimality and showing that plain RRT converges to a suboptimal path.

## Key takeaways

- RRT* rewires to converge toward the optimal path
- Plain RRT paths are feasible but jagged and long
- Narrow passages remain hard for any uniform sampler
