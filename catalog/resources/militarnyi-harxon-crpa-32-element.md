# Компанія, що постачає CRPA-антени для російських дронів, показала нову 32-елементну модель

`res:militarnyi-harxon-crpa-32-element` - blog - <https://militarnyi.com/uk/news/kompaniya-shho-postachaye-crpa-anteny-dlya-rosijskyh-droniv-pokazala-novu-32-elementnu-model/>

**By:** Militarnyi - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

English: 'Company supplying CRPA antennas for Russian drones shows a new 32-element model'. Explains CRPA (controlled reception pattern antenna) as a multi-element array with digital beamforming that nulls interference directions while keeping satellite signals, and lists vendor specs of a 32-element Harxon unit shown at DWC2026 (up to 31 simultaneous interferers, under 6.5 kg, up to 90 W).

## Key takeaways

- CRPA element count is escalating (4 => 16 => 24 => 32) as an arms race against jamming
- An N-element array can null up to N-1 interference sources
- Size, weight and power of high-element CRPAs limit them to larger airframes
- Explains why broadband jamming alone is losing effectiveness against long-range UAVs
