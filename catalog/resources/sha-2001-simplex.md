# Using Simplicity to Control Complexity

`res:sha-2001-simplex` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/MS.2001.936213>

**By:** Lui Sha - **Year:** 2001 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** paywalled

The IEEE Software article that introduced the Simplex architecture: run a high-performance but unverified controller alongside a simple, verified high-assurance controller, and switch when a monitored safety envelope is about to be left.

## Key takeaways

- Reliability comes from a simple trusted core, not from diversity alone
- Origin of the 'simplex' term used in F3269 and RTA literature
- IEEE Software 18(4):20-28
