# Time-optimal Paths for a Dubins airplane

`res:chitsaz-lavalle-dubins-airplane-2007` - paper - <https://lavalle.pl/papers/ChiLav07b.pdf>

**By:** Hamidreza Chitsaz, Steven M. LaValle - **Year:** 2007 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Extends the Dubins car with altitude and bounded climb rate and characterises time-optimal paths with the Pontryagin maximum principle. The base model for most fixed-wing geometric planners.

## Key takeaways

- Dubins car + altitude = Dubins airplane
- Low, medium and high altitude-change cases need different path shapes
- No wind in the base model; see EDuPlane for wind
