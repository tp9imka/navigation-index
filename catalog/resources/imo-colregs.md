# COLREGs - Convention on the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea, 1972

`res:imo-colregs` - standard - <https://www.imo.org/en/About/Conventions/Pages/COLREG.aspx>

**By:** International Maritime Organization - **Year:** 1972 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

IMO page for the collision regulations that define give-way and stand-on duties, lights and signals; the rule set autonomous surface vessels must encode.

## Key takeaways

- Rules 13-17 (overtaking, head-on, crossing) drive most USV avoidance logic
- Rules are written for human judgement, which makes formalization hard
