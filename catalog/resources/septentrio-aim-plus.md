# Septentrio AIM+ GNSS jamming and spoofing protection

`res:septentrio-aim-plus` - doc - <https://www.septentrio.com/aim>

**By:** Septentrio - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Vendor explanation of AIM+ interference monitoring and mitigation: narrowband and wideband (chirp) jamming suppression in the receiver's signal processing plus layered spoofing detection including Galileo OSNMA.

## Key takeaways

- Single-antenna, in-receiver mitigation rather than CRPA
- Tiered feature levels
- OSNMA is one of the anti-spoofing layers
