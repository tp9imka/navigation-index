# OpenSky: A Modular and Open-Source Simulator of Sky Polarization Measurements

`res:opensky-sky-polarization-simulator-2024` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/TIM.2024.3374965>

**By:** Antoine Moutenet, Leo Poughon, Bruno Toulon, Julien R. Serres, Stephane Viollet - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

Open simulator of what a polarimetric camera sees of the sky (intensity, angle and degree of polarization), validated against real captures, for developing polarization compasses.

## Key takeaways

- Lets you test sky-polarization heading without field data
