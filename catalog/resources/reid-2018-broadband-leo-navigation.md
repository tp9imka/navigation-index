# Broadband LEO Constellations for Navigation

`res:reid-2018-broadband-leo-navigation` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1002/navi.234>

**By:** Tyler Reid, Andrew M. Neish, Todd Walter, Per Enge - **Year:** 2018 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** paywalled

NAVIGATION 65(2) paper analysing how broadband LEO mega-constellations could provide navigation: stronger signals against jamming, much better satellite geometry from sheer numbers, which lets the signal-in-space range error be relaxed, and a benign radiation environment that allows COTS payloads.

## Key takeaways

- Early quantitative case for LEO PNT
- Geometry gain offsets worse clocks and orbits
