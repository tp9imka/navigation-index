# Honeywell Vision Aided Navigation (Alternative Navigation Suite)

`res:honeywell-vision-aided-navigation` - product - <https://aerospace.honeywell.com/us/en/products-and-services/products/navigation-and-sensors/navigation-systems/vision-aided-navigation>

**By:** Honeywell Aerospace - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Honeywell's VAN correlates live visible or IR imagery against onboard reference maps for an absolute fix, paired with its inertial units. It sits in an Alternative Navigation Suite with magnetic-anomaly and celestial modules.

## Key takeaways

- Vendor description: passive, sensor-agnostic image-to-map correlation
- Press claim: 'GPS-like' performance on Embraer E170 and AW139 tests; no numbers published
- Same problem as open UAV absolute visual localization benchmarks
