# Are State-of-the-art Visual Place Recognition Techniques any Good for Aerial Robotics?

`res:zaffar-2019-vpr-aerial-robotics` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.07967>

**By:** Mubariz Zaffar, Ahmad Khaliq, Shoaib Ehsan, Michael Milford, Kostas Alexis, Klaus McDonald-Maier - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Early benchmark of eight ground-robot VPR techniques on two aerial place-recognition datasets, comparing matching performance, processing power and memory, and laying out why aerial VPR differs (viewpoint change, payload-limited compute).

## Key takeaways

- Ground VPR does not transfer directly to aerial
- Compute and memory as first-class metrics
- Good framing for an onboard VPR budget
