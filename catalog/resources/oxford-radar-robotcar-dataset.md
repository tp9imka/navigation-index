# Oxford Radar RobotCar Dataset

`res:oxford-radar-robotcar-dataset` - dataset - <https://oxford-robotics-institute.github.io/radar-robotcar-dataset/>

**By:** Oxford Robotics Institute - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** free-registration

RobotCar route re-driven with a scanning FMCW radar plus lidars and cameras, for radar odometry and localization.

## Key takeaways

- Sensors: FMCW radar, lidar, cameras, GPS/INS
- Size class: large
