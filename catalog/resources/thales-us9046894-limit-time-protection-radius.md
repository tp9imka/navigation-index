# US 9,046,894 B2: Navigation aid method for determining the path of an aircraft

`res:thales-us9046894-limit-time-protection-radius` - doc - <https://patents.google.com/patent/US9046894B2/en>

**By:** Thales SA - **Year:** 2015 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Thales patent, priority FR 09 02066 (2009-04-28). Defines a 'limit time' at which the predicted protection radius reaches the alarm limit assuming satellite navigation is lost now, recomputed each epoch, and uses it to decide whether an approach can be completed and when to leave it. Read through a Google Patents page summary.

## Key takeaways

- Aviation already computes time until PL reaches AL
- Used for guidance decisions, not to schedule aiding fixes
