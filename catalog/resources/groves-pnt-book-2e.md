**By:** Paul D. Groves - **Year:** 2013 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

Standard reference on satellite, inertial and terrestrial radio navigation and their integration, including INS/GNSS integration architectures, dead reckoning, feature matching and integrity monitoring.

- Loose, tight and deep INS/GNSS coupling explained
- Covers fault detection and integrity
- Companion MATLAB simulation software for buyers
