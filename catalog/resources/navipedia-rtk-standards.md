# Navipedia: RTK Standards

`res:navipedia-rtk-standards` - doc - <https://gssc.esa.int/navipedia/index.php/RTK_Standards>

**By:** ESA Navipedia - **Year:** 2011 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Free encyclopedia overview of the RTCM correction message families and transport (NTRIP) used for real-time kinematic positioning. A good orientation before buying or parsing the RTCM standard.

## Key takeaways

- Explains the role of RTCM 2.x vs 3.x messages
- Covers NTRIP for internet transport
