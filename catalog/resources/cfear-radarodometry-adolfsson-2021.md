# CFEAR Radarodometry - Conservative Filtering for Efficient and Accurate Radar Odometry

`res:cfear-radarodometry-adolfsson-2021` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.01457>

**By:** Daniel Adolfsson, Martin Magnusson, Anas Alhashimi, Achim J. Lilienthal, Henrik Andreasson - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Learning-free spinning-radar odometry that keeps the strongest returns per azimuth, builds oriented surface points and registers against a keyframe history (IROS 2021).

## Key takeaways

- Simple filtering beats heavier radar pipelines
- Runs fast on a single CPU thread per the authors
