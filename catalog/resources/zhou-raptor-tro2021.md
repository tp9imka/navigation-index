# RAPTOR: Robust and Perception-aware Trajectory Replanning for Quadrotor Fast Flight

`res:zhou-raptor-tro2021` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.03465>

**By:** Boyu Zhou, Jie Pan, Fei Gao, Shaojie Shen - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Fast replanning that searches several topological paths, refines for risk so obstacles are seen earlier, and plans yaw to look into unknown space. Aggressive indoor and outdoor flights. IEEE T-RO 37(6):1992-2009.

## Key takeaways

- Perception aimed at mapping unknown space, not at VIO health
- Code lives in Fast-Planner (GPL-3.0)
