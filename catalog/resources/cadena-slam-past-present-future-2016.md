# Past, Present, and Future of Simultaneous Localization And Mapping: Towards the Robust-Perception Age

`res:cadena-slam-past-present-future-2016` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05830>

**By:** Cesar Cadena, Luca Carlone, Henry Carrillo, et al. - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Landmark survey that frames modern SLAM as factor-graph MAP estimation and discusses robustness, scalability, representations and open problems.

## Key takeaways

- Standard framing of front end vs back end
- Robustness to outliers and long-term operation remain the hard problems
