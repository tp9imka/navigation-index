# Challenges and implemented technologies used in autonomous drone racing

`res:moon-2019-adr-iros-challenges` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1007/s11370-018-00271-6>

**By:** Hyungpil Moon, Jose Martinez-Carranza, et al. - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** paywalled

Organisers and teams review the IROS 2016 and 2017 autonomous drone races: the indoor gate format and the localisation, gate detection and control methods teams used.

## Key takeaways

- Indoor gate course with onboard sensing
- Teams used modified ORB-SLAM, sensor fusion, CNN and stereo gate detection
- Localisation was the hard part
