# Acquisition, Doppler Tracking, and Positioning With Starlink LEO Satellites: First Results

`res:neinavaie-khalife-kassas-2022-starlink-first-results-taes` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/TAES.2021.3127488>

**By:** Mohammad Neinavaie, Joe Khalife, Zaher M. Kassas - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE TAES 58(3) letter with the first acquisition, Doppler tracking and positioning results from Starlink downlinks without knowing the signal structure: a generalized-likelihood-ratio acquisition test and a Kalman-filter Doppler tracker, with six satellites tracked.

## Key takeaways

- Blind acquisition of undisclosed Starlink signals
- Doppler-only positioning works with a handful of satellites
- Received the IEEE M. Barry Carlton Award
