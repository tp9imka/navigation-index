# ViNT: A Foundation Model for Visual Navigation

`res:vint-foundation-model-2023` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14846>

**By:** Shah et al., UC Berkeley - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Trains a transformer navigation policy across many robot datasets and shows it adapts to new robots and tasks with little data.

## Key takeaways

- Cross-embodiment pretraining works for navigation
- Image-goal policy plus topological graph
