# AirSim Drone Racing Lab

`res:madaan-2020-airsim-drone-racing-lab` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05654>

**By:** Ratnesh Madaan, et al. - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Describes the AirSim-based framework and the NeurIPS 2019 Game of Drones simulation competition with perception, planning/control and head-to-head tiers.

## Key takeaways

- Tiered tasks separate perception from planning and control
- 117 teams registered, 16 unique entries reached submission
