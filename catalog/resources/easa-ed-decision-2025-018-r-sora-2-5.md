# EASA ED Decision 2025/018/R: SORA 2.5 in the AMC and GM to Regulation (EU) 2019/947

`res:easa-ed-decision-2025-018-r-sora-2-5` - standard - <https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/agency-decisions/ed-decision-2025018r>

**By:** EASA - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Adopts the European version of JARUS SORA 2.5 as AMC1 to Article 11 of the EU drone regulation, the risk-assessment method for operations in the 'specific' category. This is how a civil UAS operation with a new nav function gets authorised in Europe without full type certification.

## Key takeaways

- SORA assigns ground and air risk classes, then a SAIL level that sets robustness of mitigations
- Containment requirements scale with the risk of the adjacent area (SORA method; this decision text not re-read here)
- Exact entry-into-force date not checked here
