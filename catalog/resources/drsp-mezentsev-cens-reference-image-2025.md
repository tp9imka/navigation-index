# An analysis of approaches to determining the type of reference image for correlation-extreme aircraft navigation systems

`res:drsp-mezentsev-cens-reference-image-2025` - paper - <https://harvester.nas.gov.ua/Record/drspiprikievua-article-335610/Details>

**By:** O. Mezentsev, V. Yuzefovych, O. Butochnov - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Ukrainian journal paper (Data Recording, Storage and Processing, IPRI NAS of Ukraine, 2025) on how to form reference images for correlation-extremal aircraft navigation. Argues that selective images of the Earth's surface are possible, and often preferable, as references instead of direct sighting images. Metadata from the NAS harvester record; full text not read.

## Key takeaways

- Reference-map design matters as much as the matcher
- Selective (feature-reduced) references proposed over raw imagery
- Same research line as the 2016 generalised CENS model by these authors
