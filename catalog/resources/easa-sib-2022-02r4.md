# EASA Safety Information Bulletin 2022-02R4: GNSS Outage and Alterations Leading to Communication / Navigation / Surveillance Degradation

`res:easa-sib-2022-02r4` - standard - <https://ad.easa.europa.eu/ad/2022-02R4>

**By:** European Union Aviation Safety Agency - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

EASA's running bulletin on GNSS jamming and spoofing affecting aviation since February 2022, with recommendations for authorities, ANSPs, operators and manufacturers. Revision 4 is the current issue.

## Key takeaways

- Spoofing is treated as the harder-to-detect, higher-risk threat
- Recommendations are informational, not mandatory
- Affected FIRs are tracked on the EASA website
