# Aerial Drone Competition 2026 Autonomous Flight Mission Quick Guide

`res:recf-adc-2026-autonomous-flight-guide` - competition - <https://recf.org/documents/2025/09/autonomous-flight-mission-2026-aerial-drone-competition.pdf/>

**By:** REC Foundation - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

One-page guide to the school-level autonomous flight match: a 3-minute scripted run through fixed field elements (arches, keyholes, tunnel, colour mats, panel) ending on one of three landing targets, with per-task points and resets allowed while the timer runs.

## Key takeaways

- 3 minute match, timer does not stop for resets
- No interaction with drone or computer after start except resets or e-stop
