# Absolute Positioning Using the Earth's Magnetic Anomaly Field

`res:canciani-raquet-2016-absolute-positioning-magnetic-anomaly` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1002/navi.138>

**By:** Aaron Canciani, John Raquet - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

NAVIGATION 63(2) journal paper: a particle-filter framework that aids an aircraft INS with optically pumped cesium magnetometer measurements compared against an anomaly map, while estimating the temporal variations of the field. Analyses altitude and magnetic-storm dependence with flight-test and observatory data. The article is a U.S. Government work, public domain in the USA; the related AFIT dissertation is freely available.

## Key takeaways

- Particle filter on scalar anomaly measurements
- Models temporal (diurnal/storm) field variation
- Public-domain US Government work
