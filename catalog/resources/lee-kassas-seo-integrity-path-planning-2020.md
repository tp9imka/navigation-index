# Integrity-Based Path Planning Strategy for Urban Autonomous Vehicular Navigation Using GPS and Cellular Signals

`res:lee-kassas-seo-integrity-path-planning-2020` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03761>

**By:** Halim Lee, Jiwon Seo, Zaher M. Kassas - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Ground-vehicle path planner whose cost includes the predicted horizontal protection level along each candidate path, with a constraint that HPL stays under the alert limit. GPS and LTE pseudoranges are predicted by ray tracing over 3D city models, with multiple-fault handling. ION GNSS+ 2020, doi 10.33012/2020.17589.

## Key takeaways

- Uses a protection level, not a covariance, as the planning margin
- Offline, ground vehicle, GNSS and cellular
