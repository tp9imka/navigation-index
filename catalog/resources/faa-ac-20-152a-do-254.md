# FAA AC 20-152A: Development Assurance for Airborne Electronic Hardware

`res:faa-ac-20-152a-do-254` - standard - <https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_20-152A.pdf>

**By:** FAA - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Recognises RTCA DO-254 / EUROCAE ED-80 (both 2000) for airborne electronic hardware and adds objectives on top of DO-254. Relevant when a nav function moves into an FPGA or a custom board, and when a complex COTS device such as a GPU SoC sits in the path.

## Key takeaways

- Issued 2022-10-07, cancels AC 20-152 (2005)
- Covers FPGAs, ASICs and circuit board assemblies contributing to DAL A-C functions
- Complex COTS devices (multicore SoCs, GPUs) need extra evidence; see FAA Order 8110.105B
- Our reading: a Jetson-class SoC in a DAL A/B path is a hard sell today
