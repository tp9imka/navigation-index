# RTCA DO-160G: Environmental Conditions and Test Procedures for Airborne Equipment

`res:rtca-do-160g` - standard - <https://www.rtca.org/?p=6411>

**By:** RTCA SC-135 - **Year:** 2010 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** paywalled

The environmental qualification test standard for airborne equipment: temperature, altitude, vibration, power input, RF susceptibility and emissions, lightning, ESD and more. The RTCA page also sells DO-357 (user guide) and notes a planned DO-160H revision.

## Key takeaways

- Qualifies the box, not the algorithm, but a companion computer in the nav path needs it
- RF emissions and susceptibility sections matter for any GNSS-adjacent hardware
- Paywalled; RTCA page lists Accuris as reseller
- DO-160H was planned for March 2026 per the RTCA page; publication not verified here
