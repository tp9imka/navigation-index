# Беспилотники научили видеть дорогу без GPS и ГЛОНАСС: «умную» навигацию создали в МФТИ

`res:www1-mipt-fasetka-visual-nav-2026` - blog - <https://www1.ru/news/2026/05/20/bespilotniki-naucili-videt-dorogu-bez-gps-i-glonass-umnuiu-navigaciiu-sozdali-v-mfti.html>

**By:** www1.ru - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'Drones taught to see the road without GPS and GLONASS: MIPT built smart navigation'. News on 'Фасетка' from MIPT's robotics R&D centre (NTI Foundation support): it matches temporally stable landmarks (roads, buildings, river channels, power-line pylons) to an onboard satellite map, using thermal and radar imagery for seasonal robustness, and tolerates camera tilt up to 45 degrees thanks to training on synthesised viewpoints. Claims about 6 m accuracy at 1000 m altitude in helicopter and drone tests.

## Key takeaways

- Pick landmarks that change little over time
- Off-nadir tolerance (45 degrees) via synthetic-view training is the stated novelty
- Accuracy is a press claim, no paper cited
