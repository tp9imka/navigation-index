# GNSS Spoofing and Detection

`res:psiaki-humphreys-2016-gnss-spoofing-detection` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/JPROC.2016.2526658>

**By:** Mark L. Psiaki, Todd E. Humphreys - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

Proceedings of the IEEE survey of spoofing attack types and detection/mitigation techniques, from navigation-data checks to signal distortion monitoring and multi-antenna methods.

## Key takeaways

- Taxonomy of defenses: cryptographic, signal-processing, geometric
- Multi-antenna angle-of-arrival checks are powerful
- Combining several weak tests beats any single test
