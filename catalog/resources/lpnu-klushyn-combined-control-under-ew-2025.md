# Комбіноване керування безпілотними літальними апаратами в умовах радіоелектронної боротьби

`res:lpnu-klushyn-combined-control-under-ew-2025` - paper - <https://ena.lpnu.ua/items/14c349c0-a42e-45e2-9e45-cbe78934c215/full>

**By:** Yu. S. Klushyn, Lviv Polytechnic - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

English: 'Combined UAV control under electronic warfare'. Lviv Polytechnic article proposing a fiber-optic outbound leg plus anti-jam radio return leg, EO/IR fusion, and GNSS-free navigation with visual-inertial and lidar odometry corrected by baro and magnetometer, switched by a deterministic state machine. Reports prototype results over a 40 km mission (author figures: drift under 0.7%, 96% success over 50 runs).

## Key takeaways

- Mixed fiber + radio links appear as an EW-resilience pattern in Ukrainian research
- Deterministic mode switching between nav sources is emphasised
- Numbers are self-reported prototype results
