# MIL-STD-2525D Joint Military Symbology

`res:mil-std-2525d` - standard - <https://www.jcs.mil/Portals/36/Documents/Doctrine/Other_Pubs/ms_2525d.pdf>

**By:** US Department of Defense - **Year:** 2014 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

US DoD standard defining military symbology (frames, icons, modifiers and the symbol identification code) for C2 displays. Revision E (2022) is current and sold through standards resellers; 2525D was published openly on jcs.mil.

## Key takeaways

- SIDC structure drives symbol rendering in milsymbol
- Know which revision your consumers expect (2525C vs D/E codes differ)
