# Work-in-Progress: Multi-Deadline DAG Scheduling Model for Autonomous Driving Systems

`res:yano-azumi-2025-multi-deadline-dag-autoware` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06780>

**By:** Atsushi Yano, Takuya Azumi - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

arXiv 2505.06780 preprint (related DOI 10.1109/RTSS62706.2024.00049). Splits Autoware's end-to-end timing constraint into local relative deadlines per sub-DAG and extends Global EDF to the model; evaluated on a synthetic Autoware-like workload. Abstract only read.

## Key takeaways

- Real-time scheduling of a ROS 2 perception and localization graph
- Deadlines are fixed timing budgets, not derived from navigation error
