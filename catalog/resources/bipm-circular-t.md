# BIPM Circular T

`res:bipm-circular-t` - dataset - <https://www.bipm.org/en/time-ftp/circular-t>

**By:** BIPM - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Monthly BIPM publication of UTC - UTC(k) for the national timing labs contributing to UTC, with uncertainties, plus related TAI data on the BIPM FTP server. The canonical public record of how well real national clock ensembles track UTC.

## Key takeaways

- Real clock-ensemble offsets to feed allantools or to sanity-check the scale of ensemble numbers
- 5-day sampling, published monthly: long-term behaviour only, not holdover over minutes
