# Barrier Certificate based Safe Control for LiDAR-based Systems under Sensor Faults and Attacks

`res:zhang-cheng-niu-clark-2022-lidar-barrier-faults` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.05944>

**By:** Hongchao Zhang, Shiyu Cheng, Luyao Niu, Andrew Clark - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Preprint (arXiv, no venue confirmed). GPS plus LiDAR system: fault-tolerant estimation rebuilds the LiDAR scan from each state estimate, excludes estimates that disagree with the scan, and removes possibly compromised scan sectors; control barrier certificates then keep the vehicle safe using the remaining estimates. Simulated UAV delivery in an urban environment.

## Key takeaways

- Same fault-bank-to-barrier idea applied to a UAV with a LiDAR in the loop
- Uses LiDAR consistency to exclude faulty position estimates, not a protection level
- Simulation only
