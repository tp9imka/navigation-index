# Extensions to the Dubins Airplane Method - Flight Performance Constraints in Uniform Wind

`res:schopferer-eduplane-2026` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08004>

**By:** Simon Schopferer - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Derives closed-form links between flight-performance limits and kinematic constraints, then builds iterative algorithms for 3D fixed-wing trajectories of trochoidal or circular turns and straight segments in uniform wind. Accepted to IEEE TAES per the repo README.

## Key takeaways

- Trochoids are the wind-correct analogue of Dubins arcs
- Climb at constant vertical speed or constant thrust are both handled
- Reference code is EDuPlane (Apache-2.0)
