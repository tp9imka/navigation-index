# Safety-Critical LiDAR-Inertial Odometry with On-Manifold Deterministic Protection Level

`res:zhu-2026-lio-deterministic-protection-level` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09383>

**By:** Yueqi Zhu, Yan Pan, Chufan Rui, Jiasheng Luo, Bo Zhou - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

LiDAR-inertial odometry built on an on-manifold ellipsoidal set-membership filter under an unknown-but-bounded noise assumption. The filter's feasible sets serve as deterministic online protection levels for robot navigation.

## Key takeaways

- Set-membership (bounded-error) alternative to probabilistic PLs
- Closed-form link between point-cloud noise and ICP uncertainty
- Online safety bound for LIO
