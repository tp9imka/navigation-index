# Clock-Shift-Aided Blind Doppler Matching in Passive SDR Monitoring Data for LEO Doppler Positioning

`res:park-kim-seo-2026-blind-doppler-matching` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02377>

**By:** Seunghyeon Park, Gaeun Kim, Jiwon Seo - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Associates unlabeled Doppler traces in passive SDR spectrum-monitoring scans (no raw IQ) with TLE-predicted Starlink/OneWeb satellites, using slope-consistent segments, a clock-offset search and multi-metric matching, then runs batch Doppler positioning on open UK monitoring data. Submitted to IEEE NAVICON 2026.

## Key takeaways

- Positioning from spectrum waterfalls, not IQ samples
- Receiver clock offset search is the key step
- Uses open monitoring data
