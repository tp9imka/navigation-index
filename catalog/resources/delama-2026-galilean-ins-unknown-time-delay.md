# Galilean State Estimation for Inertial Navigation Systems with Unknown Time Delay

`res:delama-2026-galilean-ins-unknown-time-delay` - paper - <https://roboticsproceedings.org/rss22/p130.html>

**By:** Giulio Delama, Martin Scheiber, Yixiao Ge, Tarek Hamel, Stephan Weiss, Robert Mahony - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

RSS XXII 2026. Equivariant filter using Galilean symmetry that estimates navigation states and an unknown GNSS measurement delay jointly. Validated on two fixed-wing UAVs and in simulation against an EKF with online delay estimation.

## Key takeaways

- Abstract: commercial receivers add unknown delays of about 50-300 ms
- Abstract: fixed-wing flights with 90 ms and 120 ms lags; simulations to 500 ms
- Abstract: EqF stays consistent, EKF becomes inconsistent as delay grows
- No code linked on the proceedings page
