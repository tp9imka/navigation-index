# OFDM-Based Positioning with Unknown Data Payloads: Bounds and Applications to LEO PNT

`res:graff-humphreys-2024-ofdm-positioning-unknown-payloads` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.19106>

**By:** Andrew M. Graff, Todd E. Humphreys - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Derives the Ziv-Zakai bound on time-of-arrival estimation for OFDM signals with both known pilots and unknown data, capturing low-SNR threshold effects that Cramer-Rao bounds miss. Evaluates maximum-likelihood and decision-directed estimators on simulated LEO non-terrestrial-network channels.

## Key takeaways

- Unknown payload symbols still carry ranging information
- ZZB vs CRB at low SNR
- Theory for opportunistic OFDM ranging
