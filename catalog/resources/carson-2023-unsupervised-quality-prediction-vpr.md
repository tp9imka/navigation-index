# Unsupervised Quality Prediction for Improved Single-Frame and Weighted Sequential Visual Place Recognition

`res:carson-2023-unsupervised-quality-prediction-vpr` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.01464>

**By:** Helen Carson, Jason J. Ford, Michael Milford - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

ICRA 2023 (doi 10.1109/ICRA48891.2023.10160679, per Crossref); arXiv 2307.01464 abstract read. Training-free prediction of how likely a VPR estimate is correct, used to weight sequence matching; real-time and VPR-technique-agnostic, tested on four datasets and three techniques.

## Key takeaways

- Training-free success prediction for VPR
- Improves precision at the high-precision operating point
