# EigenPlaces: Training Viewpoint Robust Models for Visual Place Recognition

`res:eigenplaces-2023` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10832>

**By:** Gabriele Berton, Gabriele Trivigno, Barbara Caputo, Carlo Masone - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

ICCV 2023 paper that clusters training data to show the network the same points of interest from different viewpoints, building viewpoint robustness into global descriptors without extra supervision. Trained on SF-XL.

## Key takeaways

- Viewpoint robustness, relevant to oblique aerial views
- No extra labels needed
- MIT-licensed code
