# UK quantum navigation flight trials (Infleqtion, BAE Systems, QinetiQ)

`res:uk-quantum-nav-flight-trial-2024` - program - <https://www.gov.uk/government/news/un-jammable-quantum-tech-takes-flight-to-boost-uks-resilience-against-hostile-actors>

**By:** UK Government, Infleqtion, BAE Systems, QinetiQ - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

In May 2024 an optical atomic clock and a cold-atom sensor flew on QinetiQ's RJ100 testbed from Boscombe Down, toward a quantum inertial navigation system concept. Linked to the UK National Quantum Strategy mission of quantum navigation on aircraft by 2030.

## Key takeaways

- Government claim: first publicly acknowledged in-flight tests of this kind
- Government funding stated as nearly GBP 8 million
