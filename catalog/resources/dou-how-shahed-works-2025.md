# Як модернізуються «Шахеди» і чи є там штучний інтелект. Розбір DOU

`res:dou-how-shahed-works-2025` - blog - <https://dou.ua/lenta/articles/how-shahed-works/>

**By:** Yaroslav Zhakhalov, DOU.ua - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'How Shaheds are being upgraded and whether there is AI inside. DOU breakdown'. Threat-side view of a long-range one-way UAV's navigation stack, useful for defenders: satellite navigation plus INS on pre-planned routes, 16-element CRPA antennas for jam/spoof resistance, and cellular modems used as trackers rather than as a navigation link.

## Key takeaways

- Upgraded long-range UAVs combine GNSS, INS and CRPA, which reduces the value of GNSS jamming as a defense
- Cellular modems are telemetry/tracking aids, not navigation
- Defensive conclusion in the article: interceptors outperform EW against these threats
