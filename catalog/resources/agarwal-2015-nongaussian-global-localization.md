# Motion Planning for Global Localization in Non-Gaussian Belief Spaces

`res:agarwal-2015-nongaussian-global-localization` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04634>

**By:** Saurav Agarwal, Amirhossein Tamjidi, Suman Chakravorty - **Year:** 2015 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Plans actions to disambiguate multimodal location beliefs. Physical ground-robot experiments establish close prior art for active location disambiguation; not a UAV implementation.
