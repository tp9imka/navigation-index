# Microchip SA65 Chip Scale Atomic Clock datasheet (DS00003478F)

`res:microchip-csac-sa65-datasheet` - doc - <https://components101.com/sites/default/files/2026-08/CSAC-SA65-Datasheet.pdf>

**By:** Microchip Technology - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Datasheet for the SA65 CSAC: ADEV 3e-10 at 1 s, typical aging below 9e-10 per month, +/-3e-10 frequency change over -40 to +80 C, under 120 mW, with 1PPS input for discipline to GNSS. Third-party mirror of the 9/25 revision.

## Key takeaways

- Low power atomic holdover for portable and battery systems
- 1PPS input to discipline it from a GNSS receiver
