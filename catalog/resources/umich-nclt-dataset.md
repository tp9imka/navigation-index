# The University of Michigan North Campus Long-Term Vision and LIDAR Dataset

`res:umich-nclt-dataset` - dataset - <http://robots.engin.umich.edu/nclt/>

**By:** University of Michigan - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Segway robot sessions over many months on one campus with lidar, omnidirectional camera, GPS, IMU and odometry.

## Key takeaways

- Sensors: Velodyne HDL-32E, Ladybug3, GPS/RTK, IMU
- Long-term change over seasons
