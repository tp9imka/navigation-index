# Multi-ranger deck

`res:bitcraze-multi-ranger-deck` - doc - <https://www.bitcraze.io/products/multi-ranger-deck/>

**By:** Bitcraze - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Crazyflie expansion deck with five VL53L1x ToF sensors (front, back, left, right, up) measuring up to about 4 m.

## Key takeaways

- Five-direction range scan, no forward camera needed
- Pair with the Flow deck for position hold
