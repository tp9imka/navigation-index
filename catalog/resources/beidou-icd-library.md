# BeiDou Navigation Satellite System Signal-In-Space ICDs (B1I, B3I, B1C, B2a, B2b)

`res:beidou-icd-library` - standard - <http://en.beidou.gov.cn/SYSTEMS/ICD/>

**By:** China Satellite Navigation Office - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Official English-language ICD library for BeiDou open service signals, including the BDS-3 B1C and B2a signals that are interoperable with GPS L1C/L5 and Galileo E1/E5a, plus PPP-B2b.

## Key takeaways

- BDS-3 B1C and B2a are designed for multi-GNSS interoperability
- PPP-B2b carries free regional precise corrections
- The BeiDou site is the official distribution channel
