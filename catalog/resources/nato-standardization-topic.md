# NATO Standardization (topic page)

`res:nato-standardization-topic` - doc - <https://www.nato.int/cps/en/natohq/topics_69269.htm>

**By:** NATO - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

NATO's public explanation of how STANAGs and Allied publications are produced and ratified, and how interoperability standards flow into national forces. Starting point for locating public STANAG/AP summaries.

## Key takeaways

- STANAGs record national agreement to implement an Allied publication
- Most full texts are restricted; summaries and some APs are public
