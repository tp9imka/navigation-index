# Kimera-Multi: Robust, Distributed, Dense Metric-Semantic SLAM for Multi-Robot Systems

`res:kimera-multi-tian-2021` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.14386>

**By:** Yulun Tian, Yun Chang, Fernando Herrera Arias, et al. - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Distributed multi-robot SLAM where robots exchange compact information peer to peer and jointly optimize with outlier-robust distributed pose-graph optimization.

## Key takeaways

- Peer-to-peer, no central server
- Robust to bad inter-robot loop closures
