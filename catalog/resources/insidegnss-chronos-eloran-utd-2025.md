# Chronos Technology Tests eLoran Timing Provision in the UK

`res:insidegnss-chronos-eloran-utd-2025` - blog - <https://insidegnss.com/chronos-technology-tests-eloran-timing-provision-in-the-uk/>

**By:** Inside GNSS - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Report on the ESA-funded UTC Time Distribution (UTD) project led by Chronos Technology: caesium clocks replaced at the Anthorn eLoran transmitter, and prototype receivers producing UTC-aligned 1PPS from eLoran alone in timing mode. Accuracy is described by the company as comparable to good GNSS timing receivers; no measurement report is linked.

## Key takeaways

- Current UK evidence that eLoran is being used as a timing (not positioning) backup
- Press account - treat the accuracy statement as a vendor claim
