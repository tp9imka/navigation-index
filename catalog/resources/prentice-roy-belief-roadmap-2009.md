# The Belief Roadmap: Efficient Planning in Belief Space by Factoring the Covariance

`res:prentice-roy-belief-roadmap-2009` - paper - <https://people.csail.mit.edu/prentice/papers/ijrr09-brm.pdf>

**By:** Samuel Prentice, Nicholas Roy - **Year:** 2009 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Belief-space version of the probabilistic roadmap. Factoring the covariance turns several predict and update steps into one linear transfer, so covariance along roadmap edges is cheap to predict. Shown with UWB beacon localization, choosing routes that keep position accurate. IJRR 28(11-12), doi 10.1177/0278364909341659.

## Key takeaways

- Plans routes that keep localization good, not just short
- Offline roadmap, linear-Gaussian assumption
