# В Україні представили візуальний навігаційний модуль OSCAR, що забезпечує стійкість дронів до РЕБ

`res:militarnyi-twist-robotics-oscar-visual-nav` - blog - <https://militarnyi.com/uk/news/v-ukrayini-predstavyly-vizualnyj-navigatsijnyj-modul-oscar-shho-zabezpechuye-stijkist-droniv-do-reb/>

**By:** Militarnyi - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'Ukraine unveils the OSCAR visual navigation module that makes drones resilient to EW'. Twist Robotics' OSCAR (Optical System of Coordinates with Automatic Relocalization) recognises terrain landmarks in camera frames, matches them to a map, computes coordinates and passes them to the autopilot. Vendor claims: up to 20 m accuracy, day and night, over 500,000 km flown in 24 months, development aided by an in-house combat simulator ('Obrii').

## Key takeaways

- Map-matching visual positioning is fielded as an autopilot position source, not as a research demo
- Vendor accuracy claim is ~20 m, which sets a realistic bar for hackathon VPS demos
- Simulation of combat conditions is part of the development loop
- Interfaces, weight and supported autopilots are not stated
