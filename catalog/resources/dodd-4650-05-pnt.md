# DoD Directive 4650.05: Positioning, Navigation, and Timing (PNT)

`res:dodd-4650-05-pnt` - standard - <https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/issuances/dodd/465005p.pdf>

**By:** US Department of Defense - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

DoD policy directive governing the department's PNT enterprise, including the PNT Oversight Council and responsibilities for assured PNT and navigation warfare. Change 3 (2023) updated the oversight structure.

## Key takeaways

- Establishes the DoD PNT Oversight Council
- Assigns responsibilities for PNT and navigation warfare
