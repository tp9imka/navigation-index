# VL53L1X datasheet: long distance ranging Time-of-Flight sensor (STMicroelectronics)

`res:st-vl53l1x-datasheet` - doc - <https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/vl53l1x.pdf>

**By:** STMicroelectronics - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Datasheet for the 940 nm laser ToF chip used five times on the Crazyflie Multi-ranger deck and once on the Flow deck v2. Gives the 27 deg full field of view, programmable region of interest, short, medium and long distance modes and maximum range by ambient light and target reflectance. Revision not checked; the range table was read through secondary reproductions, not the PDF text.

## Key takeaways

- 27 deg full cone per sensor, reducible with a smaller SPAD region of interest
- Long mode: about 3.6 m in the dark, about 0.73 m in strong ambient light
- Short mode about 1.35 m in any light, up to 50 Hz
