# Chance-Constrained Collision Avoidance for MAVs in Dynamic Environments

`res:zhu-alonso-mora-ccnmpc-mav-2019` - paper - <https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:4c2e8664-4eb7-45ff-9e7e-a57ae643a371>

**By:** Hai Zhu, Javier Alonso-Mora - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Chance-constrained nonlinear MPC for quadrotors that keeps collision probability with each obstacle under a threshold, with a tight bound that makes it real-time. Uncertainty from localization, sensing and disturbances; real flights with two quadrotors and two people. IEEE RA-L 4(2):776-783.

## Key takeaways

- Live, onboard version of uncertainty-aware clearance for quadrotors
- Strongest baseline for an uncertainty-inflated planner
- Code is MATLAB, GPL-3.0
