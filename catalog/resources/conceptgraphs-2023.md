# ConceptGraphs: Open-Vocabulary 3D Scene Graphs for Perception and Planning

`res:conceptgraphs-2023` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16650>

**By:** Gu et al. - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Builds object-centric 3D scene graphs with open-vocabulary labels from RGB-D and uses an LLM to plan over them.

## Key takeaways

- Compact object-level maps for LLM planning
- Uses 2D foundation models
