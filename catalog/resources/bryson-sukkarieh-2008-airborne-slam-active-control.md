# Observability Analysis and Active Control for Airborne SLAM

`res:bryson-sukkarieh-2008-airborne-slam-active-control` - paper - <http://www-personal.acfr.usyd.edu.au/m.bryson/publications/Bryson2008_TAES.pdf>

**By:** Mitch Bryson, Salah Sukkarieh - **Year:** 2008 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

IEEE TAES 44(1):261-280. Inertial SLAM on a UAV with entropy as the planning utility over finite-horizon trajectories, plus an observability analysis that lists which aircraft maneuvers excite otherwise unobservable modes such as heading. The 6-page author PDF was read.

## Key takeaways

- Aircraft maneuvers chosen for estimability, with a maneuver-to-observable-mode table
- Gaussian SLAM, no prior map, no competing position hypotheses
