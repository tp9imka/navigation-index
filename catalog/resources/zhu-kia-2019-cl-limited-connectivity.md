# Cooperative Localization under Limited Connectivity

`res:zhu-kia-2019-cl-limited-connectivity` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.13074>

**By:** Jianan Zhu, Solmaz S. Kia - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

IEEE T-RO 35(6) paper with two decentralized cooperative localization algorithms that do not maintain inter-agent correlations: one bounds the joint covariance to stay consistent, the other estimates the unknown cross-covariance by optimization. Only the two agents involved in a relative measurement communicate, with no network-wide connectivity requirement.

## Key takeaways

- Consistent CL without tracking correlations
- Pairwise communication only
- Demonstrated in simulation and a robot experiment
