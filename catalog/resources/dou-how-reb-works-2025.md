# «Інколи від РЕБ очікують більше, ніж вона може». Як радіоелектронна боротьба працює проти FPV і «Шахедів»

`res:dou-how-reb-works-2025` - blog - <https://dou.ua/lenta/articles/how-reb-works/>

**By:** Yaroslav Zhakhalov, DOU.ua - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'Sometimes more is expected from EW than it can deliver. How electronic warfare works against FPVs and Shaheds'. Explainer for engineers on defensive EW: control uplink, video downlink and GNSS as separate attack surfaces, why radio reconnaissance must precede jamming, why spoofing is weaker against drones with CRPA antennas and position-jump filters, and why fiber-optic and vision-autonomous drones bypass RF EW.

## Key takeaways

- Detection/RF reconnaissance comes before any effective jamming; protocols and frequencies shift often
- Plausibility filters on position jumps plus CRPA blunt GNSS spoofing
- Fiber-optic and onboard-vision drones remove the RF link entirely, so layered defense (EW + interceptors + fire) is the field answer
- Friendly-fire on own drones is a real cost of EW
