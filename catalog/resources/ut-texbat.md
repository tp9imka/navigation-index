# Texas Spoofing Test Battery (TEXBAT)

`res:ut-texbat` - dataset - <https://radionavlab.ae.utexas.edu/texbat/>

**By:** UT Austin Radionavigation Lab - **Year:** 2012 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Public set of recorded high-rate IQ files of GPS L1 spoofing scenarios (static and dynamic, time and position push) used as a standard benchmark for spoofing detection.

## Key takeaways

- De facto benchmark for signal-level spoofing detectors
- Recordings are large IQ files suitable for GNSS-SDR
- Terms: research use only, (c) University of Texas; users asked not to redistribute the data
