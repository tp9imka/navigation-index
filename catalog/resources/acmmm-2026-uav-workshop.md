# ACM MM 2026 Workshop on UAVs (UAVM 2026)

`res:acmmm-2026-uav-workshop` - competition - <https://www.zdzheng.xyz/ACMMM2026Workshop-UAV/>

**By:** Zhedong Zheng et al. - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Workshop and challenge site for the 4th UAVs in Multimedia workshop at ACM MM 2026. The challenge (29 Mar - 30 Jun 2026, on Codabench) uses the PairUAV dataset for last-meter relative pose alignment; workshop on 14 Nov 2026.

## Key takeaways

- Challenge shifts from retrieval to precise approach navigation
- Awards require open-source code
- PairUAV derives from University-1652, which is request-only for research use with no third-party sharing or profit
