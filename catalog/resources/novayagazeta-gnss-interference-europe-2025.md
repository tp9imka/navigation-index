# Неоднозначный сигнал

`res:novayagazeta-gnss-interference-europe-2025` - blog - <https://novayagazeta.eu/articles/2025/09/22/neodnoznachnyi-signal>

**By:** Nikolai Pershin, Novaya Gazeta Europe - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'An ambiguous signal'. Russian-language independent journalism on GNSS interference over Europe: cites an EU states' report to ICAO (about 123k affected flights in Jan-Apr 2025), Estonian, Lithuanian and Polish statistics, jamming vs spoofing mechanics, and trackers such as gpsjam.org and gpswise.aero.

## Key takeaways

- Interference has spilled far beyond the front line into civil aviation
- Spoofing shows up as position jumps of hundreds of km and inconsistent instrument readings
- Public trackers are the main open data sources cited
