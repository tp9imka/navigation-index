# UIUC Propeller Data Site

`res:uiuc-propeller-database` - dataset - <https://m-selig.ae.illinois.edu/props/propDB.html>

**By:** Michael Selig and UIUC Applied Aerodynamics Group - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Wind-tunnel measurements of thrust and power coefficients vs advance ratio for many small UAV propellers, plus geometry.

## Key takeaways

- Real propeller curves instead of guessed efficiency
- Enough to build a thrust / power map for an energy-aware planner
- Check the site's terms before redistributing data in a product
