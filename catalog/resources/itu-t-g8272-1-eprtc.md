# ITU-T G.8272.1: Timing characteristics of enhanced primary reference time clocks

`res:itu-t-g8272-1-eprtc` - standard - <https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.8272.1>

**By:** ITU-T - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Defines the enhanced PRTC: GNSS plus an autonomous primary reference (in practice cesium or equivalent) to hold time through long GNSS outages. Secondary summaries describe about 30 ns to UTC when locked and around 100 ns over multi-week holdover; read the Recommendation for normative values.

## Key takeaways

- Holdover is specified as a bound over days, not seconds
- The 2024 edition adds parametric holdover
