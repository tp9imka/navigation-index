# Real-Time Obstacle Avoidance for Manipulators and Mobile Robots

`res:khatib-potential-fields-ijrr-1986` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1177/027836498600500106>

**By:** Oussama Khatib - **Year:** 1986 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** paywalled

Peer-reviewed (IJRR). The artificial potential field method: an attractive field toward the goal and repulsive fields around obstacles, with the robot following the negative gradient.

## Key takeaways

- Cheap, reactive, a few lines of code
- Local minima and oscillation in narrow gaps are built in
- Good as a baseline or a last-resort safety layer, not a planner
