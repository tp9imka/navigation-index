# 14 Lectures on Visual SLAM: From Theory to Practice

`res:gaoxiang-14-lectures-visual-slam` - book - <https://github.com/gaoxiang12/slambook-en>

**By:** Xiang Gao, Tao Zhang, Qinrui Yan, Yi Liu - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

English translation of the popular Chinese visual SLAM textbook, from rigid-body motion and Lie algebra through camera models, nonlinear optimization, feature/direct VO, backend optimization, loop closure and dense mapping, each with C++ examples.

## Key takeaways

- Code-heavy: every chapter compiles
- Good bridge from theory to ORB-SLAM-style systems
