# AlphaPilot: Autonomous Drone Racing

`res:alphapilot-uzh-paper` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12813>

**By:** Philipp Foehn, Davide Scaramuzza, et al. - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

UZH's second-place AlphaPilot system: learned gate detection, nonlinear filtering of visual-inertial state and time-optimal trajectory planning for GPS-free racing.

## Key takeaways

- Vision-inertial state estimation, not GNSS, drove the race
- Splitting perception and planning made it robust
