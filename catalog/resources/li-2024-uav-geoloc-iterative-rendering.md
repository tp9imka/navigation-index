# Unsupervised Multi-view UAV Image Geo-localization via Iterative Rendering

`res:li-2024-uav-geoloc-iterative-rendering` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14816>

**By:** Haoyuan Li, Chang Xu, Wen Yang, Li Mi, Huai Yu, Haijian Zhang - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Preprint. Builds a 3D Gaussian splatting model from oblique UAV views and iteratively renders a satellite-like view to align with the real satellite image.

## Key takeaways

- Render-and-compare route to cross-view matching; GPU-heavy
