# ROSCon UK 2025 - State Estimation for Mobile Robotics Workshop

`res:nav2-roscon-uk-2025-state-estimation-workshop` - masterclass - <https://docs.nav2.org/rolling/tutorials/general_tutorials/roscon_uk_2025_state_estimation/>

**By:** Locus Robotics, Open Navigation - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Hands-on workshop material comparing two ROS 2 state-estimation approaches: Kalman filtering with robot_localization (EKF/UKF, covariance tuning, frames, rate handling) and factor-graph fixed-lag smoothing with fuse. Exercises and solutions are in a public repo.

## Key takeaways

- Filter vs smoother trade-offs with runnable exercises
- Directly reusable configs for IMU/odom/GPS fusion
