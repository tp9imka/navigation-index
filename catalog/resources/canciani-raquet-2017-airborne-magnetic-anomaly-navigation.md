# Airborne Magnetic Anomaly Navigation

`res:canciani-raquet-2017-airborne-magnetic-anomaly-navigation` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/TAES.2017.2649238>

**By:** Aaron Canciani, John Raquet - **Year:** 2017 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE TAES 53(1) paper describing a passive airborne navigation system (scalar magnetometer, INS, barometer, anomaly map) with a particle filter and a full measurement equation linking magnetometer readings to the map. Flight-tested over Louisa, Virginia; the authors call the reported accuracy a best case under ideal conditions.

## Key takeaways

- First flight-test validation of the AFIT MagNav filter
- Complete measurement equation
- Best-case result, survey aircraft conditions
