# Conformalized Kalman Filters for State Estimation with Trustworthy Confidence Regions

`res:weisman-2026-conformalized-kalman-filters` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27506>

**By:** Olga Weisman, Nir Shlezinger, Bracha Laufer-Goldshtein - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Preprint. Conformal calibration of Kalman-type filter confidence regions built from the filter's own first and second moments, including a quantile-regression variant and a Gaussian-mixture variant for multimodal regions. Gives sample-wise and trajectory-wise coverage statements.
