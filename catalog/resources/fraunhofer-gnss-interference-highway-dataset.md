# GNSS Interference Spectrum Highway Dataset 1

`res:fraunhofer-gnss-interference-highway-dataset` - dataset - <https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/gnss-interference-spectrum-highway-dataset-1>

**By:** Felix Ott, Lucas Heublein, Nisha Lakshmana Raichur, Alexander Ruegamer - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** free-registration

20 ms raw IQ snapshots (E1 and E6 bands, 62.5 MHz) from a sensor on a bridge over a German highway, labelled into 8 interference and 3 non-interference classes, in .npy format with train/test and few-shot splits.

## Key takeaways

- Real-world interference recorded at a highway
- Non-commercial licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
- Companion dataset 2 has fewer classes (2 interference, 1 non-interference)
