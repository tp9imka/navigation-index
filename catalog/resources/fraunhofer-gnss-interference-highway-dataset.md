# GNSS Interference Spectrum Highway Dataset 1

`res:fraunhofer-gnss-interference-highway-dataset` - dataset - <https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/gnss-interference-spectrum-highway-dataset-1>

**By:** Fraunhofer IIS - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** free-registration

Short raw IQ snapshots recorded at a German highway monitoring station, labelled into interference and non-interference classes, in .npy format with train/test splits.
