# Derivation and Extensions of the Tolles-Lawson Model for Aeromagnetic Compensation

`res:gnadt-2022-tolles-lawson-derivation` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09899>

**By:** Albert R. Gnadt, Allan B. Wollaber, Aaron P. Nielsen - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Short open reference note that re-derives the Tolles-Lawson aircraft magnetic field model (permanent, induced and eddy-current terms), states where the assumptions enter, describes the calibration procedure, and adds a second-order correction and a way to build the vector aircraft field.

## Key takeaways

- Clear derivation of the standard compensation model
- Explains the calibration-box flight
- Pairs with the MagNav challenge data
