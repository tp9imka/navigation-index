# Deep Visual Geo-localization Benchmark

`res:deep-visual-geolocalization-benchmark-2022` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.03444>

**By:** Gabriele Berton, Riccardo Mereu, Gabriele Trivigno, Carlo Masone, Gabriela Csurka, Torsten Sattler, Barbara Caputo - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

CVPR 2022 oral presenting an open framework to build, train and test visual geo-localization pipelines with swappable backbones, aggregation and negative mining, plus a large experimental study of how each choice affects recall, runtime and memory.

## Key takeaways

- Systematic guide to VPR design choices
- Common evaluation protocol
- MIT-licensed framework
