# QZSS Performance Standards and Interface Specifications (IS-QZSS)

`res:qzss-is-qzss` - standard - <https://qzss.go.jp/en/technical/ps-is-qzss/ps-is-qzss.html>

**By:** Cabinet Office, Government of Japan - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Japan's QZSS interface specifications covering GPS-compatible signals, the L6 CLAS centimetre-level augmentation and MADOCA PPP services.

## Key takeaways

- QZSS signals are GPS-interoperable and improve urban-canyon availability in Asia-Oceania
- CLAS and MADOCA give free precise positioning corrections
- Specs are published openly by the Cabinet Office
