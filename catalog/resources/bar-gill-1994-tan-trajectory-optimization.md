# Improvement of terrain-aided navigation via trajectory optimization

`res:bar-gill-1994-tan-trajectory-optimization` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/87.338654>

**By:** A. Bar-Gill, P. Ben-Ezra, I. Y. Bar-Itzhack - **Year:** 1994 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE Transactions on Control Systems Technology 2(4):336-342 (conference version at CDC 1993). Maps terrain information with an entropy/FFT analysis, runs A* for minimum terrain-aided-navigation error trajectories, then superimposes airframe maneuver limits afterwards for a flyable but suboptimal route. Abstract only read.

## Key takeaways

- Earliest aircraft source found that plans the route for TAN accuracy
- Airframe limits are applied a posteriori, not inside the search
- Unimodal error measure: no competing position hypotheses
