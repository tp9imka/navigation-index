# Team CERBERUS Wins the DARPA Subterranean Challenge: Technical Overview and Lessons Learned

`res:cerberus-subt-final-paper` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04914>

**By:** Team CERBERUS (NTNU, ETH Zurich, UNR, Oxford, et al.) - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Winning team's description of its legged-plus-aerial system: multi-modal perception, robust localization and mapping, exploration planning and artifact detection, with lessons from the final.

## Key takeaways

- Robust multi-sensor odometry and exploration were key
- Field-hardening and operator workflow mattered as much as algorithms
