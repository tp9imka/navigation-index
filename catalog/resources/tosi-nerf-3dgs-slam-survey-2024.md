# How NeRFs and 3D Gaussian Splatting are Reshaping SLAM: a Survey

`res:tosi-nerf-3dgs-slam-survey-2024` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13255>

**By:** Fabio Tosi, Youmin Zhang, Ziren Gong, et al. - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Survey of SLAM methods that use neural radiance fields or 3D Gaussian splatting as the map representation, organized by sensor and technique.

## Key takeaways

- Radiance-field maps trade compute for photorealism
- Most methods are GPU-bound and not yet embedded-ready
