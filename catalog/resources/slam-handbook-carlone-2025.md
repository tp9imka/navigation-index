# SLAM Handbook: From Localization and Mapping to Spatial Intelligence

`res:slam-handbook-carlone-2025` - book - <https://github.com/SLAM-Handbook-contributors/slam-handbook-public-release>

**By:** Luca Carlone, Ayoung Kim, Timothy Barfoot, Daniel Cremers, Frank Dellaert - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Edited community handbook covering SLAM foundations (factor graphs, state representations), sensor-specific pipelines (visual, LiDAR, inertial, leg odometry) and spatial AI topics such as learned SLAM and NeRF/Gaussian-splatting maps. Released openly in parts before the Cambridge University Press edition.

## Key takeaways

- Single up-to-date reference spanning classic estimation and learned methods
- Free chapter PDFs on GitHub
