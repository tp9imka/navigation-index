# Detecting and Mitigating Attacks on GPS Devices

`res:burbank-2024-gps-attacks-review` - paper - <https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/24/17/5529>

**By:** J. Burbank, T. Greene, N. Kaabouch - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Open-access review of GPS attack detection and mitigation focused on the GPS-denied case, including the alternative sensors and methods used to keep navigating.

## Key takeaways

- Covers both detection and what to fall back on
- Does not cover timing-only alternatives
