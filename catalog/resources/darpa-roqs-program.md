# DARPA Robust Quantum Sensors (RoQS) - Phase 1

`res:darpa-roqs-program` - program - <https://www.darpa.mil/news/2025/roqs-launches-first-phase>

**By:** DARPA MTO - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

RoQS (solicitation DARPA-PS-25-11) aims to make quantum sensors robust to platform vibration and motion so they work on real vehicles; Phase 1 launched August 2025 with compact sensors to be tested on a government helicopter plus platform integration studies.

## Key takeaways

- Problem statement: quantum sensors lose their advantage on moving platforms
- Signals where defense money is going for GNSS-independent inertial/magnetic sensing
