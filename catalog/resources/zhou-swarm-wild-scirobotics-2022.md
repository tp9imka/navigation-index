# Swarm of micro flying robots in the wild

`res:zhou-swarm-wild-scirobotics-2022` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1126/scirobotics.abm5954>

**By:** Xin Zhou, Xiangyong Wen, Zhepei Wang, Yuman Gao, Haojia Li, Qianhao Wang, Tiankai Yang, Haojian Lu, Yanjun Cao, Chao Xu, Fei Gao - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

Peer-reviewed (Science Robotics). The EGO-Planner-v2 paper: palm-sized drones fly through forest with onboard depth, a gradient-based local planner on a MINCO trajectory representation, and swarm coordination.

## Key takeaways

- ESDF-free gradient planning works on tiny compute
- Depth camera + VIO is enough for forest-scale clutter
- Same planner scales from one drone to a swarm
