# KITTI-360

`res:kitti-360-dataset` - dataset - <https://www.cvlibs.net/datasets/kitti-360/>

**By:** KIT, University of Tuebingen - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** free-registration

Successor to KITTI with longer suburban drives, 360-degree perception sensors and dense annotations, with GPS/IMU poses.

## Key takeaways

- Sensors: perspective and fisheye cameras, lidar, GPS/IMU
- Size class: very large
