# Unveiling Starlink LEO Satellite OFDM-Like Signal Structure Enabling Precise Positioning

`res:neinavaie-kassas-2024-starlink-ofdm-structure` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/TAES.2023.3265951>

**By:** Mohammad Neinavaie, Zaher M. Kassas - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE TAES 60(2) letter that estimates the structure of Starlink's OFDM-like reference signals and frame length, and proposes a blind receiver that acquires multiple satellites, estimates their reference signals and Doppler, and tracks carrier and code phase. Of six tracked satellites, three sent pure tones and three OFDM-like signals.

## Key takeaways

- Blind reference-signal estimation
- Carrier and code phase from Starlink
- Bridges pure-tone Doppler to OFDM ranging
