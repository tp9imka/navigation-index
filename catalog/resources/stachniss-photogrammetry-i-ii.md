# Photogrammetry I & II

`res:stachniss-photogrammetry-i-ii` - lecture-series - <https://www.ipb.uni-bonn.de/photogrammetry-i-ii/>

**By:** Cyrill Stachniss, University of Bonn - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Recorded two-semester photogrammetry course (2015/16 playlist): camera models, direct linear transform, projective geometry, epipolar geometry, bundle adjustment, and image-based 3D reconstruction. The geometric backbone for visual navigation and map-matching against imagery.

## Key takeaways

- Camera calibration and orientation from first principles
- Bundle adjustment as used in VIO and visual geolocalization
- Useful before attempting aerial image-to-map registration
