# Ant-Mutated Immune Particle Filter Design for Terrain Referenced Navigation with Interferometric Radar Altimeter

`res:kang-yu-2021-ira-trn-particle-filter` - paper - <https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/13/11/2189>

**By:** Suktae Kang, Myeong-Jong Yu - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Open-access Remote Sensing paper proposing a particle filter variant (ant-colony mutation inside an immune auxiliary particle filter) to cope with low-grade interferometric radar altimeter errors in TRN. Evaluated by Monte Carlo TRN simulation on a UAV.

## Key takeaways

- Targets low-cost IRA measurement errors
- Particle diversity management for TRN
- Simulation-only evaluation
