# Google Smartphone Decimeter Challenge 2023-2024

`res:google-gsdc-2023` - competition - <https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/smartphone-decimeter-2023>

**By:** Google, ION - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** free-registration

Kaggle competition with raw GNSS and IMU logs from Android phones in cars, scored on positioning accuracy against high-grade truth.

## Key takeaways

- Large public smartphone GNSS dataset with truth
- Winning solutions used factor graphs and carrier phase
- Data also on IEEE DataPort under CC BY; Kaggle competition rules not verified
- Sensors: phone raw GNSS, IMU
- Size class: medium
- Kaggle account required
