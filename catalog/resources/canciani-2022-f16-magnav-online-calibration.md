# Magnetic Navigation on an F-16 Aircraft Using Online Calibration

`res:canciani-2022-f16-magnav-online-calibration` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/TAES.2021.3101567>

**By:** Aaron Canciani - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE TAES 58(1) paper on magnetic anomaly navigation flown on an F-16, where the platform's magnetic interference is far larger than on survey aircraft and drifts. A combined batch and online calibration gives better accuracy than batch-only calibration on 1.5-hour flights.

## Key takeaways

- Operational fighter platform, not a quiet survey aircraft
- Online calibration needed for drifting platform fields
- Key evidence for MagNav on military aircraft
