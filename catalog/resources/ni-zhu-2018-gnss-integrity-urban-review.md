# GNSS Position Integrity in Urban Environments: A Review of Literature

`res:ni-zhu-2018-gnss-integrity-urban-review` - paper - <https://hal.science/hal-01709519>

**By:** Ni Zhu, Juliette Marais, David Betaille, Marion Berbineau - **Year:** 2018 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

IEEE T-ITS 19(9) review of GNSS integrity monitoring for urban transport, contrasting it with aviation integrity (multipath, NLOS, different risk definitions) and listing open research issues. Open copy on HAL.

## Key takeaways

- Why aviation RAIM does not carry over directly to urban ground vehicles
- Survey of urban FDE and protection-level methods
- Open research problems list
