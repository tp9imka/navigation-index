# Robust Safety under Stochastic Uncertainty with Discrete-Time Control Barrier Functions

`res:cosner-stochastic-dtcbf-rss2023` - paper - <https://roboticsproceedings.org/rss19/p084.html>

**By:** Ryan K. Cosner, Preston Culbertson, Andrew J. Taylor, Aaron D. Ames - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Finite-time safety bounds, from martingale theory, for discrete-time CBF controllers under unbounded stochastic disturbances such as estimation noise. RSS 2023.

## Key takeaways

- Gives a probability of staying safe over a horizon, not a hard guarantee
- Theory for the noisy-estimate case
