# FAA SAFO 24002: Recognizing and Mitigating GPS/GNSS Disruptions

`res:faa-safo-24002-gnss-disruptions` - doc - <https://nbaa.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/SAFO24002.pdf>

**By:** US Federal Aviation Administration - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

FAA Safety Alert for Operators describing jamming vs spoofing effects on aircraft systems, typical locations near conflict and counter-UAS areas, and recommended crew and operator actions.

## Key takeaways

- Hybrid GPS/inertial solutions can also be corrupted by spoofing
- Revert to conventional procedures and report to ATC
- Disruptions cluster around conflict zones and C-UAS protection areas
