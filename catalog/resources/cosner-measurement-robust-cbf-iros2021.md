# Measurement-Robust Control Barrier Functions: Certainty in Safety with Uncertainty in State

`res:cosner-measurement-robust-cbf-iros2021` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.14030>

**By:** Ryan K. Cosner, Andrew W. Singletary, Andrew J. Taylor, Tamas G. Molnar, Katherine L. Bouman, Aaron D. Ames - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Combines measurement-robust CBFs with the backup-set method to keep safety under imperfect state estimates. Simulation and a Segway with onboard stereo-camera state estimation. IROS 2021, doi 10.1109/IROS51168.2021.9636584.
