# VectorNav - Iridium STL-aided INS collaboration with NAL Research

`res:vectornav-stl-aided-ins` - company - <https://www.vectornav.com/about/news>

**By:** VectorNav Technologies - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

VectorNav makes small tactical-grade IMU/GNSS-INS units widely used on research drones (PX4 has a driver). In April 2025 it announced work with NAL Research on Iridium STL-aided INS for GNSS-denied operation.

## Key takeaways

- Collaboration announcement, not a shipped product feature
- VN-series units are a common drop-in for PX4 testbeds
