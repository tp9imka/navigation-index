# В поисках автономного полета

`res:vcru-zimkov-autonomous-flight-2025` - blog - <https://vc.ru/2649708-v-poiskah-avtonomnogo-poleta>

**By:** A. Zimkov (vc.ru) - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'In search of autonomous flight' (15 Dec 2025). Practitioner comparison of GNSS, ultrasonic indoor positioning (Geoscan Pioneer 'Locus' as example) and optical flow plus laser rangefinder for agricultural, warehouse and indoor drones. The author picks optical flow + rangefinder on ArduPilot, mentions Holybro H-Flow and MTF-01 modules, and reports an OpenMV camera + TFMini Plus build that held autonomous hover up to 10 m.

## Key takeaways

- Optical flow + rangefinder judged the most universal low-cost option
- Ultrasonic systems are sensitive to beacon calibration (1 cm offsets matter)
- Civil use cases only
