# Дроны: Автономные беспилотные воздушные системы

`res:lektorium-geoscan-autonomous-drones-course` - course - <https://www.lektorium.tv/drones>

**By:** M. Alekseev (Geoscan), M. Bakustina, Lektorium, Lyceum No. 239 - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** free-registration

English: 'Drones: autonomous unmanned aerial systems'. Free self-paced Russian online course built on Geoscan competition experience: drone basics, simple programming including indoor and outdoor navigation systems, companion-computer interaction (Python, Raspberry Pi, autopilot protocol, mapping), computer vision with marker recognition and vision-based navigation, and swarm control with ROS. Lesson videos (e.g. ultrasonic local navigation, vision system) were published on YouTube in Dec 2022.

## Key takeaways

- Beginner path from flight basics to vision-based navigation and swarms
- Uses the Geoscan Pioneer ecosystem
- Free, including certificate
