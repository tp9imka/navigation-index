# Навигация без GPS, которая уже работает

`res:habr-nav-without-gps-overview-2025` - blog - <https://habr.com/ru/companies/ruvds/articles/900284/>

**By:** alizar, RUVDS - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'Navigation without GPS that already works'. Overview of deployed or demonstrated non-GNSS methods: accelerometer-based subway positioning, a star-tracker drone prototype (Raspberry Pi 5, night only, ~4 km accuracy), quantum-magnetometer map matching (AQNav), quantum accelerometers, and barometric tunnel detection.

## Key takeaways

- Broad menu of alternative PNT methods with honest limits (night-only, km-level)
- Magnetic anomaly navigation is presented as the most mature quantum-sensing use
