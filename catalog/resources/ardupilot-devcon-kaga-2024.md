Three days of recorded ArduPilot developer talks; navigation-relevant sessions include 'High Altitude Non-GPS' (Sanket Sharma), 'CINS (DCM replacement)' (Pieter Van Goor, ANU), indoor flight and position offsets. Day 2 stream: youtube.com/live/jbRQSE0r9L8.

## Key takeaways

- Day 2 stream contains the non-GPS talk
- Shows current ArduPilot estimator work
- Primary-source talk on high-altitude GPS-free flight
- New CINS attitude/position estimator explained
