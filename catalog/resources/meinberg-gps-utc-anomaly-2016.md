# Global GPS time anomaly on Tue, Jan 26th (2016)

`res:meinberg-gps-utc-anomaly-2016` - report - <https://www.meinbergglobal.com/english/news/global-gps-time-anomaly-on-tue-jan-26th.htm>

**By:** Meinberg - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Vendor note on the January 2016 event in which GPS receivers worldwide, including all of Meinberg's own, showed time jumps of about 13 microseconds; later analyses traced it to faulty UTC correction parameters uploaded to some satellites.

## Key takeaways

- A non-adversarial GNSS time fault hit many receivers at once
- Argues for holdover and cross-checks even without an attacker
