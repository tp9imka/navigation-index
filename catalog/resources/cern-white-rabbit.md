# White Rabbit (CERN project page)

`res:cern-white-rabbit` - doc - <https://white-rabbit.web.cern.ch/>

**By:** CERN - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Project page for White Rabbit, the CERN-led extension of PTP and synchronous Ethernet that distributes sub-nanosecond time over fibre; links to specs, hardware and users.

## Key takeaways

- Sub-ns time distribution inside a facility without GNSS at each node
- Open hardware and HDL on the Open Hardware Repository
