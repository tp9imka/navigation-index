# Visual Language Maps for Robot Navigation

`res:vlmaps-2022` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05714>

**By:** Huang et al. - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Fuses visual-language features into 3D maps so robots can localize landmarks named in open-vocabulary language and navigate to them.

## Key takeaways

- Language-indexed spatial maps
- Enables spatial instructions like 'between X and Y'
