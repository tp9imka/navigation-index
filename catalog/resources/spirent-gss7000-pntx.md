# Spirent GSS7000 and PNT X GNSS/PNT simulators

`res:spirent-gss7000-pntx` - product - <https://spirent.com/products/gss6300>

**By:** Spirent - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Spirent's lab simulators generate multi-GNSS RF with controllable interference and spoofing (meaconing, trajectory spoofing, nav data spoofing) for hardware-in-the-loop receiver testing; PNT X extends this to combined PNT/sensor simulation.

## Key takeaways

- Lab-grade, export-controlled tooling; judges from industry will know it
- Open alternatives are SDR-based signal simulators - legal only into a cable or shielded enclosure
