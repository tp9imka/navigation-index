# UASTHN: Uncertainty-Aware Deep Homography Estimation for UAV Satellite-Thermal Geo-localization

**By:** Jiuhong Xiao, Giuseppe Loianno et al. - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Adds uncertainty estimation to satellite-thermal homography so a navigation filter can weight or reject visual fixes.
