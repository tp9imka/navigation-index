# MILUV: A Multi-UAV Indoor Localization dataset with UWB and Vision

`res:miluv-dataset-2025` - dataset - <https://doi.org/10.25452/figshare.plus.28386041.v1>

**By:** Mohammed Ayman Shalaby, Syed Shabbir Ahmed, Nicholas Dahdah, Charles Champagne Cossette, Jerome Le Ny, James Richard Forbes - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Multi-UAV indoor dataset (three quadcopters, 36 experiments) with UWB ranging including raw timestamps, channel impulse response and inter-robot ranges, stereo and downward cameras, IMU, laser height, magnetometer and motion-capture ground truth, in LOS and NLOS. Data on Figshare+ under CC BY 4.0; the devkit (decargroup/miluv) is MIT. Paper: arXiv 2504.14376.

## Key takeaways

- CC BY 4.0 data, MIT devkit
- Inter-robot UWB for cooperative localization tests
- CIR data for NLOS classification
