# State Estimation and Localization for Self-Driving Cars (Coursera)

`res:coursera-state-estimation-localization-sdc` - course - <https://www.coursera.org/learn/state-estimation-localization-self-driving-cars>

**By:** University of Toronto, Steven Waslander, Jonathan Kelly - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** free-registration

Course 2 of the UofT Self-Driving Cars specialization: least squares, KF/EKF/ES-EKF/UKF, IMU and GNSS sensor models, LiDAR scan matching with ICP, culminating in an error-state EKF that fuses IMU, GNSS and LiDAR on CARLA data.

## Key takeaways

- Final project is a ready template for GNSS/IMU/LiDAR fusion
- Explicit error-state EKF derivation
- Free to audit
