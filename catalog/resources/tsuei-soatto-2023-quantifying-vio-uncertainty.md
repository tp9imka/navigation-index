# Quantifying VIO Uncertainty

`res:tsuei-soatto-2023-quantifying-vio-uncertainty` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16386>

**By:** Stephanie Tsuei, Stefano Soatto - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Preprint. Monte Carlo study of the XIVO monocular EKF VIO under feature-track noise, drift and attribution (association) errors and IMU noise and drift. Attribution errors hurt most, and even small noise gives a more than 1 in 100 chance of a run that looks like the high-noise case.

## Key takeaways

- Wrong feature associations are the dominant VIO error source
- Constant-velocity flight is not exciting enough for good observability
- Single benchmark runs understate tail risk
