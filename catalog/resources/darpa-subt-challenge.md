# DARPA Subterranean (SubT) Challenge

`res:darpa-subt-challenge` - competition - <https://www.darpa.mil/research/challenges/subterranean>

**By:** DARPA - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

DARPA's 2018-2021 challenge for autonomous robot teams mapping and searching tunnels, urban underground and caves with no GNSS and degraded comms; systems and virtual tracks.

## Key takeaways

- Scoring rewarded accurate localization of reported artifacts, not just coverage
- Heterogeneous teams (legged + aerial) won
- Communication-denied autonomy was decisive
