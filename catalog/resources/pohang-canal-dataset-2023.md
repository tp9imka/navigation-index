# Pohang Canal Dataset: A Multimodal Maritime Dataset for Autonomous Navigation in Restricted Waters

`res:pohang-canal-dataset-2023` - dataset - <https://sites.google.com/view/pohang-canal-dataset>

**By:** Chung, Kim, Lee, Kim (KAIST) - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Multimodal maritime dataset from a 7.5 km route through a narrow canal, port areas and near-coast waters, with LiDARs, marine radar, stereo, infrared and omnidirectional cameras, AHRS and RTK GPS. Also on the AWS Open Data registry.

## Key takeaways

- Includes marine radar and LiDAR together - good for radar odometry at sea
- RTK ground truth and SLAM baseline trajectory included
- Non-commercial licence
