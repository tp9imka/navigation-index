# Regulation (EU) 2021/821 - Dual-use export control regime

`res:eu-dual-use-regulation-2021-821` - standard - <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/821/oj>

**By:** European Union - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

The EU's recast dual-use export control regulation covering items, software and technology including navigation and avionics categories, with public-domain and basic-research de-control notes.

## Key takeaways

- Category 7 covers navigation and avionics items
- Public-domain and basic scientific research notes protect open publication
