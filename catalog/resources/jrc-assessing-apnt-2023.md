# Assessing Alternative Positioning, Navigation and Timing Technologies for Potential Deployment in the EU

`res:jrc-assessing-apnt-2023` - report - <https://rntfnd.org/wp-content/uploads/EU-Assessing-APNT-March-2023.pdf>

**By:** European Commission Joint Research Centre - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

JRC test campaign at Ispra of commercial alternative PNT systems (terrestrial beacons, LEO, eLoran-like, fibre timing and others) for DG DEFIS. One of the few side-by-side public evaluations of A-PNT products.

## Key takeaways

- Mature commercial A-PNT already exists for positioning or timing
- Recommends a system-of-systems approach
- Warns the diverse tests do not allow a fair head-to-head ranking
