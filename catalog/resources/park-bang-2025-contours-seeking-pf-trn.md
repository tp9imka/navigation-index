# Contours-Seeking Proposal Density Particle Filter and Resilient Terrain-Referenced Navigation

`res:park-bang-2025-contours-seeking-pf-trn` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15489>

**By:** Junwoo Park, Hyochoong Bang - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Author's accepted version of an IEEE TAES 61(6) (Dec 2025) paper. Proposes a particle-filter proposal that nudges particles along terrain slopes toward the most probable terrain contours (Gaussian mixture random forcing plus auxiliary sampling) to fight degeneracy under multimodal likelihoods and unknown prediction bias.

## Key takeaways

- Addresses PF degeneracy, the classic TRN failure mode
- Robust to unknown INS bias and multimodal noise
- Simulation study
