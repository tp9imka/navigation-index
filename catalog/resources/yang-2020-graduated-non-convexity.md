# Graduated Non-Convexity for Robust Spatial Perception: From Non-Minimal Solvers to Global Outlier Rejection

`res:yang-2020-graduated-non-convexity` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.08605>

**By:** Heng Yang, Pasquale Antonante, Vasileios Tzoumas, Luca Carlone - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

IEEE RA-L 5(2), 2020. Graduated non-convexity wraps a non-minimal solver to reject outliers without an initial guess; implemented in GTSAM as GncOptimizer.

## Key takeaways

- Abstract: robust to 70-80% outliers in the tested problems
- No global optimality guarantee, but strong empirical robustness
- Available off the shelf in GTSAM
