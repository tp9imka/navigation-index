English: 'MTF02 optical flow sensor'. August 2025 Habr review of the MicoAir MTF02 module, which combines an optical flow camera with a ToF rangefinder and talks MAVLink to ArduPilot and PX4, for position hold and motion sensing without GPS.

## Key takeaways

- Cheap off-the-shelf flow + range module for GNSS-free hover
- Integrates as a MAVLink sensor in ArduPilot/PX4
