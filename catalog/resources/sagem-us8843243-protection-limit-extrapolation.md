# US 8,843,243 B2: Method and system for determining protection limits with integrated extrapolation over a given time horizon

`res:sagem-us8843243-protection-limit-extrapolation` - doc - <https://patents.google.com/patent/US8843243B2/en>

**By:** Sagem Defense Securite - **Year:** 2014 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Sagem (now Safran) patent, priority FR0956070 (2009-09-07). Extrapolates the Kalman error model to a future moment with a constant transition matrix built from maximum or mission dynamics, then computes protection limits at that moment. No alert-limit crossing time and no aiding scheduling. Read through a Google Patents page summary.

## Key takeaways

- Forward prediction of protection limits over a horizon
- Independent of past carrier dynamics by construction
