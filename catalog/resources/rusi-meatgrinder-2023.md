# Meatgrinder: Russian Tactics in the Second Year of Its Invasion of Ukraine

`res:rusi-meatgrinder-2023` - report - <https://www.rusi.org/explore-our-research/publications/special-resources/meatgrinder-russian-tactics-second-year-its-invasion-ukraine>

**By:** Jack Watling, Nick Reynolds, J. Watling, N. Reynolds (RUSI) - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

RUSI special report on Russian battlefield adaptation, including the density of Russian electronic warfare and its heavy effect on Ukrainian UAVs, with a widely cited estimate of around 10,000 drone losses per month.

## Key takeaways

- Dense EW coverage along the front is weighted toward defeating UAVs
- Loss figures came from interviews and are contested
- Explains why EW-resilient navigation became a top Ukrainian requirement
- Describes dense, layered Russian EW deployment
- Drone attrition to EW drives demand for non-GNSS navigation
