# GPS Spoofing WorkGroup 2024 - Final Report

`res:opsgroup-gps-spoofing-final-report-2024` - report - <https://ops.group/blog/gps-spoofing-final-report/>

**By:** OPSGROUP - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Crowd-sourced industry report from a six-week OPSGROUP workgroup of pilots, controllers, regulators and manufacturers on GPS spoofing hitting civil flights, mainly near Middle East and Black Sea / Baltic conflict areas. Comes with crew guidance and a technical guide.

## Key takeaways

- Spoofing degrades terrain-warning (EGPWS) functions, which may stay wrong after GPS returns
- No quick fix: mitigation is crew awareness, procedures and keeping ground navaids
- Strong evidence that integrity monitoring and 'is my position plausible?' checks matter more than raw accuracy
