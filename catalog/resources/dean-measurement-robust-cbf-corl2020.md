# Guaranteeing Safety of Learned Perception Modules via Measurement-Robust Control Barrier Functions

`res:dean-measurement-robust-cbf-corl2020` - paper - <https://proceedings.mlr.press/v155/dean21a.html>

**By:** Sarah Dean, Andrew J. Taylor, Ryan K. Cosner, Benjamin Recht, Aaron D. Ames - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Measurement-robust CBFs choose safe inputs when the state comes from an uncertain, possibly learned, measurement model, and give a way to estimate how much perception error the controller tolerates. Simulated Segway. CoRL 2020, PMLR 155.

## Key takeaways

- Safety filter that accounts for state-estimate error
- Not tested on a quadrotor
