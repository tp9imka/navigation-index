# rtklibexplorer blog

`res:rtklibexplorer-blog` - blog - <https://rtklibexplorer.wordpress.com/>

**By:** Tim Everett - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Long-running practitioner blog on getting centimetre-level RTK/PPK from low-cost u-blox receivers with the demo5 RTKLIB fork, with configuration walk-throughs and data analysis.

## Key takeaways

- Configuration recipes that save hours
- Honest comparisons of receivers and settings
- Practical RTK tuning tips you will not find in papers
- Maintainer of the demo5 RTKLIB fork
