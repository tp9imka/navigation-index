# How to fool a GPS (TEDxAustin)

`res:ted-humphreys-how-to-fool-a-gps` - talk - <https://www.ted.com/talks/todd_humphreys_how_to_fool_a_gps>

**By:** Todd Humphreys - **Year:** 2012 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Accessible TEDx talk from the head of UT Austin's Radionavigation Lab on GPS jamming and spoofing and why civil GPS is trivially counterfeitable, given around the time of his lab's drone and yacht spoofing demonstrations.

## Key takeaways

- Why unauthenticated civil GNSS is a systemic risk
- Good opener for a resilient-PNT pitch
