# Error characteristics of a model-based integration approach for fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles

`res:mwenegoha-2021-vdm-error-characteristics-fixed-wing` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1017/S0373463321000424>

**By:** Hery A. Mwenegoha, Terry Moore, James Pinchin, Mark Jabbal - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

The Journal of Navigation 74(6). An EKF with the VDM as process model, driven by autopilot control inputs and fed with GNSS and IMU; the state vector holds navigation, IMU error, wind error and VDM parameter states. Monte Carlo simulation over a trajectory with high- and low-dynamics segments and a variable wind profile, with GNSS outages of 10 to 90 s followed by re-acquisition.

## Key takeaways

- Wind error states and VDM parameter states are estimated jointly with navigation states
- Simulation only; the trajectory deliberately mixes high- and low-dynamics segments
