# NextVision HackLab 2026: «Виявити. Супроводити. Перемогти.»

`res:nure-nextvision-hacklab-2026` - competition - <https://nure.ua/wp-content/uploads/2026/3chelenzh-nextvision-hacklab-2026_ok.pdf>

**By:** Kharkiv National University of Radio Electronics (NURE) - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'NextVision HackLab 2026: Detect. Track. Win.' Flyer for a student hackathon on drone detection held 22-24 April 2026 at NURE in Kharkiv. Teams of three build prototypes that detect and track small, fast objects including UAVs using optical, thermal, acoustic, radar or hybrid sensing, with requirements on range, day/night and all-weather operation, and trajectory tracking. Judged on accuracy with few false alarms, noise and interference robustness, novelty and practical value.

## Key takeaways

- Defensive C-UAS detection and tracking, not interception
- Explicit criteria: low false-alarm rate and robustness to noise
- Deliverable can be a working prototype or a conceptual model with simulation or video
- Useful template for scoping a 48h C-UAS detection entry
