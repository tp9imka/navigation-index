# Visual Odometry: Part I - The First 30 Years and Fundamentals / Part II - Matching, Robustness, Optimization, and Applications

`res:scaramuzza-vo-tutorial` - paper - <https://rpg.ifi.uzh.ch/docs/VO_Part_I_Scaramuzza.pdf>

**By:** Davide Scaramuzza, Friedrich Fraundorfer - **Year:** 2011 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Two-part IEEE Robotics & Automation Magazine tutorial (Part II at rpg.ifi.uzh.ch/docs/VO_Part_II_Scaramuzza.pdf). Explains the VO problem formulation, 2D-2D/3D-2D motion estimation, feature matching, outlier rejection and local optimization in a compact, readable form.

## Key takeaways

- Best single-read primer on VO design choices
- Part II covers RANSAC and windowed bundle adjustment
