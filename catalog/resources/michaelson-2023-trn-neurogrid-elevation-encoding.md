# Terrain-Relative Navigation with Neuro-Inspired Elevation Encoding

`res:michaelson-2023-trn-neurogrid-elevation-encoding` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/PLANS53410.2023.10139925>

**By:** Kristen Michaelson, Felix W. Wang, Renato Zanetti - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE/ION PLANS 2023. Generates position measurements from terrain elevation with NeuroGrid, a brain-inspired encoding, and fuses them with an INS using a measurement rejection rule and online covariance, motivated by the cost of storing and searching large elevation maps.
