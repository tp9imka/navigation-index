# Способи навігації БПЛА у складних умовах зовнішнього середовища

`res:kpi-hehelskyi-avrutov-uav-nav-complex-env-2025` - paper - <https://mgsys.kpi.ua/article/view/334103>

**By:** O. Hehelskyi, V. Avrutov (Igor Sikorsky KPI) - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'UAV navigation methods in complex environmental conditions' (Mechanics of Gyroscopic Systems, No. 49, 2025). Reviews GNSS/IMU integration, lidar navigation, geomagnetic navigation and computer-vision methods (SLAM, visual odometry) for GPS-denied flight, listing each one's weaknesses: jamming and spoofing, lidar processing load, magnetometer calibration, and visual dependence on light and texture. Concludes that a condition-specific multi-sensor combination is needed.

## Key takeaways

- KPI gyroscopic-systems department perspective: inertial core plus aiding sources
- Geomagnetic navigation is included alongside vision and lidar
- No single technology is universal; pick sensors per operating conditions
