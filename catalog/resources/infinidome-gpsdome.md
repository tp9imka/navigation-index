# infiniDome GPSdome / GPSdome 2 small-UAS anti-jam module

`res:infinidome-gpsdome` - product - <https://www.gpsworld.com/infinidome-releases-gpsdome-2-dual-band-anti-jamming-technology/>

**By:** infiniDome - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

infiniDome sells retrofit anti-jam modules that sit between antenna elements and a commercial GNSS receiver on small drones and vehicles; GPSdome 2 is dual-band with up to four elements for nulling several jammer directions.

## Key takeaways

- Vendor claim: CRPA-like protection without CRPA cost or export restrictions
- Weight figures differ across press releases (500 g in the linked GPS World piece, 350 g elsewhere)
