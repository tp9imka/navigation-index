# Optimal Stochastic Vehicle Path Planning Using Covariance Steering

`res:okamoto-tsiotras-covariance-steering-2019` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.03380>

**By:** Kazuhide Okamoto, Panagiotis Tsiotras - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Steers both mean and covariance of a linear stochastic system to a goal while obstacles are handled as non-convex chance constraints, using mixed-integer convex programming over a convex decomposition of free space. IEEE RA-L 2019.

## Key takeaways

- Closed-loop covariance gives less conservative paths than open-loop growth
- Linear model, offline
