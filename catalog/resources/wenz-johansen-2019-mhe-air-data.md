# Moving Horizon Estimation of Air Data Parameters for UAVs

`res:wenz-johansen-2019-mhe-air-data` - paper - <https://hdl.handle.net/11250/2630200>

**By:** Andreas Wenz, Tor Arne Johansen - **Year:** 2019 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems (NTNU Open record; abstract-level read). Estimates wind, angle of attack and lift coefficients from IMU, GNSS and pitot data with a moving horizon estimator instead of an EKF, validated on two UAVs. The group's related ICUAS papers state that attitude changes are needed for persistence of excitation at initialisation and from time to time in flight.

- Excitation requirement for wind and lift-coefficient estimation is stated, not gated automatically (abstract level)
