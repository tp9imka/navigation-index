# Tackling the Kidnapped Robot Problem via Sparse Feasible Hypothesis Sampling and Reliable Batched Multi-Stage Inference

`res:zhang-2026-kidnapped-robot-sparse-hypotheses` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01219>

**By:** Muhua Zhang, Lei Ma, Ying Wu, Kai Shen, Deqing Huang, Henry Leung - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement 2026 (doi 10.1109/TIM.2026.3694741, per arXiv); abstract read. Passive 2D global relocalization from one LiDAR scan against an occupancy grid while the robot stays still: RRT-sampled feasible position hypotheses, coarse ranking and early termination, and a translation-tolerant scan-to-map metric for the final pick. Ground robot with a non-panoramic LiDAR.

## Key takeaways

- Multi-hypothesis scan-to-map relocalization while stationary is current ground-robot practice
- Picks the best hypothesis from one scan; no stated refusal to commit on near-ties
