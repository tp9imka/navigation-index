# EUROCAE WG-114 / SAE G-34: ED-324 / ARP6983 (process standard for ML in aeronautical products)

`res:eurocae-wg-114-ed-324-arp6983` - doc - <https://www.eurocae.net/working-group/wg-114>

**By:** EUROCAE WG-114, SAE G-34 - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Working-group page for the joint EUROCAE/SAE standard on developing and certifying aeronautical products that implement machine learning. As of 2026-11-09 it is a draft, with a target publication date of 31 Dec 2026 on the WG page.

## Key takeaways

- Not published yet; drafts circulated to regulators (Draft 7 cited at an FAA exchange in Aug 2025)
- Initial scope: offline supervised learning
- Expected to be the ML analogue of DO-178C once released
