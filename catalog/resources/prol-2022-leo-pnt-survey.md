# Position, Navigation, and Timing (PNT) Through Low Earth Orbit (LEO) Satellites: A Survey on Current Status, Challenges, and Opportunities

`res:prol-2022-leo-pnt-survey` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2022.3194050>

**By:** Fabricio S. Prol, Ruben Morales Ferre, Zainab Saleem, Petri Valisuo, Christina Pinell, Elena Simona Lohan, Mahmoud Elsanhoury, Mohammed Elmusrati, Saiful Islam, Kaan Celikbilek, Kannan Selvan, Jaakko Yliaho, Kelly Rutledge, Arto Ojala, Luca Ferranti, Jaan Praks, M. Zahidul H. Bhuiyan, Sanna Kaasalainen, Heidi Kuusniemi - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Open-access IEEE Access survey of LEO-PNT: system design steps and challenges, candidate physical-layer parameters, design tools and simulators, satellite-ground channel models, and commercial prospects, written by a multidisciplinary Finnish-led team.

## Key takeaways

- Broad map of the LEO-PNT design space
- Lists simulators and channel models
- Good starting reading for a LEO-PNT hackathon track
