# NeBula: Quest for Robotic Autonomy in Challenging Environments; TEAM CoSTAR at the DARPA Subterranean Challenge

`res:nebula-costar-subt-paper` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11470>

**By:** Team CoSTAR (NASA JPL et al.) - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Describes the NeBula belief-space autonomy stack: multi-modal positioning, mapping, traversability, risk-aware mission planning and multi-robot reasoning; CoSTAR won the Urban circuit.

## Key takeaways

- Reasoning over uncertainty in localization improves mission decisions
- Redundant odometry sources were essential underground
