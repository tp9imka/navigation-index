# GNSS Data Processing, Vol. I: Fundamentals and Algorithms (ESA TM-23)

`res:esa-gnss-data-processing-vol1` - book - <https://gssc.esa.int/navipedia/GNSS_Book/ESA_GNSS-Book_TM-23_Vol_I.pdf>

**By:** J. Sanz Subirana, J.M. Juan Zornoza, M. Hernandez-Pajares (ESA) - **Year:** 2013 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Free ESA textbook covering GNSS measurement modelling, SPP, differential positioning and PPP algorithms with full equations. Pairs with the gLAB tool.

## Key takeaways

- Complete measurement model incl. clocks, troposphere, ionosphere
- SPP through PPP derivations
- Designed to accompany gLAB exercises
