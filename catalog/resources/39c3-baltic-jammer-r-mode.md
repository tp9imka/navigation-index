# Who cares about the Baltic Jammer? Terrestrial Navigation in the Baltic Sea Region (39C3)

`res:39c3-baltic-jammer-r-mode` - talk - <https://media.ccc.de/v/39c3-who-cares-about-the-baltic-jammer-terrestrial-navigation-in-the-baltic-sea-region>

**By:** Niklas Hehenkamp, DLR team - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

39th Chaos Communication Congress talk from DLR researchers on R-Mode, a terrestrial maritime navigation backup built on existing radio beacons and coastal infrastructure, tested in the heavily jammed Baltic. Covers real-world jamming impacts, transmitter/receiver build-out and the path from research to critical infrastructure.

## Key takeaways

- Concrete view of Baltic jamming/spoofing effects on ships, aircraft, timing
- Terrestrial backups as an alternative to space-based ones
