# Optimal Sensor Scheduling for Resource-Constrained Localization of Mobile Robot Formations

`res:mourikis-roumeliotis-2006-optimal-sensor-scheduling` - paper - <https://experts.umn.edu/en/publications/optimal-sensor-scheduling-for-resource-constrained-localization-o/>

**By:** Anastasios I. Mourikis, Stergios I. Roumeliotis - **Year:** 2006 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE Transactions on Robotics 22(5):917-931 (doi 10.1109/TRO.2006.878947). Robots in a formation share limited processing and bandwidth; the paper picks the measurement frequency of each relative and absolute sensor to minimise the trace of the weighted steady-state covariance under a cap on total EKF updates, as a convex SDP. Abstract only read.

## Key takeaways

- Closest robotics framing of compute-constrained localization: allocate update rates under a processing cap
- Steady-state frequencies, not event decisions
- Measurements assumed to always succeed; no processing delay
