# Visual place recognition for aerial imagery: A survey

`res:moskalenko-2024-aerial-vpr-survey` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00885>

**By:** Ivan Moskalenko, Anastasiia Kornilova, Gonzalo Ferrer - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Robotics and Autonomous Systems 183 (2025) survey and evaluation methodology for VPR on aerial imagery. Benchmarks several VPR methods and shows that the zoom level and overlap chosen when cutting satellite map tiles strongly affect results. Code in prime-slam/aero-vloc.

## Key takeaways

- How to tile satellite maps for VPR matters
- Hands-on benchmark with code
- Directly useful for drone-to-satellite geolocalization
