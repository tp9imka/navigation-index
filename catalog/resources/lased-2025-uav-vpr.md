# Visual Place Recognition for Large-Scale UAV Applications

`res:lased-2025-uav-vpr` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15089>

**By:** Ioannis Tsampikos Papapetros, Ioannis Kansizoglou, Antonios Gasteratos - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Introduces LASED, a large aerial VPR training set of about a million images from 170,000 locations across Estonia sampled over a decade, and uses steerable (rotation-equivariant) CNNs to handle the heading ambiguity of nadir UAV images.

## Key takeaways

- Multi-year aerial imagery for cross-season training
- Rotation-equivariant networks for unknown heading
- Aerial counterpart to GSV-Cities
