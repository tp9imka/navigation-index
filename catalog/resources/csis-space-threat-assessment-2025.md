# Space Threat Assessment 2025

`res:csis-space-threat-assessment-2025` - report - <https://www.csis.org/analysis/space-threat-assessment-2025>

**By:** Clayton Swope, Kari A. Bingen, Makena Young, Kendra LaFave, CSIS Aerospace Security Project - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Annual CSIS Aerospace Security Project review of counterspace capabilities, with sections on GPS jamming and spoofing in Eastern Europe and the Middle East.

## Key takeaways

- Puts terrestrial GNSS jamming in the wider counterspace picture
- Useful citation for problem framing in pitches
- Country-by-country counterspace inventory
- Tracks GNSS electronic attack incidents year over year
