# IMAV 2026 Competition Rules for Indoor and Outdoor Competitions

`res:imav-2026-rulebook` - competition - <https://2026.imavs.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Rulebook_IMAV2026_V3.pdf>

**By:** Renaud Kiefer, Thomas Pavot - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Rulebook for the International Micro Air Vehicle competition 2026: indoor net arena with an obstacle course, dark-room inspection, payload drop, turbine inspection and precision landing, with detailed scoring.

## Key takeaways

- Autonomy factor: 1.0 onboard, 0.7 off-board compute or beacons, 0.4 manual intervention
- Mass factor 1.13 (3 kg) to 2.0 (0.1 kg)
- Risk-graded points: smaller window and harder bar score more
- Landing bonus on a static or moving ArUco platform
