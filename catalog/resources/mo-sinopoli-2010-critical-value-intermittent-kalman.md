# Towards Finding the Critical Value for Kalman Filtering with Intermittent Observations

`res:mo-sinopoli-2010-critical-value-intermittent-kalman` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.2442>

**By:** Yilin Mo, Bruno Sinopoli - **Year:** 2010 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

arXiv 1005.2442 preprint. The abstract restates the Sinopoli et al. 2004 result: with Bernoulli observation arrival there is a critical arrival probability below which the Kalman filter diverges, with only bounds for general systems, and works towards its exact value. Abstract only read.

## Key takeaways

- Models fix success as random i.i.d. arrival
- Gives the condition under which too-rare fixes make the filter diverge
- Arrival is not chosen and not correlated in time
