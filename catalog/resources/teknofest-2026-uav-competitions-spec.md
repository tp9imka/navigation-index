# TEKNOFEST 2026 UAV Competitions Specification (EN)

`res:teknofest-2026-uav-competitions-spec` - competition - <https://cdn.teknofest.org/media/upload/userFormUpload/2026_%C4%B0HA_Yar%C4%B1%C5%9Fmalar%C4%B1_%C5%9Eartnamesi_EN_VBU_Ee7DO.pdf>

**By:** TEKNOFEST, TUBITAK - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Specification for the International and High School UAV competitions. Rotary-wing teams fly an autonomous figure-eight around two poles, then find two targets at unknown positions by image processing and drop payloads on them. An autonomy coefficient scales the score.

## Key takeaways

- Coefficient c: 1.0 autonomous take-off, flight and landing; 0.7 with manual take-off or landing
- Single camera on the auxiliary computer; image processing may run in the air or on the ground
- Drop distance measured from target centre up to 10 m
