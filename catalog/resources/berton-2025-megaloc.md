# MegaLoc: One Retrieval to Place Them All

`res:berton-2025-megaloc` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17237>

**By:** Gabriele Berton, Carlo Masone - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Preprint. A single image-retrieval model trained across many localization datasets, meant as a general retrieval front end for VPR and visual localization.

## Key takeaways

- MIT code; weight terms not checked separately
- Candidate retrieval stage to compare with AnyLoc and DINOv2 GeM
