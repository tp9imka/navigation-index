# NIST IR 8323r1: Foundational PNT Profile - Applying the Cybersecurity Framework for the Responsible Use of PNT Services

`res:nist-ir-8323r1-pnt-profile` - standard - <https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8323/r1/final>

**By:** NIST - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Maps the NIST Cybersecurity Framework functions (identify, protect, detect, respond, recover) onto systems that produce or consume PNT data. A checklist-style profile for building resilient PNT into an architecture.

## Key takeaways

- Revision 1 supersedes the 2021 original
- Organized around CSF functions, applicable to both GNSS and non-GNSS sources
- NIST has signalled alignment with CSF 2.0
