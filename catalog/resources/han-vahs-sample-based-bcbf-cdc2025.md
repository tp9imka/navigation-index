# Risk-Aware Robot Control in Dynamic Environments Using Belief Control Barrier Functions

`res:han-vahs-sample-based-bcbf-cdc2025` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04097>

**By:** Shaohang Han, Matti Vahs, Jana Tumova - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Extends belief CBFs from Gaussian EKF beliefs to sample-based beliefs, using concentration bounds on tail risk so multimodal or skewed beliefs are handled, with robustness to a bounded distribution shift. Simulated underwater tracking and collision avoidance at about 1 kHz per the abstract. CDC 2025 (Crossref doi 10.1109/CDC57313.2025.11312006). Abstract read only.
