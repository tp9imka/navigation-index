# IEC61850 SV and PTP traffic under GNSS jamming

`res:kit-iec61850-ptp-gnss-jamming-dataset` - dataset - <https://doi.org/10.35097/2en2xs4njx2ecbru>

**By:** Clemens Fruboese (KIT KASTEL) - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Jamming half of the KIT KASTEL captures: IEC 61850 Sampled Values and PTP traffic while the substation time server's GNSS is jammed, about 594 GB pcap tar on KIT RADAR, CC BY-NC 4.0, identifiers pseudonymised.

## Key takeaways

- Shows how a real grandmaster announces loss of GNSS and enters holdover on the wire
- Pair with the spoofing half to contrast detectable loss vs silent drift
