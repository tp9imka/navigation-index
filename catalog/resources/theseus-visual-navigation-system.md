# Theseus Visual Navigation System (YC launch)

`res:theseus-visual-navigation-system` - company - <https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Ln6-theseus-gps-denied-navigation-for-drones>

**By:** Theseus - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

US startup whose founders started at the February 2024 El Segundo Defense Tech Hackathon (Aviation Week coverage). Its VNS uses a camera, an IMU and pre-loaded satellite imagery to estimate position and feeds the drone a substitute GPS signal.

## Key takeaways

- Vendor claims: integration under 30 minutes; six months from hackathon to a SOCOM sale
- Direct precedent that a hackathon visual-nav demo can become a product - and that judges have seen this pitch
