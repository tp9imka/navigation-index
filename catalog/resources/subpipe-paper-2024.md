# SubPipe: A Submarine Pipeline Inspection Dataset for Segmentation and Visual-inertial Localization

`res:subpipe-paper-2024` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.17907>

**By:** Alvarez-Tunon, Marnet, Antal, Aubard, Costa, Brodskiy - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Describes an AUV dataset from a pipeline inspection mission with camera, side-scan sonar and navigation data, with VIO and segmentation baselines.

## Key takeaways

- Open underwater VIO benchmark with ground truth
- Side-scan sonar segmentation labels
