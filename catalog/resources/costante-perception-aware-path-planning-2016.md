# Perception-aware Path Planning

`res:costante-perception-aware-path-planning-2016` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04151>

**By:** Gabriele Costante, Christian Forster, Jeffrey Delmerico, Paolo Valigi, Davide Scaramuzza - **Year:** 2016 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Online planner that scores paths by the photometric information a direct visual localizer would get, and replans as textured areas are discovered. Quadrotor demonstration. The authors' group lists it as T-RO 2016; venue not confirmed here.

## Key takeaways

- Original statement of perception-aware planning for VIO
- Texture, not only geometry, decides a good path
