# Edge-based Monocular Thermal-Inertial Odometry in Visually Degraded Environments

`res:etio-thermal-inertial-odometry-2022` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10033>

**By:** Yu Wang, et al. - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Thermal-inertial odometry that tracks thermal image edges instead of corner features, targeting darkness and smoke where visible cameras fail.

## Key takeaways

- Thermal edges are more stable than corners in low-contrast IR
- Relevant for night and obscurant operations
