# Caltech Aerial RGB-Thermal Dataset in the Wild

`res:cart-caltech-aerial-rgbt-2024` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08997>

**By:** Connor Lee, Matthew Anderson, Nikhil Raganathan, Xingxing Zuo, Kevin Do, Georgia Gkioxari, Soon-Jo Chung - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Aerial RGB-thermal dataset over natural US terrain with synchronized GPS and IMU, with benchmarks for thermal segmentation, RGB-to-thermal translation and VIO.

## Key takeaways

- Thermal aerial data is expensive to collect - this fills a gap
- Includes VIO benchmark with GPS/IMU
- Dataset is for non-commercial use only (README: CC BY-SA 4.0 terms)
