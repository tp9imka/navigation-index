# On the Necessity of Real-Time Principles in GPU-Driven Autonomous Robots

`res:ali-2025-real-time-principles-gpu-robots` - paper - <https://robotics.cs.unc.edu/publications/Ali2025_ICRA.pdf>

**By:** Syed W. Ali, Angelos Angelopoulos, Denver Massey, Sarah Haddix, Alexander Georgiev, Joseph Goh, Rohan Wagle, Prakash Sarathy, James H. Anderson, Ron Alterovitz - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

ICRA 2025 (doi 10.1109/ICRA55743.2025.11128627). Shows on a simulated high-speed forest drone that interference from non-flight-critical GPU work slows reaction time and causes crashes, and applies the TimeWall component-based real-time framework to isolate obstacle detection and control. Abstract and introduction read.

## Key takeaways

- Evidence that the onboard GPU is a contended resource on a drone stack
- That contention is the condition under which fix scheduling pays off
