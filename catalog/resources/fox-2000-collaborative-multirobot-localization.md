# A Probabilistic Approach to Collaborative Multi-Robot Localization

`res:fox-2000-collaborative-multirobot-localization` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1008937911390>

**By:** Dieter Fox, Wolfram Burgard, Hannes Kruppa, Sebastian Thrun - **Year:** 2000 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** paywalled

Autonomous Robots 8(3) paper extending sample-based Markov (Monte Carlo) localization to robot teams: when one robot detects another, both robots' beliefs are updated. Real-robot and simulation experiments show faster and more accurate global localization than single-robot localization.

## Key takeaways

- Particle-filter view of cooperative localization
- Robot-to-robot detections synchronize beliefs
- Handles global localization, not only tracking
