# Reliable urban vehicle localization under faulty satellite navigation signals

`res:gupta-gao-2024-reliable-urban-localization-faulty-gnss` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1186/s13634-024-01150-2>

**By:** Shubh Gupta, Grace Gao - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

EURASIP J. Adv. Signal Process. 2024; earlier preprint arXiv 2101.06380. Particle filter with a Gaussian-mixture measurement likelihood that is robust to several faulty GNSS measurements, plus a test statistic from the particle distribution for integrity monitoring.

## Key takeaways

- Peer-reviewed; abstract only
- Urban GNSS, not terrain: the TRN version is the open angle
- Preprint reports fewer false alarms and lower integrity risk than an earlier PF integrity monitor
