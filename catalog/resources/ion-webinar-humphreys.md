# First results from three years of GNSS interference monitoring from low Earth orbit

`res:ion-webinar-humphreys` - talk - <https://www.ion.org/publications/webinar-humphreys.cfm>

**By:** Todd E. Humphreys, Lakshay Narula - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

UT Radionavigation Lab results from detecting and geolocating terrestrial GNSS jammers using receivers on LEO satellites, revealing persistent interference hotspots in conflict regions.

## Key takeaways

- Space-based RFI monitoring is practical
- Maps of jamming hotspots tied to conflict zones
