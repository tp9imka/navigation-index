# Разработана система автономной визуальной навигации роя дронов

`res:spbran-konovalov-swarm-visual-nav-2025` - blog - <https://spbran.ru/news/pdf/razrabotana-sistema-avtonomnoy-vizualnoy-navigacii-roya-dronov>

**By:** SPC RAS (St Petersburg FRC RAS) - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'A system for autonomous visual navigation of a drone swarm has been developed' (press release, 25 April 2025). SPC RAS describes a software prototype that navigates a UAV swarm from camera data under jamming or without satellite navigation, needing only ordinary satellite imagery (no height maps or geophysical fields) and no hardware beyond a video camera. Quotes postgraduate K. Konovalov.
