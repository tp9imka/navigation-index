# Honeywell HGuide o480 single-card INS and HGuide i700 IMU

`res:honeywell-hguide-o480` - product - <https://www.honeywellaerospace.com/us/en/about-us/press-release/2025/05/honeywell-new-single-card-resilient-navigation-system>

**By:** Honeywell Aerospace - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

HGuide o480 (May 2025) is a single-card INS for small UAVs and ground robots that accepts velocity aiding (e.g. Honeywell radar velocity sensor) and embeds a Septentrio receiver with AIM+ anti-jam/anti-spoof. The HGuide i700 IMU is marketed as near navigation grade with a no-license-required export classification.

## Key takeaways

- Shows the commodity pattern: tactical IMU + velocity aid + hardened GNSS in one card
- Export classification is a selling point, which matters for team hardware choices
