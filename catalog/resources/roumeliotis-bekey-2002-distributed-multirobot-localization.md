# Distributed multirobot localization

`res:roumeliotis-bekey-2002-distributed-multirobot-localization` - paper - <https://doi.org/10.1109/TRA.2002.803461>

**By:** Stergios I. Roumeliotis, George A. Bekey - **Year:** 2002 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

IEEE Transactions on Robotics and Automation 18(5) paper that decomposes the centralized cooperative-localization Kalman filter into one modified filter per robot ('collective localization'). Demonstrated on three robots and compared with a decentralized information filter. A foundational reference for exact (non-CI) distributed cooperative localization.

## Key takeaways

- Centralized-equivalent estimator split across robots
- Cross-correlations are handled exactly, at a communication cost
- Shows accuracy gain from relative robot-to-robot measurements
