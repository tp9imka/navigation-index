# Onboard Ranging-based Relative Localization and Stability for Lightweight Aerial Swarms

`res:li-onboard-relative-localization-swarms` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05853>

**By:** Shushuai Li et al. (TU Delft MAVLab) - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Presents onboard UWB ranging with an EKF for relative localization among small quadrotors, with a stability analysis of formation control built on those estimates.

## Key takeaways

- Relative localization needs no anchors
- Observability depends on relative motion between agents
