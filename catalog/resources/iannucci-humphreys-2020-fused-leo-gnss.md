# Fused Low-Earth-Orbit GNSS

`res:iannucci-humphreys-2020-fused-leo-gnss` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.12334>

**By:** Peter A. Iannucci, Todd E. Humphreys - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Accepted to IEEE TAES. Proposes 'fused LEO GNSS': a cooperative PNT service hosted on a broadband mega-constellation's own transmitters, clocks and spectrum. Assesses the opportunity cost to the operator in time-space-bandwidth product and energy, concluding that a near-instantaneous-fix service would cost a small fraction of Starlink downlink capacity.

## Key takeaways

- Cooperative (operator-supported) LEO PNT vs opportunistic use
- Opportunity-cost argument in downlink capacity
- Foundation for the UT group's LEO PNT work
