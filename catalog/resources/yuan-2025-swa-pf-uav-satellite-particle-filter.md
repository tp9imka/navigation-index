# SWA-PF: Semantic-Weighted Adaptive Particle Filter for Memory-Efficient 4-DoF UAV Localization in GNSS-Denied Environments

`res:yuan-2025-swa-pf-uav-satellite-particle-filter` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13795>

**By:** Jiayu Yuan, Ming Dai, Enhui Zheng, Chao Su, Nanxing Chen, Qiming Hu, Shibo Zhu, Yibin Cao - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

arXiv preprint. Particle filter over a 4-DoF UAV pose that weights particles by semantic agreement between the UAV image and a low-resolution satellite map; announces a multi-altitude dataset (MAFS) as 'will be available'. Abstract only read; multimodal posteriors are not discussed there.

## Key takeaways

- Passive estimator side: a possible hypothesis generator for active disambiguation
- Dataset release not confirmed
- Abstract: errors below 10 m and 10x efficiency over feature extraction methods
- Code repo has no license file; MAFS terms not checked
