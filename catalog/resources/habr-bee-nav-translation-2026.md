# Уловки, подсмотренные у медоносных пчёл, помогают дронам возвращаться домой без GPS

`res:habr-bee-nav-translation-2026` - blog - <https://habr.com/ru/articles/1050704/>

**By:** Viacheslav Golovanov (translator), Etiido Uko (New Atlas) - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'Tricks borrowed from honeybees help drones return home without GPS'. Russian translation of a New Atlas piece on TU Delft's Bee-Nav: after a short exploratory flight capturing panoramas, a tiny network (3.4 KB indoors, 42 KB outdoors) estimates direction and distance home. Reported outdoor runs exceeded 600 m, with ~70% success in wind.

## Key takeaways

- Homing can be learned with kilobyte-size models
- Wind tilt degrades panoramic matching
- Shows Russian tech media tracking GNSS-free homing closely
