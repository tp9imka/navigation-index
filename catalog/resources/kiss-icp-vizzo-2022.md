# KISS-ICP: In Defense of Point-to-Point ICP -- Simple, Accurate, and Robust Registration If Done the Right Way

`res:kiss-icp-vizzo-2022` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15397>

**By:** Ignacio Vizzo, Tiziano Guadagnino, Benedikt Mersch, et al. - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Argues that a carefully engineered point-to-point ICP with motion compensation and adaptive thresholds matches far more complex LiDAR odometry across platforms.

## Key takeaways

- Few parameters, works across sensors and platforms
- Strong baseline before reaching for complex pipelines
