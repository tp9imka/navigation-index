# DARPA Robust Optical Clock Network (ROCkN)

`res:darpa-rockn` - doc - <https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/robust-optical-clock-network>

**By:** DARPA - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Program to field low-SWaP optical atomic clocks with timing holdover beyond GPS satellite clocks, in a portable airborne variant and a transportable land/sea variant, to keep precise time without GNSS.

## Key takeaways

- Two technical areas: portable and transportable clocks
- Two two-year phases from physics package to operational clock
