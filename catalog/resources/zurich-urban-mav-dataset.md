# The Zurich Urban Micro Aerial Vehicle Dataset

`res:zurich-urban-mav-dataset` - dataset - <https://rpg.ifi.uzh.ch/zurichmavdataset.html>

**By:** University of Zurich RPG - **Year:** 2017 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Low-altitude urban MAV flight over streets with camera, IMU and GPS plus Google Street View imagery for appearance-based localization.

## Key takeaways

- Sensors: camera, IMU, GPS
- Use for aerial-to-street-view localization
