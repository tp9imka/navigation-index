# Navipedia - ESA GNSS encyclopedia

**By:** European Space Agency, ESA - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

ESA's free wiki covering GNSS fundamentals, systems, signals, receivers, augmentation and applications. The fastest authoritative lookup for any GNSS term.

## Key takeaways

- Peer-reviewed style articles maintained by ESA
- Covers GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou, SBAS
- Best free reference for GNSS concepts
- Good RAIM and error-model explanations
- Reliable quick reference for GNSS equations
- Covers Galileo signal and service details
