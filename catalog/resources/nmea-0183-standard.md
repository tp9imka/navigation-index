# NMEA 0183 Interface Standard

`res:nmea-0183-standard` - standard - <https://www.nmea.org/nmea-0183.html>

**By:** National Marine Electronics Association - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** paywalled

The ASCII serial sentence standard (GGA, RMC, GSA, GSV, VTG...) used by nearly all GNSS receivers and marine electronics. The official standard is sold; open references cover the common sentences.

## Key takeaways

- Talker IDs distinguish GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou and multi-GNSS output
- Checksummed ASCII over serial, easy to parse and to fake
- Still the lowest-common-denominator GNSS output
