# Initialization-Free LEO Doppler Positioning via Semidefinite Relaxation: A Cold-Start Approach

`res:leo-doppler-cold-start-2025` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17198>

**By:** Baoshan Song, Weisong Wen, Qi Zhang et al. - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Formulates LEO Doppler positioning as an optimization solvable by semidefinite relaxation so it does not need a good initial position guess.

## Key takeaways

- Removes the initialization problem in Doppler positioning
