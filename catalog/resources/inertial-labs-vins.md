# Inertial Labs VINS visual-aided INS (with Maxar Raptor)

`res:inertial-labs-vins` - product - <https://inertiallabs.com/inertial-labs-launches-visual-aided-ins-for-gps-compromised-paths/>

**By:** Inertial Labs (VIAVI) - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Launched June 2025, VINS combines an Inertial Labs INS with Maxar Raptor software that matches camera frames to Maxar Precision3D satellite-derived 3D maps for absolute position on long-range UAV flights.

## Key takeaways

- Vendor claims: about 35 m horizontal / 5 m vertical without GNSS
- Options for Iridium-based PNT and M-code receivers
- Commercial map product (Precision3D) is the moat; open teams use free orthophotos and DEMs
