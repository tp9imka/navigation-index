# Vyriy створила маякову навігацію для дронів, яка працює без GPS і зв’язку

`res:devua-vyriy-beacon-navigation-2026` - blog - <https://dev.ua/news/vyriy-stvoryla-maiakovu-navihatsiiu-dlia-droniv-iaka-pratsiuie-bez-gps-i-zviazku-1772792856>

**By:** dev.ua - **Year:** 2026 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

English: 'Vyriy built beacon navigation for drones that works without GPS or a data link'. Ground radio beacons placed along a route in advance; an onboard board ranges to several beacons by delay or signal strength and trilaterates, like a local terrestrial GNSS. Frequencies, range and accuracy are not disclosed.

## Key takeaways

- Local terrestrial ranging beacons are a fielded GNSS substitute in Ukraine
- Works without any link to the operator once the route is confirmed
- No performance numbers published
