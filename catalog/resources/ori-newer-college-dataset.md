# Newer College Dataset

`res:ori-newer-college-dataset` - dataset - <https://ori-drs.github.io/newer-college-dataset/>

**By:** Oxford Robotics Institute - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Handheld lidar plus stereo and multi-camera sequences around New College, Oxford, with precise ground truth from a survey-grade prior map. Now hosted on Hugging Face.
