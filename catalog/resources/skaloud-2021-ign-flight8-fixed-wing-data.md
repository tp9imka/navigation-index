# Fixed-Wing Micro UAV Open Data With Digicam And Raw INS/GNSS - IGN Flight 8

`res:skaloud-2021-ign-flight8-fixed-wing-data` - dataset - <https://zenodo.org/record/4476489>

**By:** Jan Skaloud, Davide Antonio Cucci, Kenneth Joseph Paul - **Year:** 2021 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Zenodo record from EPFL TOPO: one fixed-wing micro UAV flight with raw IMU, GNSS and camera data. Single 5.9 GB zip, CC BY 4.0.

## Key takeaways

- Raw INS/GNSS from a real small fixed-wing, permissive license
- Motor and airspeed logging not stated on the record
- Same group as the VDM navigation work
