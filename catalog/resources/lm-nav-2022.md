# LM-Nav: Robotic Navigation with Large Pre-Trained Models of Language, Vision, and Action

`res:lm-nav-2022` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04429>

**By:** Shah et al. - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Combines an LLM for landmark extraction, CLIP for grounding and a visual navigation model to follow long route instructions outdoors.

## Key takeaways

- No language-annotated robot data needed
