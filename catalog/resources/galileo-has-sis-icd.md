# Galileo High Accuracy Service Signal-In-Space ICD (HAS SIS ICD) Issue 1.0

`res:galileo-has-sis-icd` - standard - <https://www.gsc-europa.eu/sites/default/files/sites/all/files/Galileo_HAS_SIS_ICD_v1.0.pdf>

**By:** EU / European GNSS Service Centre - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

Defines the free Galileo High Accuracy Service: PPP orbit, clock and bias corrections broadcast on E6-B (and via internet), including message structure and Reed-Solomon encoding.

## Key takeaways

- Free PPP corrections broadcast from the satellites themselves
- Enables decimetre-level PPP without a ground subscription
- E6-B message decoding details for receiver builders
