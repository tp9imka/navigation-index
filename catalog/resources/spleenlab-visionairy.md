# Spleenlab VISIONAIRY perception and GNSS-free navigation software

`res:spleenlab-visionairy` - product - <https://auterion.com/safe-autonomous-drone-operations/>

**By:** Spleenlab - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

German company Spleenlab sells VISIONAIRY, a modular perception suite (VIO, map-based relocalization, EO/IR and LiDAR SLAM) that integrates with PX4/Auterion and ROS; used by Quantum Systems and Blueflite for GNSS-denied navigation.
