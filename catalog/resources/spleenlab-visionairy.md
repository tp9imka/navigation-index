# Spleenlab VISIONAIRY perception and GNSS-free navigation software

`res:spleenlab-visionairy` - product - <https://auterion.com/safe-autonomous-drone-operations/>

**By:** Spleenlab - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

German company Spleenlab sells VISIONAIRY, an AI perception suite for drones (vendor description: VIO, map-based relocalization, EO/IR and LiDAR SLAM) that joined the Auterion ecosystem in 2022 and integrates with PX4/Auterion and ROS. Quantum Systems later acquired Spleenlab and uses it for GNSS-denied navigation (press).

## Key takeaways

- Vendor description only; no published accuracy
- Module list mirrors what open VIO + VPR repos offer
