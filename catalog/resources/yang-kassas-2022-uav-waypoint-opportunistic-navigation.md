# UAV Waypoint Opportunistic Navigation in GNSS-Denied Environments

`res:yang-kassas-2022-uav-waypoint-opportunistic-navigation` - paper - <https://ece.osu.edu/sites/default/files/2022-09/UAV_waypoint_opportunistic_navigation_in_GNSS_denied_environments.pdf>

**By:** Yanhao Yang, Joe Khalife, Joshua Morales, Zaher M. Kassas - **Year:** 2022 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

IEEE TAES 58(1):663-678. A greedy one-step multi-objective motion planner chooses UAV acceleration and heading within speed and acceleration limits, trading progress to a waypoint against position uncertainty estimated from signals of opportunity. Reports 95% success vs 36% for flying straight, plus a real cellular-SOP flight. Accepted preprint read (abstract and method).

## Key takeaways

- Clear evidence that 'fly straight and wait' can lose to information-aware maneuvering
- Greedy one-step, Gaussian EKF, RF pseudoranges: not multimodal map matching
