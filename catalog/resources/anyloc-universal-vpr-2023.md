# AnyLoc: Towards Universal Visual Place Recognition

`res:anyloc-universal-vpr-2023` - paper - <https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00688>

**By:** Keetha et al. - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Shows that unsupervised aggregation of foundation-model (DINOv2) features gives place recognition that generalizes across domains including aerial imagery, without VPR-specific training.

## Key takeaways

- Foundation features + VLAD work training-free
- Aerial domain included in the evaluation
