# DOD adopts 5 principles of artificial intelligence ethics

**By:** US Department of Defense - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Announcement of the DoD's AI ethical principles - responsible, equitable, traceable, reliable, governable - recommended by the Defense Innovation Board.

## Key takeaways

- Traceable and governable map to logging and safe fallback in nav autonomy
- Judges increasingly ask how an AI component can be disengaged
