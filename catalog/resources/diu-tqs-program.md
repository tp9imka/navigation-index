# DIU Transition of Quantum Sensing (TQS)

`res:diu-tqs-program` - program - <https://www.diu.mil/latest/dius-transition-of-quantum-sensing-tqs-field-testing-to-begin-across-five>

**By:** Defense Innovation Unit - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

DIU programme fielding quantum sensors for PNT: lines of effort cover quantum inertial sensors, gravimeters for gravity-aided naval navigation, quantum magnetic sensing, magnetic navigation and component insertion, with more than 10 field tests planned in the first ~12 months (announced March 2025).

## Key takeaways

- 18 named performers including Honeywell, Lockheed Martin, Q-CTRL, AOSense, Anduril, Northrop Grumman, Vector Atomic
- Honeywell flew a quantum MagNav test on an Embraer 170 under TQS (press)
