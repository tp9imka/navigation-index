# EASA and IATA comprehensive plan to mitigate the risks of GNSS interference

`res:easa-iata-gnss-interference-plan-2025` - report - <https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2025-releases/2025-06-18-01/>

**By:** IATA, EASA - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Joint plan from a May 2025 EASA/IATA workshop setting out actions on reporting and monitoring, prevention and mitigation, airspace/infrastructure (backup navaids) and civil-military coordination against GNSS interference.

## Key takeaways

- Four pillars: reporting, prevention, infrastructure, coordination
- Retaining conventional navaids is explicitly part of resilience
- Calls for better civil-military sharing of interference data
- Standard radio calls and NOTAM Q-codes for interference
- Tighter controls on jammer sales
- Keep a minimum network of conventional navaids as backup
