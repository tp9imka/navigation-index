# Online Training: Mobile Robotics (Basics + Robotics blocks)

`res:stachniss-mobile-robotics-online-training` - lecture-series - <https://www.ipb.uni-bonn.de/online-training-robotics/>

**By:** Cyrill Stachniss, University of Bonn - **Year:** 2020 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

A curated self-study path of short recorded lectures from Bonn's Photogrammetry & Robotics lab, going from linear algebra, rotations and least squares through Bayes filters, EKF localization, particle filters, ICP, RANSAC and graph-based SLAM. Each topic pairs a '5 Minutes with Cyrill' teaser with a full-length lecture.

## Key takeaways

- Fastest structured on-ramp to probabilistic state estimation
- Covers EKF/MCL localization and pose-graph SLAM end to end
- Links the older full SLAM course playlist

**Concepts:** [Sensor fusion and state estimation](../../catalog/concepts/sensor-fusion.md), [Visual SLAM](../../catalog/concepts/visual-slam.md), [LiDAR odometry and SLAM](../../catalog/concepts/lidar-slam.md)
