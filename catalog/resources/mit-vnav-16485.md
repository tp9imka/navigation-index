# MIT 16.485 Visual Navigation for Autonomous Vehicles (VNAV)

`res:mit-vnav-16485` - course - <https://vnav.mit.edu/>

**By:** Luca Carlone, MIT SPARK Lab - **Year:** 2023 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** open

MIT graduate course on the math and software of visual navigation: 3D geometry, Lie groups, quadrotor modelling and control, trajectory optimization, feature tracking, two-view geometry, nonlinear optimization, VIO and SLAM. Lecture slides/notes and ROS 2 based lab handouts are public, with lab code on GitHub.

## Key takeaways

- Labs walk from ROS basics to a quadrotor VIO stack
- Strong on certifiable/robust estimation
- Lecture notes downloadable per topic
