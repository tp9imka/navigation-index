# Ukraine's Autonomous Killer Drones Defeat Electronic Warfare

`res:ieee-spectrum-ukraine-drones-ew-2025` - blog - <https://spectrum.ieee.org/ukraine-killer-drones>

**By:** IEEE Spectrum - **Year:** 2025 - **Level:** intro - **Access:** open

Reporting on how Ukrainian drone developers respond to pervasive jamming of radio links and satellite navigation, including onboard optical navigation that keeps the drone flying after links are lost.

## Key takeaways

- Jamming makes onboard, map-based navigation a front-line requirement
- Optical navigation is spreading to cheap drones
