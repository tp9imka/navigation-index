# Safran VersaPNT and Geonyx resilient PNT units

`res:safran-versapnt` - product - <https://safran-navigation-timing.com/solution/defense/>

**By:** Safran - **Year:** 2024 - **Level:** intermediate - **Access:** open

Safran's defense PNT range includes VersaPNT (GNSS receiver + IMU + disciplined oscillator with jamming/spoofing detection and NTP/PTP time serving) and the Geonyx HRG-based INS; GPS World reports a NAVKITE kit combining Geonyx M with VersaSync for French Navy commandos.

## Key takeaways

- Combined position + timing holdover in one box is the standard defense product shape
- Detection flags and holdover timing are reproducible in open stacks at lower grade
