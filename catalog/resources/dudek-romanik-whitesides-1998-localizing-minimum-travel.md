# Localizing a Robot with Minimum Travel

`res:dudek-romanik-whitesides-1998-localizing-minimum-travel` - paper - <https://cim.mcgill.ca/~mrl/pubs/dudek/siamj_abs.html>

**By:** Gregory Dudek, Kathleen Romanik, Sue Whitesides - **Year:** 1998 - **Level:** advanced - **Access:** paywalled

SIAM Journal on Computing 27(2). A robot in a known polygon whose start matches several look-alike candidate positions must travel to tell them apart. Minimum-travel localization is shown NP-hard and an approximation strategy with a bounded travel ratio is given. Abstract only read.

## Key takeaways

- Optimal hypothesis elimination by travel is NP-hard: use heuristics and say so
- Frames localization as a verification tour over candidate positions
