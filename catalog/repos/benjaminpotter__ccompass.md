Header-only C library for processing sky polarization images to support heading estimation; successor to compyss.

**Why it matters:** Polarization compass gives heading without magnetometers or GNSS, robust to magnetic disturbance.

**Hackathon notes:** Needs a polarization camera (or simulated images).

**Concepts:** [Celestial and star-tracker navigation](../concepts/celestial-nav.md)
