Robot-centric elevation mapping for rough terrain navigation, started at ETH Zurich and now maintained by ANYbotics, that fuses range data into a probabilistic 2.5D map. This entry is the leggedrobotics fork of ANYbotics/elevation_mapping.

**Why it matters:** Classic baseline for terrain mapping on legged and wheeled robots.

**Hackathon notes:** ROS 1; this fork is dormant since 2022, while upstream ANYbotics/elevation_mapping was updated in 2024. Prefer the GPU version.
