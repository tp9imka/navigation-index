Multi-sensor dataset with infrared and visible video plus audio clips of drones, birds, airplanes and helicopters, from a Swedish thesis project.

**Why it matters:** Lets a team prototype sensor fusion for C-UAS (thermal + RGB + audio) on one consistent set of targets and distractors.

**Hackathon notes:** Ready to download; good for a fused IR+audio classifier demo in a weekend.
