Python port of the demo5 RTKLIB positioning core, currently focused on post-processed kinematic (PPK) solutions.

**Why it matters:** Readable Python version of RTK math, good for teaching, prototyping new filters or swapping in ML components.

**Hackathon notes:** Slow compared to C RTKLIB and PPK only; last pushed 2023. Ideal for instrumenting or modifying the filter during a hackathon.
