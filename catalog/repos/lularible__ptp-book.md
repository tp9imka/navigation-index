Open book on PTP (IEEE 1588 / gPTP) in Chinese with an English translation: BMCA, the four-timestamp maths, transparent clocks, hardware timestamping, security, a walk through linuxptp source, and a ~1000-line ptp-lite implementation.

**Why it matters:** Fast self-study path for a team member who has to explain or modify PTP during the event.

**Hackathon notes:** Read the English chapters for BMCA and the servo; ptp-lite is small enough to instrument for a delay-attack demo.
