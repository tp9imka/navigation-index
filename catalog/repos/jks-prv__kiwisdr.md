Web-accessible shortwave SDR receiver (10 kHz - 30 MHz) with built-in software GPS and a Loran-C/eLoran monitor extension.

**Why it matters:** Public KiwiSDR network offers remote access to LF signals for eLoran/Loran experiments without owning hardware.

**Hackathon notes:** Use public KiwiSDR receivers to look at LF navigation signals; not a positioning solver.
