Playground GNSS positioning code in Python/numpy that computes a static GPS position (50-100 m accuracy per the README) from RINEX 2.11 observations, with parsers for RINEX nav/obs, SP3 and IONEX plus Klobuchar, IONEX and VMF1 delay models. Works on RINEX files, not raw IF samples, so there is no acquisition or tracking stage.

**Why it matters:** Python-readable receiver chain for teaching and quick experiments.

**Hackathon notes:** Unmaintained since 2020; use for learning.
