Racing/freestyle-oriented flight controller firmware focused on attitude control and low-latency filtering; GPS rescue mode is its main navigation feature.

**Why it matters:** Dominant firmware on FPV drones seen in current conflicts; understanding its limited navigation (GPS Rescue only) matters for C-UAS and EW-impact analysis.

**Hackathon notes:** Not a navigation platform; use for FPV-class hardware or to study how small drones react to link loss and GNSS jamming.
