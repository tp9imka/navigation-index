GitHub mirror of LibrePilot, the community continuation of OpenPilot for CC3D/Revolution boards, with the GCS bundled. Main development was on Bitbucket and has been quiet for years.

**Why it matters:** Historical reference for the OpenPilot-era INS13 estimator and UAVTalk protocol; not a base for new work.

**Hackathon notes:** Read-only reference. Hardware is end-of-life.
