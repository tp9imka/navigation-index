Gazebo Classic MAV simulator from ETH ASL with multirotor models, IMU and odometry sensor plugins and example controllers.

**Why it matters:** Classic reference for MAV sim used in many VIO and planning papers; its IMU noise models are still frequently copied.

**Hackathon notes:** ROS1 and Gazebo Classic only; use for reproducing older papers rather than new builds.
