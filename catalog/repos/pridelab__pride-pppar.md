Wuhan University's open-source multi-GNSS precise point positioning package with ambiguity resolution, aimed at geodetic-grade post-processing.

**Why it matters:** Free PPP-AR gives cm-level positions without a local base station, a key capability where RTK networks are unavailable.

**Hackathon notes:** Linux build; needs precise products downloaded. Post-processing only, so plan for hours of data.
