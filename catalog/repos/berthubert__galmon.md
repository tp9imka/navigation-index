Open-source GNSS monitoring network and software that collects navigation data from volunteer receivers worldwide and publishes live health, ephemeris and timing statistics for Galileo, GPS, BeiDou and GLONASS.

**Why it matters:** Independent constellation monitoring, valuable for spotting anomalies and checking broadcast data integrity.

**Hackathon notes:** Run a feeder with a u-blox receiver, or just consume the public galmon.eu data.
