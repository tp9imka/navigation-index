LSTM (Bayesian and standard) classifiers that identify individual jammers from IQ recordings, from an ICL-GNSS 2024 paper on how environment and receiver affect classification.

**Why it matters:** Fingerprinting which jammer is present is a step toward attribution and targeted mitigation.

**Hackathon notes:** Needs your own IQ recordings in the expected file layout; reuse the model code on Jammertest data.
