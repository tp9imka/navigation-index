Official test catalog and plan for Jammertest, Norway's annual live GNSS jamming and spoofing event, maintained by the Norwegian Public Roads Administration.

**Why it matters:** The reference for which interference scenarios were transmitted when, needed to label any Jammertest recording.

**Hackathon notes:** Use the per-year plan to map timestamps in Jammertest datasets to emitted scenarios.
