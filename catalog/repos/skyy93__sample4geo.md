Cross-view geo-localization training with hard negative sampling, reporting strong results on drone-satellite and street-satellite benchmarks with a simple contrastive setup.

**Why it matters:** A strong, simple baseline that is easy to retrain for your own drone/satellite pairs.

**Hackathon notes:** Pretrained checkpoints help; fine-tune on your tile set if time allows.
