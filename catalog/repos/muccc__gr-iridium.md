GNU Radio blocks and apps that detect and demodulate Iridium bursts from SDR recordings.

**Why it matters:** The receive front-end for Iridium signals-of-opportunity experiments; burst timing and Doppler are the observables a LEO Doppler positioner needs.

**Hackathon notes:** No LICENSE file at the root; source headers carry GPL-3.0-or-later. Needs an SDR and L-band antenna or recorded IQ. Positioning itself is not included.

**Concepts:** [Signals of opportunity](../../catalog/concepts/signals-of-opportunity.md), [Software-defined GNSS receivers](../../catalog/concepts/gnss-sdr.md)
