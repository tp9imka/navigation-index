Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer: a feed-forward network predicting camera poses, depth and point maps from one or many images (CVPR 2025 best paper).

**Why it matters:** Instant pose and geometry from a handful of frames; a building block for learned relocalization and mapping.

**Hackathon notes:** GPU inference via PyTorch; Hugging Face demo for quick tests. Not a full SLAM system by itself.
