Event-based stereo visual odometry using spatio-temporal consistency of two event cameras for mapping and tracking.

**Why it matters:** Event cameras keep working under high dynamic range and fast motion, where frame cameras blur or saturate.

**Hackathon notes:** Needs stereo event cameras or recorded datasets; ROS 1.

**Concepts:** [Visual(-inertial) odometry](../concepts/vio.md), [Visual SLAM](../concepts/visual-slam.md)
