MATLAB-style utility functions from UT Austin's Radionavigation Lab for simulating Starlink Ku-band downlink signals, with playground examples.

**Why it matters:** Lets teams experiment with Starlink as a signal of opportunity for PNT without capturing real Ku-band data.

**Hackathon notes:** Small codebase; pair with the Humphreys Starlink signal-structure paper.
