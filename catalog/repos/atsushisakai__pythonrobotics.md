Large collection of readable Python implementations of robotics algorithms: localization (EKF, particle filter), SLAM, path planning (A*, RRT*, D*, state lattice), path tracking and more, with animations.

**Why it matters:** Best teaching and prototyping resource; copy a planner or filter into a hackathon prototype in minutes.

**Hackathon notes:** Pure numpy/matplotlib; no build. Good for a quick 2D planner over a rasterized DEM.

**Concepts:** [Path and motion planning](../concepts/path-planning.md), [Sensor fusion and state estimation](../concepts/sensor-fusion.md), [Visual SLAM](../concepts/visual-slam.md)
