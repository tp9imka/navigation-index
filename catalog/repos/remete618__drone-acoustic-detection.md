Open field-test toolkit for acoustic and radar detection of low-signature drones on COTS hardware, aiming at FPV detection baselines.

**Why it matters:** FPV-class detection baselines are missing in literature; this gives a reproducible method.

**Hackathon notes:** MIT, but the README frames it as research and educational use. No field recordings ship in the repo - the experiments run on synthesized signals (--mock) until you record your own. Follow the field protocol to measure your own detection ranges.
