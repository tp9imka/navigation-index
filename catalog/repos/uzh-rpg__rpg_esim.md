Event camera simulator from UZH RPG that renders event streams from simulated scenes.

**Why it matters:** Generates event-camera data for high-speed or low-light VIO research without hardware.

**Hackathon notes:** ROS1 and older build tooling. For converting existing video to events, rpg_vid2e is easier.
