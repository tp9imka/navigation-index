Open-source LoRa/FLRC radio control link (2.4 GHz and sub-GHz) with high packet rates, telemetry and MAVLink passthrough modes.

**Why it matters:** Dominant open RC link in FPV; its behaviour under jamming and its telemetry options matter for EW-resilience and C-UAS work.

**Hackathon notes:** Flash with ExpressLRS Configurator; receivers are cheap.

**Concepts:** [Electronic warfare resilience](../concepts/ew-resilience.md), [Counter-UAS and drone detection](../concepts/counter-uas.md)
