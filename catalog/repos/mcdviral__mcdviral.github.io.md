Site repo for MCD, a multi-campus dataset across Asia and Europe with spinning and non-repetitive lidar, stereo cameras, IMUs, UWB ranging, survey-grade prior maps and continuous-time ground truth.

**Why it matters:** Covers localization against prior maps and UWB-aided fusion in large outdoor areas.

**Hackathon notes:** Data downloads and tutorials are linked from the site. Licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, intended for non-commercial academic use.
