Mutual relative localization for UAV swarms using blinking ultraviolet LEDs and UV-sensitive cameras, from CTU MRS.

**Why it matters:** Radio-silent relative localization, attractive when RF is jammed or must stay off.

**Hackathon notes:** Needs special UV hardware for real use; simulated in the MRS Gazebo setup.
