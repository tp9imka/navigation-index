ROS 2 centralized lidar-ranging-inertial state estimation for robotic swarms that offloads optimization to a server and uses UWB inter-robot ranges (ICRA 2024).

**Why it matters:** Shows how UWB ranging tightens swarm state estimation when GNSS is out.

**Hackathon notes:** ROS 2 Foxy and GTSAM 4.2; build is sensitive to versions.

**Concepts:** [Swarms and multi-agent navigation](../concepts/multi-agent.md), [LiDAR odometry and SLAM](../concepts/lidar-slam.md), [RF localization and direction finding](../concepts/rf-localization.md), [Sensor fusion and state estimation](../concepts/sensor-fusion.md)
