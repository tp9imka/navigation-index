Plug-in module adding Direct Position Estimation (positioning directly in the correlation domain) to MATLAB two-step software receivers such as SoftGNSS.

**Why it matters:** DPE is studied for robustness in weak-signal, multipath and interference conditions; this is one of few open implementations.

**Hackathon notes:** Research code; computationally heavy. Useful as a stretch comparison against conventional tracking.
