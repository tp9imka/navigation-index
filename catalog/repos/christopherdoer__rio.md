EKF-based radar-inertial odometry using 4D mmWave radar ego-velocity measurements.

**Why it matters:** Cheap automotive radars give velocity in fog/smoke/darkness; a minimal radar odometry template.

**Hackathon notes:** ROS 1; uses the author's radar ego-velocity estimator. Works with TI mmWave-class sensors.
