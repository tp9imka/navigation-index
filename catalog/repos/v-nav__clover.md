Archived fork of the Clover framework holding team V-NAV's CopterHack 2022 entry (article in docs/ru/v-nav.md on branch v-nav_article): a multi-camera plus IMU optical navigation module using graph-based SLAM with CNN-extracted features, aimed at indoor and GNSS-denied industrial-site flights, with a companion Pixhawk FMUv6U + CM4 + Coral board concept.

**Why it matters:** Shows how a Russian student team scoped GNSS-free navigation on the Clover/PX4 stack; a design reference more than working code.

**Hackathon notes:** Read the article on the v-nav_article branch; the fork itself is mostly upstream Clover.
