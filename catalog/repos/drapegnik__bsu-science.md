LaTeX sources of science reports from BSU (Belarus, FAMCS), including a diploma 'Разработка алгоритмов навигации БПЛА' that compares SIFT/SURF/ORB, surveys SLAM and SfM 3D mapping, and demonstrates SLAM + ROS + webcam + SfM to localise a UAV on a 3D terrain map without GPS.

**Why it matters:** A short student-level map of the visual-navigation design space in Russian.

**Hackathon notes:** Slides only (see the GitHub Pages presentation); no runnable pipeline.
