Dataset, ROS nodes and DWM1001 firmware from the IROS 2020 paper on UWB localization for autonomous UAV flight: several anchor configurations, flights inside and outside the anchor hull, and an anchor auto-positioning method.

**Why it matters:** Cheap Decawave hardware recipe plus real flight data for UWB-aided GNSS-denied flight.

**Hackathon notes:** Use the data to tune a UWB EKF before buying hardware. No licence file. Not updated since 2022.
