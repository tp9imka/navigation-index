Learning-based event-inertial odometry: a learned event-patch front-end coupled with IMU pre-integration in a factor graph, evaluated on ten public event benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** Recent open event+IMU odometry with a permissive license, the candidate if the challenge stresses high-dynamic-range or night flight.

**Build notes:** MIT. Code uploaded 2025-07. Needs a GPU and event data (VECtor, etc.); no event camera on most hackathon rigs.
