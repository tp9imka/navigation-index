# Anti-spoof autopilot configurations, tested: what keeps PX4 and ArduPilot flying under a coherent GNSS drag

<!-- about: repo:px4/px4-autopilot, repo:ardupilot/ardupilot, repo:ardupilot/pymavlink, repo:mavlink/mavlink -->

`samples/autopilot-spoof-response` showed that both EKFs end up on a spoofed position: a
coherent drag (position and velocity moved together) is fused, and a rejected one is reset
onto after 7 s (EKF2) / 10 s (EKF3) when nothing else is fused. This sample tries 13
configurations that add a second source or a consistency check, and flies each against the
same coherent drag at 0.3, 1 and 3 m/s plus a clean flight, 3 seeds each: **156 SITL
flights**, PX4 v1.17.0 SIH and ArduCopter 4.6.3, ran 2026-11-06.

**Short answer.**

- **ArduPilot: a 95-line Lua applet that switches EKF3 to source set 2 (ExternalNav).**
  [`scripts/gnss_extnav_switch.lua`](scripts/gnss_extnav_switch.lua) +
  [`configs/ardupilot/lua-switch.parm`](configs/ardupilot/lua-switch.parm). Switched 2.3-3.5 s
  after onset at 1 and 3 m/s, max EKF error 9.1-11.7 m, no false switch on 3 clean flights.
  It does not catch 0.3 m/s in time (1 of 3 caught, at 91 s).
- **PX4: `EKF2_EV_CTRL 4` (VIO velocity) + `EKF2_GPS_MODE 1` + GNSS gates 3.**
  [`configs/px4/ev-vel-gpsmode1-gates3.params`](configs/px4/ev-vel-gpsmode1-gates3.params).
  EKF2 dropped GNSS by itself 8.3-15.1 s after onset at 1 and 3 m/s and kept flying on VIO
  velocity, max EKF error 4.7-12.5 m. Not at 0.3 m/s. Without the tighter gates only 3 m/s was
  held. After GNSS is dropped it stays out for the rest of the flight (dead reckoning).
- **Nothing onboard caught a 0.3 m/s drag.** Only the external companion monitor (VIO + visual
  geolocation, solution separation) did, at 44-51 s, error <= 14 m. It is also the only PX4
  config that held all 9 spoofed flights.
- **Parameters alone do not help** on either autopilot: fuse-all-velocities, `EK3_GLITCH_RAD 0`
  and EV velocity in EKF2 mode 0 were all believed 92-101 % like the defaults.

## What each result means

**ArduPilot `lua-switch` (rank 1).** The applet dead-reckons from the GPS fix at arming with
ExternalNav (VIO) velocity and never re-anchors on GPS. It recovers ExternalNav velocity from
EKF3's own innovations, which EKF3 computes for reporting even when that source is not fused
(`AP_NavEKF3_PosVelFusion.cpp` 520-528, `extNavVelInnov`; innovation = state - observation,
line 488). It trips on GPS vs dead-reckoning position > 25 m, or GPS vs VIO velocity > 0.7 m/s
for 2 s, then calls `ahrs:set_posvelyaw_source_set(1)` once (set 2 = ExternalNav position +
velocity, `golden.parm` / `v2.parm`). At 1 and 3 m/s the velocity test fired at 2.3-3.5 s.
The position switch then reset EKF3 onto the companion's GNSS-free reference, so the EKF
error at the end of each flight was 4.9-5.9 m (the reference's own map-registration bias,
see golden-demo). On clean flights the dead-reckoning distance peaked at 11.5 m (the
applet's own 5 s reports), so the 25 m threshold has about 2x margin over ~170 s of flight
with this simulated VIO (bias 0.05 / -0.03 m/s). A 0.3 m/s drag never trips the velocity
test and reaches 25 m only near the end of the mission (one seed tripped at 91 s, after the
EKF error had already peaked at 27 m).

**`lua-switch-fuse-all-vel`**: same results within 0.5 m. `docs/autopilot-gnss-fault-handling.md`
warned that `EK3_SRC_OPTIONS 1` keeps GNSS velocity fused after the switch; here it did not
change the outcome (end error 5.0-5.8 m vs 4.9-5.9 m). Leaving it off is still the simpler
choice.

**ArduPilot `dual-gps-affinity`**: two receivers, only GPS1 spoofed, `EK3_AFFINITY 1`. At
3 m/s the onset velocity step made lane 0 (GPS1) score worse and EKF3 printed `EKF3 lane
switch 1` 0.1 s after onset (all 3 seeds): error <= 2.4 m for the rest of the flight. At 0.3 and 1 m/s
no lane switched and the spoofed lane was believed (92-95 m at 1 m/s). Lane selection
compares innovation scores; a drag slow enough to stay inside the gate never loses.

**ArduPilot `dual-gps-blend`** (`GPS_AUTO_SWITCH 2`): blended position = the average of
spoofed and honest receivers (equal reported accuracy), so the EKF believed exactly half the
spoof (capture ratio 0.48-0.50 in all 9 flights). "Safe" at 0.3 m/s only because half of
28 m is under the bar.

**ArduPilot `glitch-clip`, `fuse-all-vel`**: no reject, believed 92-100 %. Clipping
(`EK3_GLITCH_RAD 0`) and fusing VIO velocity alongside GPS do not separate a coherent drag
from the truth: the GPS still dominates position. Both produced 5-6 `EKF3 lane switch`
messages per 3 m/s flight (default: 1 in 9 flights) without changing the outcome.

**PX4 `ev-vel-gpsmode1-gates3` (best onboard PX4).** With EV velocity fused, a GNSS velocity
3 SD out is rejected without losing aiding. GNSS position then drifts away from the EV-aided
prediction, fails its gate, and after the 7 s timeout `EKF2_GPS_MODE 1` refuses the reset
because EV velocity still aids: `stopGnssPosFusion()` and `gnss_fault = true`
(`gps_control.cpp` 189-205; `isGnssPosResetAllowed` 272-297). `gnss_fault` blocks the reset
path, and a restart without reset needs near-zero test ratios (`gps_control.cpp` 210-236), so
GNSS stays out while the spoof persists: the rest of the flight is VIO dead reckoning. At 3 m/s
GNSS was out 8.3-9.4 s after onset, at 1 m/s 14.9-15.1 s. With 5 SD gates
(`ev-vel-gpsmode1`) 1 m/s took 30-93 s and two of three flights went past 25 m.

**PX4 `ev-vel` (mode 0)**: worse than default at 1 m/s (113-120 m). GNSS velocity rejected,
and in mode 0 a reset onto GNSS is still allowed when only velocity aiding remains
(`isGnssPosResetAllowed`, from source; the individual resets were not isolated in these logs).

**PX4 `agp-gpsmode1` (ranked last, false alarms 3/3).** SIH's `sensor_agp_sim` (truth + 2 m
noise, `eph` 20 m) as aux global position. In all 12 flights EKF2 stopped GNSS position
fusion before the attack (`cs_gnss_pos` false, `cs_gnss_hgt_fault` true in every flight, no
other config ever showed `gnss_hgt_fault`). The "held" spoofed flights flew on AGP alone,
with 12-21 m deviation even on clean flights. One flight first timed out in the climb
(`TimeoutError: takeoff`) and was re-flown. Why `gnss_hgt_fault` is raised with AGP enabled
was not diagnosed. Do not read this row as a recipe.

**PX4 `companion-monitor`**: golden-demo-px4's monitor acting (`EKF2_GPS_CTRL 0` then
`EKF2_EV_CTRL 5`). Same detector as golden-demo; this run adds 0.3 m/s and 3 seeds. It is the
only config that caught 0.3 m/s (solution separation, 44-51 s). PX4 v1.17 has no onboard
scripting, so this is the PX4 equivalent of the ArduPilot Lua applet, one hop further away.
ArduPilot with the same companion monitor is measured in `golden-demo` (52-flight Monte Carlo)
and was not re-flown here.

All ArduPilot files load after the golden-demo base: `copter.parm`, `golden.parm` (SRC1 = GPS,
SRC2 = ExternalNav), `inject.parm` (`GPS1_TYPE 14`), `v2.parm` (`EK3_SRC2_VELXY 6`,
`VISO_VEL_M_NSE 0.2`). The ExternalNav stream is the companion's GNSS-free reference
(VIO dead reckoning + replayed real visual geolocation) as `VISION_POSITION_ESTIMATE` and VIO
as `VISION_SPEED_ESTIMATE`. On PX4 the same reference goes out as `ODOMETRY` at 10 Hz; with
`EKF2_EV_CTRL 4` only its velocity is fused. The PX4 files are `NAME VALUE` lines: `param set`
each one in the PX4 shell, or load them with QGC after converting.

## Run

```sh
cd samples/autopilot-resilient-configs
python3 resil_sweep.py --python /path/to/venv/bin/python        # 156 flights, ~100 min in 3+3 lanes; skips logs in runs/
python3 resil_sweep.py --list --cases lua                       # case names (substring filter)
python3 resil_sweep.py --python ... --cases ardupilot-lua-switch-coherent-1-s1 --force
python3 resil_analyse.py runs/*.raw.jsonl --out data/flights.json --ranking data/ranking.json --markdown
python3 -m unittest -v test_resilient_configs                   # 16 tests; 1 skips without pymavlink
```

Requirements: the `samples/sitl-docker` images (`nav-sitl-px4:v1.17.0`, `nav-sitl-client:latest`,
`nav-sitl-ardupilot:copter-4.6.3`), Docker, and pymavlink for the ArduPilot companion
(`--python`; the PX4 companion runs in the client container). Containers are named
`resil-*`; ArduPilot lanes publish host ports 15830-15839. Logs go to `runs/` (git-ignored,
~6 MB per flight).

Wall time: 5809 s for 151 flights in 3 PX4 + 3 ArduPilot lanes, plus 314 s for 4 smoke
flights (2 kept) and 316 s for one re-flight. About 3.7 min per PX4 flight, 3.2 min per
ArduPilot flight.

## Limits and honest caveats

- SITL only, linux/arm64 Docker on Apple Silicon. PX4 SIH and ArduPilot SITL, no wind, ideal
  IMUs. 3 seeds per cell is a smoke test, not a statistic.
- **The second source is simulated and good.** VIO = truth velocity + 0.1 m/s noise + a fixed
  0.05 / -0.03 m/s bias; visual geolocation is the golden-demo replay table. Every recipe here
  is only as good as that source. A real VIO with scale drift or dropouts will move the
  Lua threshold margin (11.5 m of 25 m used on clean flights) and the PX4 dead-reckoning error.
- **After GNSS is dropped, both winners dead-reckon or ride the reference for the rest of the
  flight.** The missions here end ~100 s after onset. PX4's `gnss_fault` latch never brings
  GNSS back on its own; the Lua applet is one-way by design (switching back to set 1 resets
  onto GPS immediately; see the doc).
- **Two receivers, one spoofed** is the easy case. A spoofer in view of the airframe reaches
  both antennas unless one is protected (CRPA, authenticated signal, different band). The
  dual-GPS rows measure the EKF logic, not that assumption.
- The injected GNSS reports good accuracy and never sets a spoofing flag (`GPS_INPUT` /
  `HIL_GPS`); receiver-side spoof detection was not in the loop.
- Only coherent drags east, onset 25 s into the mission, capped at 150 m. Steps and
  position-only drags are in `autopilot-spoof-response`.
- PX4 GNSS-out times come from 2 s samples of `estimator_status_flags`, so they are +-2 s.
- `agp-gpsmode1`: the GNSS height fault that disabled GNSS in every flight was not diagnosed.
- Not tested: optical flow on either autopilot (no optical-flow simulator module under
  `src/modules/simulation` at PX4 v1.17.0, by directory listing; SIH internals not checked; ArduPilot SITL flow was not wired into the companion), Lua threshold tuning (a lower
  position threshold would catch 0.3 m/s sooner at the cost of the false-alarm margin),
  ArduPilot companion monitor (see golden-demo), Plane / fixed-wing, hardware.
