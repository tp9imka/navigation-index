Each item: what changed, the source, and what it means for a navigation hackathon project.
Scope is navigation only; weapon autonomy and terminal guidance debates are out of scope.

## 1. Fiber-optic drones reduce RF reliance, not navigation need

- Fiber-optic FPV drones take control and video over a cable and cannot be radio-jammed; both sides
  fielded them at scale in 2025
  ([Lowy Institute, Jun 2025](https://www.lowyinstitute.org/the-interpreter/fibre-optic-drones-reshape-ukraine-s-technological-war));
  Russian fiber drones reached Kramatorsk (Oct 2025) and Kharkiv
  ([Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2025/10/05/russia-hits-kramatorsk-with-fiber-optic-drone-immune-to-ukrainian-jamming/),
  [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-fpv-drone-on-fiber-optic-cable-reaches-kharkiv-for-1st-time-since-start-of-full-scale-invasion-prosecutors-office-says/)).
  Range is cable-limited and spools leave tell-tale cables ([TWZ](https://www.twz.com/news-features/inside-ukraines-fiber-optic-drone-war)).
- Implication: fiber solves the short-range, operator-in-the-loop case by keeping a human pilot
  on video. Longer-range, low-altitude and autonomous segments still need onboard navigation, and
  jamming of GNSS stays the norm (`res:ngrn-ukraine-deftech-2025`). It also shifts some C-UAS
  detection away from RF sensing toward EO/acoustic.

## 2. Cheap visual navigation modules became a commodity

- Several vendors sell camera-to-map modules for small UAS: Twist Robotics OSCAR (Jan 2026),
  Vermeer, Blue Arrow in Ukraine; Theseus, UAVOS NavAI, Spleenlab, Auterion Visual Navigation
  elsewhere (see [products](gnss-denied-nav-products.md)). Theseus started at a February 2024
  hackathon ([Aviation Week](https://aviationweek.com/aerospace/emerging-technologies/how-trio-engineers-developed-gps-denied-drone-under-500)).
- IEEE Spectrum (2025) reports onboard optical navigation as the Ukrainian answer to jamming
  (`res:ieee-spectrum-ukraine-drones-ew-2025`).
- Implication: "drone matches camera to satellite map" is no longer novel to judges. Novelty
  moves to robustness (night/IR, seasonal change, low altitude, featureless terrain), integrity
  (knowing when the match is wrong) and measured error on open data.

## 3. LEO PNT moved from papers to launches

- Xona Pulsar-0 launched mid-2025; FCC conditional approval for the constellation
  ([SmallSat News](https://smallsatnews.com/2025/07/01/xona-space-systems-has-launched-their-pulsar-0-pnt-satellite/)).
- TrustPoint launched its third C-band demo satellite in June 2025
  ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250625377848/en/TrustPoint-Launches-Third-Satellite-and-Successfully-Establishes-First-Contact)).
- Iridium PNT ASIC unveiled Oct 2025, commercially available Jul 2026
  ([GPS World](https://www.gpsworld.com/iridium-pnt-asic-now-commercially-available-bringing-resilient-gnss-protection-to-devices-worldwide/)).
- Implication: dedicated LEO PNT service is not available to a hackathon team. Starlink/Iridium
  signals-of-opportunity work (`repo:przemyslawpiekarski/starlink-leo-pnt`, `res:kassas-starlink-navigation`)
  is the reachable version and needs SDR hardware plus recorded data.

## 4. Quantum navigation flight tests multiplied (claims still vendor-reported)

- UK flew an optical atomic clock and cold-atom sensor on an RJ100 in May 2024
  ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/un-jammable-quantum-tech-takes-flight-to-boost-uks-resilience-against-hostile-actors)).
- Q-CTRL reported quantum MagNav flight results in April 2025 against an INS
  ([Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2025/04/14/q-ctrl-overcomes-gps-denial-with-quantum-sensing-reports-quantum-advantage/)).
- SandboxAQ reported 150+ flight hours with Acubed in 2025 and a drone test in Sep 2026 without
  error figures ([Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/09/09/sandboxaq-tests-gps-free-navigation-on-northrop-grumman-drone/)).
- DIU TQS (Mar 2025) and DARPA RoQS (Phase 1 Aug 2025) fund field-ready quantum sensors
  ([DIU](https://www.diu.mil/latest/dius-transition-of-quantum-sensing-tqs-field-testing-to-begin-across-five),
  [DARPA](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2025/roqs-launches-first-phase)).
- Implication: the sensors are out of reach, but the software problem (map matching, aircraft
  noise compensation, filter integration) is open, with public data (`res:magnav-challenge-site`).

## 5. Galileo OSNMA became an operational service

- The European Commission declared OSNMA Initial Service on 24 July 2025
  ([EUSPA](https://www.euspa.europa.eu/newsroom-events/news/testing-operations-galileo-osnma-service-now-available-users));
  EU smart tachographs in new trucks and buses must use it from December 2025
  ([GSC one-year note](https://www.gsc-europa.eu/node/7832)).
- Implication: authenticated Galileo navigation data is free and live. Open decoders exist
  (`repo:algafix/osnma`, `repo:daniestevez/galileo-osnma`). OSNMA authenticates the nav message, not
  the signal timing, so it does not stop meaconing/replay alone; pairing it with consistency checks
  is a credible small project.

## 6. Terrestrial and timing backups got national budgets

- UK announced ~GBP 155M for a timing centre, eLoran and GNSS warning in Nov 2025
  ([RNT Foundation](https://rntfnd.org/2025/11/19/uk-announces-155m-investment-in-timing-centre-eloran-gnss-warning/)).
- Implication: "detect and warn" is a funded government need in its own right, separate from
  on-vehicle alt-nav.

## 7. GNSS-denied navigation is a standing hackathon challenge

- EUDIS 5th edition (Oct 2026) made GNSS-denied navigation one of two common challenges across
  eight sites ([EC DEFIS](https://defence-industry-space.ec.europa.eu/5th-edition-eudis-defence-hackathon-here-and-we-are-looking-our-next-8-local-organisers-2026-04-28_en)).
- Implication: judges will have seen many attempts. See [README](README.md) for positioning.
