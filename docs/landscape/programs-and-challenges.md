## Patterns in the problem statements

1. **Small, cheap, many.** DASA, EDF and Brave1 statements all stress unit cost and SWaP for
   mass-produced small UAS. A solution that needs a FOG IMU or a quantum sensor misses the brief.
2. **Fusion and integrity, not one sensor.** DASA asks for AI spoofing detection and multi-source
   fusion; EDF 2024 says "multi-sensor integration". The deliverable is a trustworthy estimate.
3. **Plug-in to existing autopilots.** "Replacement for COTS GNSS" (DASA) and Theseus's
   "substitute GPS signal" mean drop-in via MAVLink / NMEA / UBX, not a new autopilot.
4. **GNSS-denied navigation is now a standing hackathon challenge** (EUDIS 2026, European Defense
   Tech Hackathon tracks). Expect it, and expect other teams to arrive with the same
   satellite-image matching demo.
