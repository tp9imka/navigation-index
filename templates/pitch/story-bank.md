# Story bank

Ready opening hooks for a PNT-resilience pitch, 10 per brief family, each tied to a measured
result in the hub. Every number was checked against [evidence-matrix.md](../../docs/evidence-matrix.md)
(snapshot 2026-11-06) and the linked sample README. Use one or two per pitch, in the "problem"
or "why it matters" beat ([pitch-structure.md](pitch-structure.md#the-five-beats)).

How to read a row:

- **Hook**: the sentence you say. Short, concrete, no adjectives.
- **Number**: what was measured, with the input label and run count. Say the label in the same
  breath: `[real]` recorded public data, `[SITL]` real autopilot firmware with simulated
  sensors, `[synthetic]` simulated vehicle and sensors (sometimes over real terrain, maps or
  traffic), `[mixed]` part real, part simulated.
- **Say also**: the caveat. If a judge would find it in the README, say it first.

These are context numbers, not your results. A hook sets up the problem; your own measured
number answers it. Never say "we showed" about a hub number.
